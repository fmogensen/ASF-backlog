§2.7. A fourth source for the existing filer, on a stable signature, with a floor so a quiet day is
not a defect. Everything else — the S2's `decided: true`, the parent Epic, the same-day no-op, the
`file_bug` approval hold — is the existing filer's, unchanged.

**Files**

- `asf/tick/file_bugs.py` — `SPEND_WINDOW_DAYS`, `SPEND_FLOOR_USD`, `SPEND_SIG`,
  `spend_signatures(root, now, conv=None)`; `cmd_file_bugs`' fourth `signatures.update` (`:237`);
  the module docstring's source list (`:4-7`) gains the line.
- `tests/test_file_bugs.py` — `SpendSignatureTests` (new).

**Steps**

1. The three constants of §2.7, with §1.4's reasoning as their comments: the window is 7 days, the
   floor is `$20.0` of labelled spend ("a quiet day is not a defect"), the signature is the
   constant `'spend: heavy share over threshold'` — stable, so the existing filer bumps rather than
   files a second Bug every day (P10 of §1.3).
2. `spend_signatures(root, now, conv=None)` reads `metrics/sessions/*.jsonl` through `_jsonl_lines`
   the way the other three sources read their streams, keeps the events whose `ts` is inside the
   window (`parse_iso`, the same cutoff shape as `ci_signatures`), and asks
   `metrics.heavy_share` and `metrics.spend_by_kind_model` — the pure functions Task 5 wrote — for
   the share and the per-cell rows. `conv or DEFAULTS` gives `heavy_share_pct`.
3. It yields nothing when the share is `None` (nothing labelled), when the labelled spend is below
   `SPEND_FLOOR_USD`, or when the share is not strictly above `conv.heavy_share_pct` (P10).
4. Otherwise one signature, shaped exactly as §2.7 fences it: the title naming the share, the
   window and the threshold; `severity: 'S2'`; `runs: []`; and evidence lines — the window line
   first, then the heaviest `kind/label` cells with their session counts and USD, which is what a
   bump re-reads each day.
5. `cmd_file_bugs` adds `signatures.update(spend_signatures(root, now, conv))` as its fourth
   source, after `rule_violation_signatures`. The `--file-bug-level` hold path already loops over
   every signature, so a product holding `file_bug` gets this one held too, with no new branch.
6. `SpendSignatureTests` per §3.4's second half, over a hand-written `metrics/sessions/<day>.jsonl`
   in a temp record root.
