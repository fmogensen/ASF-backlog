## Description
stories: S-17352, S-17353

after: Task 1, Task 2

§2.4, §2.5 and the `found_in` seam of §2.7: how often a session ends badly, and whether one item
ends badly the same way twice. Both read `metrics/sessions/*.jsonl` under the record clone and
nothing else (§1.3 — the registry and the job logs are not `file-bugs`' to read), both classify
through Task 1's `outcome_class`, both take their thresholds from Task 2's conventions.

**Files**

- `asf/tick/file_bugs.py` — `SESSION_WINDOW_H`, `EVIDENCE_CAP`, a `_session_events` window helper,
  `outcome_signatures`, `repeat_signatures`; `_file_or_bump_bug` reading `info.get('found_in', 'ci')`;
  the two `signatures.update(...)` lines in `cmd_file_bugs`; the module docstring naming the new
  sources
- `tests/test_file_bugs.py` — `metrics/sessions` in `make_repo`, a `write_session_line` helper,
  `OutcomeRateTests`, `RepeatFailureTests`

**Steps**

1. `SESSION_WINDOW_H = 24` and `EVIDENCE_CAP = 20` beside `CI_REFUSAL_WINDOW_H`, with the reason
   §1.4 gives for 24 (a rate over a week hides a bad day and files late).
2. `_session_events(root, now)`: the one pass both sources share —
   `_jsonl_lines(os.path.join(root, 'metrics', 'sessions', '*.jsonl'))`, `stale.parse_iso` on `ts`,
   drop what is outside the window or unparseable, `lifecycle.outcome_class(ev.get('result'))`,
   drop the `None`s, yield `(ts, ev, cls)` in stream order.
3. `outcome_signatures(root, now, conv)`: count by class over that pass; with
   `total >= conv.outcome_min_sessions`, every class in `lifecycle.FAILING_CLASSES` whose share is
   **strictly over** `conv.outcome_share_pct` (unrounded — PD10) yields
   `signature: f'outcome: {cls}'`, `severity: 'S2'`, `found_in: 'dev'`, `runs: []` (PD11), the
   title §2.4 shows (`round()`ed share, the threshold, `n of total in 24h`, through `truncate`),
   and the newest `EVIDENCE_CAP` evidence lines — `ts`, job, `(item)`, `on <branch>`, the verbatim
   `result`. The signature carries the class and nothing else: the rate moves every tick, the Bug
   must not.
4. `repeat_signatures(root, now, conv)`: group the same pass by `(ev.get('item') or ev.get('task'), cls)`
   over the failing classes; a group of at least `conv.repeat_failure_n` yields
   `signature: f'repeat: {key}: {cls}'`, `severity: 'S3'`, `found_in: 'dev'`, `runs: []`, no
   `links`, §2.5's title, and one evidence line per event capped at `EVIDENCE_CAP`.
5. `_file_or_bump_bug`: `'found_in': info.get('found_in', 'ci')` (`asf/tick/file_bugs.py:189`).
   That is the whole change to it — the three existing sources pass no `found_in` and stay
   byte-identical, `last_filed`, `count`, the History line and the `links.runs` merge included.
6. `cmd_file_bugs`: the two `signatures.update(...)` lines of §2.7, after the three that are
   there. The approval branch, the filed/bumped/skipped tally and the summary line are untouched.
7. The module docstring's source list gains the two new bullets, naming the stream each reads.
8. `make_repo` makes `metrics/sessions`; `write_session_line(root, day, obj)` beside
   `write_tick_line` (PD16).
9. `OutcomeRateTests` per T3: 49 events in window, 7 of them `failed: not pushed: 2 uncommitted
   file(s), 1 unpushed commit(s)` → one Bug, `signature: outcome: not pushed`, `severity: S2`,
   `decided: true` (the existing S1/S2 rule), `found_in: dev`, `14 %` and the threshold in the
   title, at most 20 body lines. Then: 4 of 49 (8 %) → nothing; 7 of 10 (70 %, under the floor) →
   nothing; an event older than 24 h in neither numerator nor denominator; `result: running` lines
   changing no percentage. Then the guard: a second run the same day → `0 filed, 0 bumped`; a run
   dated the next day → `count: 2`, one new `## History` line, the title as first filed.
10. `RepeatFailureTests` per T4: two `dead pid` events for `T-0091` → one Bug,
    `signature: repeat: T-0091: dead pid`, `severity: S3`, `decided: false`, the title naming the
    Task a
