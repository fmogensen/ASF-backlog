The spec's `## Decisions → In` bullet 2 and `## The design` §2 in full: `ruling_ready`, and the
third opener it becomes for `publish_gap` and `republish_steps`. **This is the Task the card was
filed for.** B-0123 went round six times in a day because a ruling that said "ready" opened no
publish and a bare `failed` run is held by nothing, so the branch simply stayed off origin and the
next session was launched onto it to say the same thing again. When this lands, that branch goes
out on the first tick.

Read **PD2** (the branch is the item's own fix branch — build the fixture that way or the test
proves nothing), **PD6** (nothing is held, deliberately — here is the trace), **PD7** (why the
empty-branch neighbours cannot be reached), **PD5** (`publish_refused` lands on the second tick,
and that is not a bug to fix here) and **PD11** (read the log, and put the `kind` check first).
