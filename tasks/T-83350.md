The gate never asks the classifier (P3). A lost-runner red there is sent BACK as a correct round
and a session is launched against a head nothing is wrong with (P4); a phantom there is waited out
for good, or waited out and then gated locally (P5). One helper and three one-line call sites, in
the order the spec fixes, answer both.

**Files**

- `asf/harvest/lane.py` — `judged_pending` new, module level, beside `not_required_red` (`:5620`);
  `GitHubHost.infra_gate` new, immediately after `cut_short` (which ends `:5078`); three call
  sites in `check_gate` (`:5080`). `head_red` (`:4805`), `stale_red` (`:4870`), `cut_short`
  (`:5052`), `stalled` (`:4993`), `recheck` (`:4530`), `trunk_red` (`:5229`), `send_back`
  (`:3797`), `wait` (`:3670`) and `pr_checks` (`:5331`) keep their bodies.
- `tests/test_gate_infra_red.py` — new, the whole module.
