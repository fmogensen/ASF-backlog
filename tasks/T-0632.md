The spec's `## The design` §6 in full. After this lands, every landing gate on every product —
`local` by default, which is all of them today — writes one `modules: {name: seconds}` key on its
`gates.jsonl` line, and the card's named target stops being a story: `tests.test_cutover`'s share of
the gate's wall clock is in the record from the next harvest on.

Two things are load-bearing. `product_gate` keeps its **4-tuple** and gains a **callback** (D11):
four tests replace it with a stub returning a bare 4-tuple (P4, re-read at `tests/test_harvest.py:1365`,
`:1406`, `:2590` and `tests/test_lane.py:760`), and a stub that never calls `on_modules` must simply
yield no timings — that round trip *is* the Task's acceptance. And the new line must be **invisible**
to every reader of a gate's text; PD18 measured all six of them, and names the one format change
that would break it.

Read **PD9 before writing `module_seconds`** — the only log it will ever read in `ci` mode carries
the line twice. **PD18 before choosing the line's wording or its place.** **PD16 before trusting a
number in this Gate.**

**Files**

- `tools/run_tests.py` — one helper folding `results` into `{module: seconds}` through `parse()`'s
  own reading, capped at the ten slowest, and one `out(...)` of the `slowest modules:` line between
  `out('')` and `out(summary(results, …))` (`:256-258`). `parse`, `summary`, `finish`, `plan` and
  the `--list`/`-v`/`--shards` surface are untouched; the line prints on every run, not under `-v`.
- `asf/harvest/harvest.py` — `product_gate` gains `on_modules=None`, called with the parsed dict
  after the **test command's** process finishes, green or red, and never for the two check scripts
  (`i == 0 and conv.test_command`, the same guard `MODULE_COUNT_RE` and `red_modules` already sit
  behind, `:877-895`); the 4-tuple and every existing return are unchanged. `record_gate` gains
  `modules=None` and writes it as one more key on the line (P14: `gates_from_ledger` builds its
  event from named keys, so an added key is ignored and there is no migration).
- `asf/harvest/lane.py` — `gate_groups` passes the dict it collected to the `ledger(...)` call it
  already makes (`:2585-2588`), and `gate_one_set`'s `ledger` closure (`:2766-2767`) hands it to
  `record_gate`. The closure's own clock is not touched — the ledger's `seconds` and
  `product_gate`'s log line are two independent measurements and stay that way (`docs/plans/f-0159.md`
  PD6).
- `asf/ci_gate.py` — `module_seconds(text)` and nothing else: `{module: seconds}` off the **last**
  `slowest modules:` line (PD9), `{}` when the line is absent or unparseable.
- `tests/test_run_tests.py` — one class for the line: it is printed, it is before the summary, it
  names the ten slowest of eleven modules, and `parse` reads the same `(tests, seconds, verdict,
  counts)` from the output with the line present as without it.
- `tests/test_harvest.py` — `on_modules` is called on a green gate **and** on a red one, with the
  dict the output carried; a `product_gate` stub returning a bare 4-tuple still works; `record_gate`
  writes `modules` and `gates_from_ledger` reads the line unchanged (the existing
  `gates.jsonl` test at `:2840-2890` is the fixture to extend, not to rewrite).
- `tests/test_ci_gate.py` — `Timings`: `module_seconds` round-trips the line, wins on the **second**
  of two (PD9), and returns `{}` on a text without it.
