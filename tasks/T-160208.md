**Steps**

1. `pool.Row.__init__` gains `base=''` and `base_sha=''`. `ports._spawn` gains the same two keyword
   arguments and puts them on the `Row`; `ports.launch` reads them off `meta` and passes them to
   every `_spawn` call in both lanes; `_meta` keeps them on the ledger row.
2. `spawn`: thread one `base`, defaulting to `product.main`, through the four places holding the
   trunk literal. `make_worktree(..., base='')` resolves `base = base or product.main` once and
   passes it to `_place_worktree`, `_checkout_branch(path, branch, base)` and `_rebase_onto_trunk` —
   renamed `_rebase_onto_base(..., base='')`, `base` defaulting to `main`, the
   `git rebase -q origin/<base>` arm and `trunk_rebase_needed` read `base`, and
   `lifecycle.publish(..., main=main)` keeps the trunk. In `_place_worktree` the fresh cut (`:818`)
   becomes `f'origin/{base}'`, as does the stray-commit count at `:802` (`K8`).
3. `spawn.spawn`: pass `base=row.base` into `make_worktree`, and build the `runtime.Job` with
   `base=row.base or product.main`, so both remote runtimes print "checked out on branch
   `<branch>` (base `<base>`)" unchanged (`P8`). Add `'ASF_BASE': row.base or ''` to the worker env
   dict at the same site, and `'base'`/`'base_sha'` to the session `record` dict.
4. `cli.cmd_pre_push`: both reads of the diff take the base from the environment, as
   `os.environ.get('ASF_BASE') or trunk` — the `added_paths` call and the "cannot diff against"
   line that reports an unreadable one. `added_paths`' base is already a parameter, so
   `asf/prepush.py` is untouched; an unresolvable base stays "not checked" (`D12`, `P9`).
5. `kernel.briefs`: a new `STACKED` block beside `EARLY_SPEC`, in the spec's §2.3 wording — the base
   branch, the fork sha, the predecessor id, "build on it", "do not merge or cherry-pick the trunk
   or the base into your branch", "commit nothing that belongs to the predecessor", the PR opened
   against the base, the footprint diffed against `origin/<base>`. Append it for a `build` launch
   carrying `launch.base`, where `EARLY_SPEC` is appended for a plan; the predecessor is the item's
   one open `after:` edge. Set `row.base = launch.base` on the floor `Row`.
6. `briefs.build`: the field dict gains `'base': <the row's base> or <main>`, and the
   `cut from origin/{main}` line becomes `{base}` in the three build-lane templates. Since `base`
   renders as the trunk name with no base given, every golden fixture under
   `tests/fixtures/briefs/golden/` stays byte-identical: do not regenerate them. The document
   templates are left alone (`D3`).
7. Create `tests/test_stacked_worktree.py`: one case per acceptance line on `S-135957`'s card, all
   15, against a scratch repository with a real trunk, a real base and a commit on each — the five
   worktree cases, `ASF_BASE` in the environment, the four `cmd_pre_push` cases (a stacked push
   inside its footprint **not** refused among them), the brief's three, the cloud line.
