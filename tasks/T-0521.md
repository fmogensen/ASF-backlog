§2.1 and nothing else. `health.push_gap`'s body moves down to `asf/workers/lifecycle.py` beside
`unpushed_commits` and `push_gap(ev)`, and `health.push_gap` becomes a one-line delegation that
keeps its name, its signature and every caller. This is the move f-0103 made for `outcome_class`,
for the reason §1.4 gives: the gate must refuse on exactly the condition `judge` will later fail the
run on, and two implementations of "is this pushed" drift the day one of them changes a git flag.

in — byte for byte the same answer for the same worktree, which is what the last clause of the T1
fence asserts.
