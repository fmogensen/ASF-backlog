**Steps**

1. `covers(glob, path)`: one cached regex per glob — `**` → `.*`, `*` → `[^/]*`, `?` → `[^/]`,
   `[...]` kept as a class, every other character escaped, anchored both ends; then the
   directory-prefix rule, a wildcard-free glob that is a prefix of `path` at a `/` boundary
   (`asf/harvest` and `asf/harvest/` both cover `asf/harvest/harvest.py`, `asf/x` covers nothing of
   `asf/xy.py`). `fnmatch` is not imported — P6 is why, and the docstring says so.
2. `outside(paths, grant)`: the members of `paths` no glob of `grant` covers, in `paths` order,
   deduplicated. `grant` empty or None is the caller's problem, not this function's — it returns
   every path, and `refusal` is what never asks with `None`.
3. `within(inner, outer)`: the first glob of `inner` that no glob of `outer` contains, or None. The
   conservative test is `covers(outer_glob, inner_glob)` over the inner glob's own text, so
   `asf/harvest/**` is inside `asf/**` and `asf/other.py` is not inside `asf/harvest/**`.
4. `deliverable_of` moved verbatim from harvest, and `already_on_trunk` re-pointed at it (PD13).
5. `grant_for(conv, branch, item, card)`, §2.3's four rules in order: the card's `grant:`; else, for
   a `spec` or `plan` branch, `[deliverable_of(...), conv.review_path(item.lower(), '*')]`; else the
   card's `writes:`; else **None**. The docstring states that `None` and `[]` are different answers
   and that no card can express `[]` today.
6. `refusal(conv, branch, item, card, touched)`: `None` when the grant is None or nothing is
   outside; else `Edge(BOUNDARY_KIND, text)` with `.outside` carrying the whole list. The text is
   §2.3's one line — at most five paths, then `(+N more)`, the grant named, then the sentence that
   nothing is reverted and the two ways out, with the item id in the `grant: <glob>` hint.
7. `mode`/`mode_warning` per PD7, and `MODES = ('hold', 'warn', 'off')`.
8. The convention field, its default comment, and the example yaml block.
