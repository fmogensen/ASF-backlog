The spec's `### asf/groom/delivery.py (new)` and `### asf/conventions.py and docs/products.example.yaml`.
Pure functions over `canonical` and three integers: which items are deliverable, how they group,
what fits a budget, the proposal lines that come out, and — for Task 2 — `deliveries` and
`releasable`. It imports `asf.feeder.footprint` and `asf.groom.shape`, never the other way round.

**Files**

- `asf/groom/delivery.py` — **new**. Module docstring naming F-0102 and the import direction.
  `TOKENS_PER_GLOB = 2000`, `CHARS_PER_TOKEN = 4` (PD13), and
  `Member = namedtuple('Member', 'id type title writes rank tokens')`. The seven functions of the
  spec's block: `is_small`, `deliverable_items`, `estimate_tokens`, `group`, `budget_cut`,
  `delivery_proposals`, `deliveries`, `releasable`.
- `asf/conventions.py` — three module-level defaults beside `DEFAULT_BATCH_MAX_GLOBS` (`:74`),
  each with its one-line doc comment: `DEFAULT_DELIVERY_MAX_ITEMS = 4`,
  `DEFAULT_DELIVERY_MAX_GLOBS = 6`, `DEFAULT_DELIVERY_MAX_TOKENS = 60000`; and three fields on
  `Conventions` beside `batch_max_globs` (`:150`): `delivery_max_items: int`,
  `delivery_max_globs: int`, `delivery_max_tokens: int`.
- `docs/products.example.yaml` — three commented-out lines at their defaults under
  `conventions:`, after `batch_max_globs` (`:49`), under one comment naming
  `asf.groom.delivery` and saying that `delivery_max_items: 0` turns deliveries off.
- `tests/test_delivery.py` — **new**: `SmallTest`, `EligibleTest`, `GroupTest`, `BudgetTest`,
  `ReleasableTest`, built on plain `canonical` dicts (`{id: {'meta': {...}}}`), no repo.
- `tests/test_conventions.py` — `DeliveryCeilingTests`: the three defaults, the three names in
  `Conventions.field_names()`, and a yaml `delivery_max_items: 0` reaching the field (PD14).
