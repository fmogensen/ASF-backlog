§2.6's third guard. The first two are the closing rules themselves and `sticky`, which Tasks 1 and
2 land; this Task is the cap, and the end-to-end assertion that a `Closed` item whose evidence has
gone quiet stays closed and stays unemitted.

**Files**

- `asf/feeder/rows.py` — `CAPPED_KINDS` (P10); `_capped(row, attempts, limit, product)` as §2.6
  fences it; `candidates` applying it after `hold_unlanded` (P11); the module docstring's
  STALEMATE paragraph (`:14`) widened from Bugs to every launching kind.
- `tests/test_feeder.py` — the cap's cases beside `AttemptOrderTest` (`:427`), whose B-0026
  assertions are unchanged and are the proof `bug_rows` was not touched.

**Steps**

1. `CAPPED_KINDS = frozenset({CARD_SPEC, STARVED_SPEC, STARVED_PLAN, PLAN_CODE, CONFLICT, STALE})`
   — §1.1's own enumeration, with `BUG → FIX`, `FIX → CORRECT`, `STALEMATE` and
   `GROOM → ADJUDICATE` excluded and why named in the constant's comment (P10).
2. `_capped` exactly as §2.6 fences it: `None` above the limit, the `STALEMATE → ADJUDICATE` row at
   it (`brief_kind='adjudicate'`, the row's own branch kept), the row itself below it.
3. `candidates` maps it over the rows after `hold_unlanded`, for rows whose `launches` is true and
   whose kind is in `CAPPED_KINDS`, dropping the `None`s. Ordering is untouched: `_capped` returns
   a row with the same `tier` and `feature_id`, so `key()` sorts it where a STALEMATE row sorts
   (`KIND_ORDER`, `asf/feeder/rows.py:378`).
4. The end-to-end half of S-15556: a `Closed` item is not open (`rows.is_open`, P8 of the spec), so
   it produces no row of any kind — asserted through an index whose item is `Closed` and whose
   evidence lines have gone, with `sticky` having kept it there.
