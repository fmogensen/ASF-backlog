after: Task 1

The spec's design §4, and the card's third acceptance line. A `Closed` card whose work the trunk
cannot account for keeps its open PR: the fact is written once on the lane record as `decide`,
said once in one line, re-said only when its reason changes, and dropped when the fact goes —
the card reopened, or the trunk caught up. Nothing is closed, no state moves, no correction is
written and no session is launched.

Task 1 sets `f['undecided']` and nothing reads it; this Task is the reader. After Task 1 alone a
branch in this shape behaves exactly as it does on the trunk today, which is why the split is
safe.

**Read this Task from `docs/plans/f-0314.md`, not from this card** — the 4000-character cut
(`asf/record/plan_tasks.py:36`) puts this Task's **Gate** and **Acceptance** past the card's end.
Three decisions bind it and are on no card: **`PD6`** (`advance` has a second empty
short-circuit above the transition loop, and the spec's placement leaves an undecided empty
branch unreported — call `note_undecided` in both places), **`PD17`** (how S-84507's "keeps the
lane state it had" must be read so it does not contradict P2) and **`PD18`** (a branch with no
lane record yet gets no mark, and why that is the rule).

**Files**

- `asf/harvest/lane.py` — a new method `Lane.note_undecided(self, f)` with the spec's §4
  docstring verbatim, placed beside `Lane.advance` (`:2361-2423`); two call sites in `advance`
  — the first statement inside the empty short-circuit at `:2368`, and once after the transition

- `tests/test_closed_item_prs.py` — `UndecidedReportTests`, one test per bullet of the spec's
  **S-84507** block, beside Task 1's two classes.

**Steps**

   `f"{f['item']} is {f['undecided']} in the record but no commit on {self.trunk} carries its work"`.
