**Steps**

1. `asf/kernel/resolvers.py`: `WRITE_GRANT = 'write-grant'`, §2.4's `WRITE_ASK_RE` verbatim, and
   `MAX_ASKED = 4`.
2. `write_ask(text)` → the backticked path-shaped tokens a permission question names, in order,
   or `[]`: `WRITE_ASK_RE` fires and the text carries at least one and at most `MAX_ASKED`
   backticked tokens that `PATH_TOKEN_RE` (`:371`) accepts. Text only — the card's `writes:` is
   the resolver's business, not the classifier's (**PD3**, **PD15**).
3. `write_grant_by_rule(paths, it, items, test_paths, trunk_files)` → the granted paths or `[]`,
   **its one home** (D-0007): every requested path matches a `test_paths` glob, is **not** in
   `trunk_files` (so it is a file this item adds, not one it edits), and is in no other live
   item's `writes:`. Its docstring names both callers — this class, and F-0358 §2.3's by-rule
   grant for a finding outside the footprint — and says the rule is all-or-nothing: one path the
   rule does not cover grants none of them. `BY_RULE` is §2.4's string verbatim.
4. `needs_writes` (`:404`) gains the same call in front of its three existing answers, and the
   three stay exactly as they are — one rule, two callers, no copy.
5. `asf/kernel/decide.py`: `_write_grant(it, text, facts, config, parked=())` beside
   `_needs_writes`, returning `(answer, resolvers.WRITE_GRANT, granted)` or `None`. It reads the
   asked paths with `resolvers.requested_writes(' '.join(write_ask(text)), it.writes,
   lambda p, g: _overlap([p], g))` — the one parser the REPORT field already uses (P12), so one
   reading of "a path, and whether `writes:` covers it" serves both shapes — then
   `write_grant_by_rule(paths, it, facts.items, tuple(config.test_paths),
   facts.trunk_files or ())`. `None` when the knob is off, no path is outside `writes:`, more than
   `MAX_ASKED` are asked, the rule grants none, or any asked path matches `config.risk_high`.
   Called from `_auto_answer` after `_finding_head`. When it answers, the Stuck it replaces is
   cleared and the `ApplyAnswer` carries `writes=granted` through Task 1's third tuple member
   (**PD5**) — the same path the `needs-writes` grant takes today.
6. When the rule refuses, nothing is granted and the question stays the console's: the Stuck's
   reason names the paths and **which clause refused them** — on the trunk, written by another
