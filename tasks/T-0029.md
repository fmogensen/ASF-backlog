**Steps**

1. `quota_cell` keeps its shape: the two `not_configured` guards, the account loop, the
   `', '.join(parts)`. It gains `from asf.workers import quota as quota_mod` (already imported)
   and calls `quota_mod.guards_from_config(cfg)` once, before the loop.
2. Inside the loop, stop collapsing `None` (D6): `u = source.read(a)` may be `None`, and the
   `except Exception` fallback becomes `None` too, not `{}` — an account whose source raised is
   an account that cannot be read, which the band judges `stop`. Read the two percentages from a
   separate `u or {}` so `?` still prints.
3. Append the band to the account's part when it is not `free`: `f'{name} {five}%/{seven}% {state}'`.
   The band word only — no `— why`; `asf workers quota` is the view that names the window.
   `test_quota_through_the_quota_source` (`tests/test_views.py:102`, a free account at 12/40)
   must stay green unchanged, so a free account's part keeps its exact current text.
4. Update the `Quota 5h/7d` bullet in the module docstring to say the cell names the band.
5. Add `test_quota_cell_names_the_band` to `StatusViewTests`.

# tests/test_views.py — added to class StatusViewTests
    def test_quota_cell_names_the_band(self):
        cfg = {'scheduler': {'kind': 'none'},
               'worker_pool': {'quota_command': 'echo', 'accounts': [{'name': 'w1'}]}}
        with mock.patch('asf.workers.quota.CommandQuotaSource.read',
                        lambda self, a: {'five_h_pct': 12, 'seven_d_pct': 91}):
            self.assertEqual(self.rows(cfg)['Quota 5h/7d'], 'w1 12%/91% cooldown')
        with mock.patch('asf.workers.quota.CommandQuotaSource.read',
                        lambda self, a: {'five_h_pct': 96, 'seven_d_pct': 12}):
            self.assertEqual(self.rows(cfg)['Quota 5h/7d'], 'w1 96%/12% stop')
        with mock.patch('asf.workers.quota.CommandQuotaSource.read', lambda self, a: None):
            self.assertEqual(self.rows(cfg)['Quota 5h/7d'], 'w1 ?%/?% stop')
