**Steps**

1. `asf/logs.py` gains `rotate(path, cap)` with the docstring of §2: over `cap`,
   `os.replace(path, path + '.1')`, returning the rotated path or `None`; one generation
   (`GENERATIONS`), a pre-existing `.1` replaced. A missing path, a path under its cap and a
   rename that fails are all `None` and raise nothing — a log is never a reason to fail, which is
   `harvest.keep_red_output`'s own rule today. The `carry=` parameter is Task 3's.
2. The two live copies of those four lines delegate, behaviour unchanged:
   `asf/approvals.py:1112-1118` loses its inline rotation for
   `logs.rotate(path, logs.LOG_MAX_BYTES)` before the append, and `asf/harvest/harvest.py:893-895`
   the same inside `keep_red_output`. `HOOK_ERROR_LOG_MAX_BYTES` and `GATE_RED_MAX_BYTES` stay
   exported under their own names, each equal to `logs.LOG_MAX_BYTES`:
   `tests/test_approvals.py:733-746` reads the first and is the proof that *that* call site did
   not move, so it stays green untouched.
3. The three detached-child spawns gain a rotation on the line **above** the `open` that hands the
   file over, at `logs.LOG_MAX_BYTES`: `asf/tick/step_harvest.py:136`,
   `asf/harvest/product_checks.py:126`, `asf/harvest/rebuild_check.py:133`. The parent rotates,
   never the child and never at a child's exit: before the `open` is the one instant the factory
   holds no fd on the path, so the rename moves a complete file and the child's own output begins
   in the fresh one. At exit it would be the second harvest child renaming the file the first is
   still writing (`asf/tick/step_harvest.py:88-91` lets a second spawn run), which is the
   mid-run rename the spec measured.
4. `asf/tick/step_harvest.py:42-43` takes its path from the module too (`logs.path(...)`), so that
   log's path and its rotation are minted in one place, as the other two already are after Task 1.
5. `tests/test_logs_tree.py` gains `RotateTests` — the rotation half of `S-134155`, one case per
   acceptance line: over and under cap, a replaced generation, a missing path, a path that cannot
   be written, both delegated call sites still rotating at their own cap through the module, each
   of the three spawns rotating before its `open` and leaving an under-cap log alone, the child's
   whole output in the live file, and a detached child's log capped at the asf-opened cap rather
   than the clock cap. Over a temporary `ASF_HOME`, never the operator's home.
