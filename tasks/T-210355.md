**Steps**

1. Create `asf/clock.py` — the only module under `asf/` that reads the wall clock, with a module
   docstring in the house voice saying so and naming why: a test or a product path that changes
   behaviour at midnight, at 30 days or at a year is otherwise written by accident and found by
   the trunk going red. `SHIFT_VAR = 'ASF_CLOCK_SHIFT_DAYS'`.
2. `shift()` → `datetime.timedelta`, read from `SHIFT_VAR` **once per process** (a module-level
   sentinel, not a re-read per call) and accepting whole or fractional days. Unset, empty, or
   unparsable is `timedelta(0)` and never an exception — a malformed variable must not take the
   factory down.
3. `now(now=None)` → an aware UTC `datetime`: `now` when given (returned as it is, with no clock
   read at all), else the wall clock plus `shift()`. `iso(now=None)` → `'%Y-%m-%dT%H:%M:%SZ'`.
   `today(now=None)` → `'%Y-%m-%d'`. `stamp(now=None)` → epoch seconds, the one `time.time()` in
   the product. `monotonic()` → `time.monotonic()`, **never shifted**: it measures spans, not
   dates, and a shifted monotonic makes an elapsed time negative.
4. Delegate the named doors — **eleven, not twelve: there is no `flake._now`** (K6), and
   `asf/kernel/flake.py` is not in this Task's footprint. Each becomes a one-line delegation that
   holds no raw read and gains the optional `now` its callers can thread, with no call site
