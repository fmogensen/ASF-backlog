## Description

§2.1: one top-level map in the product file, the frozen `Probe` it loads into, and the
`config_problems` hook that refuses every shape §2.1 enumerates. Unset, the whole Feature is off —
`load` returns `None` and nothing below this Task ever runs. This Task also creates the package
every later Task writes into.

**Files**

- `asf/probe/__init__.py` — the package docstring: what the probe is, and the line that ASF ships
  no journey engine (§1.2)
- `asf/probe/config.py` — `Probe` (frozen dataclass), `PROBE_FIELDS`, `load(product)`,
  `config_problems(block)`
- `asf/env.py` — `'probe'` in `PRODUCT_FIELDS` (l. 484) and in `NESTED_FIELDS` (l. 515), one
  `config_problems` call in `validate_product_text` beside `ci_queue`'s (l. 646), a
  `Product.probe` property beside `app_host` (l. 780)
- `docs/products.example.yaml` — the documented block of §2.1, beside `app_host` (l. 305)
- `tests/test_probe_config.py` — T1
- `tests/test_env.py` — the two `validate_product_text` cases of T1

**Steps**

1. `asf/probe/__init__.py`: the docstring, and no import — the package is a namespace, and every
   module under it is imported by its own name.
2. `Probe`: frozen, with `workflow`, `role`, `identity`, `secrets` (a two-key mapping),
   `mailbox` (a five-key mapping), `journeys` (a tuple), `origins` (a tuple), `artifact`,
   `timeout`. `PROBE_FIELDS` is the nested-field map `env.NESTED_FIELDS` reads, so an unknown key
   is `probe.<key> is not a field` from the validator that already says that.
3. `load(product)` returns `None` for a product with no `probe:`, else a `Probe` with §2.1's
   documented defaults and `origins` defaulting to `(product.app_host,)`.
4. `config_problems(block)` returns one `(dotted key, why)` per breach, in §2.1's order: not a
   map; no `workflow`; a `role` no runner in `ci.pool` carries (`ci_pool.load_pool`,
   `ci_pool.roles`); a `secrets` missing either key, or holding a value that is not a secret
   name; a `mailbox` missing `from`, `subject` or `code`; a `code` that will not compile or has
   no capturing group; a `max_age_s` that is not a positive integer; a `journeys` that is not a
   list of non-empty strings; an `origins` entry that is not an `https://` URL; a `timeout`
   outside `<n>s|m|h`; and a block with neither `origins` nor `app_host` — which is a problem,
   not a default, there being nothing to probe.
5. `asf/env.py`: the three lines. The `config_problems` call is `for dotted, why in
   probe_config.config_problems(data.get('probe')):`, the shape l. 638 and l. 646 already have.
6. `docs/products.example.yaml`: §2.1's block verbatim, with its comments, and the sentence that
   unset means no probe and no change to any close.
7. `tests/test_probe_config.py`: T1's table, one assertion per row — including the `\d{6}` with no
   capturing group, the `(\d{6}` that will not compile, `max_age_s` of `0`, `-1` and `"5m"`, a
   `journeys` of `"sign-up"` and of `["", " "]`, an `origins` of `http://x` and `x.example.com`,
   a `timeout` of `"soon"` — and no problem at all for §2.1's own block.
8. `tests/test_env.py`: `validate_product_text` over a product file carrying §2.1's block reports
   nothing; over one with `probe: {workflows: probe.yml}` it reports
   `probe.workflows is not a field`.
