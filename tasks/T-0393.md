## Description
stories: S-33554
writes: asf/metrics/metrics.py, tests/test_quota_pressure.py
after: Task 3

§2.4. Two constants and one function with no clock, no file and no product in it: `day` is an
argument, the events are an argument, and the answer is a 5-tuple. This is the whole of the card's
trigger, and it is the piece a reader can check by hand.

`measured` is the part that matters most and is easiest to get wrong. A day with no tick at all, and
a day whose every tick was written before this counter existed, are **not** quiet days — they are
days with no evidence, counted neither for the trigger nor against it (D5). A rule that reads them
as zeros produces `0 of 7 days` for the whole first week after this Feature lands, which is a
judgement dressed as a measurement, and the card's first acceptance is precisely that this line is
not that.

**Files**

- `asf/metrics/metrics.py` — `PRESSURE_WINDOW`, `PRESSURE_DAYS`, `quota_pressure`
- `tests/test_quota_pressure.py` — `RuleTests`

**Steps**

1. `asf/metrics/metrics.py`: `PRESSURE_WINDOW = 7` and `PRESSURE_DAYS = 3` beside the other module
   constants, under §2.4's `#:` comment copied as it stands — the trigger the card fixes, constants
   and not configuration (D6). No config key, no lookup, no override argument beyond the two
   keywords the function already takes for its own tests.
2. `quota_pressure(ticks, day, window=PRESSURE_WINDOW, need=PRESSURE_DAYS)` with §2.4's docstring
   copied as it stands, returning `(fired, days, held, seats, measured)`.
3. The window is `days_back(day, window)` (`:74`) — the module's own day arithmetic, so the rule and
   the cost table agree on what seven days means. A tick belongs to a day by `t['ts'][:10]`, the
   same slice `render_daily` and `append_event` use.
4. Every read of the two keys is `.get` (PD11): a line already on disk lacks them and must not raise
   and must not read as 0. `measured` is the sorted list of the window's days on which at least one
   tick **carries** `quota_held`; `days` is the sorted list of those on which some tick carries
   `quota_held > 0`; `held` is the sum of `quota_held` over the window's ticks; `seats` is the
   largest `quota_seats` seen in the window; `fired` is `len(days) >= need`.
5. Ticks outside the window are dropped, not merely unweighted — a caller that hands the function a
   month of ticks gets the same answer as one that hands it seven days.
6. `tests/test_quota_pressure.py`, `RuleTests`, over hand-built tick dicts and a fixed `day` string
   — no file, no clock, no product: three of the seven days carrying `quota_held > 0` → `fired`
   true, `days` those three in order; two → false. A day whose every tick carries `quota_held: 0`
   is in `measured` and not in `days`. A day with no tick at all, and a day whose ticks carry
   neither key, are in neither list. The eighth day back carries `quota_held: 9` and changes
   nothing — not `fired`, not `days`, not `held`, not `seats`. `held` is the sum over the window and
   `seats` the maximum, asserted with two different values so a sum cannot pass for a max. And
   `quota_pressure([], day)` is `(False, [], 0, 0, [])` — the shape the scorecard's `—` branch
   reads.
