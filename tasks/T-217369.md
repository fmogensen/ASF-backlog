**Steps**

1. `asf/record/stage.py`: `snapshot` skips the record's **top-level** `archive/`, matched on
   `os.path.relpath(dirpath, root)` and not on the directory name, so a folder called `archive`
   deeper in the record is still walked (D-0016). `SKIP_DIRS` keeps its meaning and its one member; the
   new skip is its own line with its own comment: the walk is a per-write cost every staged writer
   pays, and the archive is the one part of the record no writer but the archiver touches — 73 MB
   read per staged write, for nothing. This is the saving ingest, the plan minter, `file-bugs`,
   `set` and the widen pass each get.
2. `asf/record/stage.py`: `WRITERS` (`:29-31`) gains `'archive'`, in the order the tick runs it —
   after `reopen`, before nothing: it is the fifth record-tail part and runs before the final index.
3. `asf/tick/cadence.py`: `RECORD_TAIL` gains `'archive'` and `DEFAULT_EVERY_N` gains
   `'archive': 12`, so `tick.every_n.archive` is a legal key (`gateable()` reads `RECORD_TAIL`, P8)
   and `deferred_max_age_s` (3600) runs the part at least hourly whatever the number says.
4. `asf/tick/tick.py`: `run_record_tail` (`:145-189`) runs the archiver under `due('archive')`,
   inside `timed('archive')`, **before** the final `do_index`, as
   `stage.guarded(root, 'archive', fn, only=paths, product=product)` where `paths` is
   `archive.plan(root, product)`'s own relpath list (**PD6**, D-0015) — the plan first, pure, then the
   writer over exactly what it named. The tick line carries `archive moved=<n> held=<m>` from the
   same place the other record parts report, and the commit subject is
   `archive: <n> cards, <m> notes`. A pass that moves nothing costs one closure over cards the tick
   has already loaded.
5. `tests/test_record_archive_readers.py`: S-211572's twelve lines. `load_items` is unchanged —
   assert the function's source is byte-identical to this base's, so a later counter or `scope=`
   argument fails here (D-0002, **PD9**) — and returns the working-set cards only; it opens no file
   under the archive (patched `builtins.open`); the kernel's record port returns the working set
   only and reports no archived card unreadable; `asf index` writes an index with no archived entry,
   and an archived card whose entry a stale `index.json` still held is dropped by the next run;
   `asf check` reports no finding for any archived card, opens no archived card file, and reports
   **no missing folder** on a record that has archived nothing (**PD3**); `asf check --staged` lays
   out the item folders only and does not refuse a commit that moves cards into the archive, and
   still refuses one that deletes a card another card references; the measured invariant fact of
   block M1 — one move's staged change gives `_card_paths` the removal side and all six record
   invariants no finding (**PD7**); `snapshot` skips the record's top-level `archive/` and still
   walks a `tasks/archive/` one; and `guarded` over a deliberately bad move puts both sides back and
   leaves another writer's output untouched.
