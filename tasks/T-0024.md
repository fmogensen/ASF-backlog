## Description
stories: S-7052
writes: asf/harvest/harvest.py, tests/test_harvest.py

**Files**

- `asf/harvest/harvest.py` — `harvest_branch`, `land_ff`
- `tests/test_harvest.py`

**Steps**

1. Both paths scan **every unpublished commit** — after their own gate, and before their push and
   before their dry-run line — with the repo of the branch (the product repo or the record) as
   `repo`. The reason text is `redaction: <n> finding(s) — <first finding>`, the finding formatted
   per D8, and the gate writes its §4 ledger lines with `where='harvest'`.
2. `harvest_branch`: after the rebase, the `harvest: regenerate index.json` commit and `run_gate`,
   and before `push_ff` or `push_branch`, `redact.scan_unpublished(tmp, sha, pats)`. Findings →
   `hold(job, 'redaction: …')`, exactly as its other gate failures are held, and **no push**.
3. `land_ff`: after `product_gate`, and before `push_ff`, the same scan. Findings →
   `hold_with_correction(state_dir, branch, record, 'redaction', <every formatted finding, one per
   line>, out)`, and **no push**. `lifecycle.hold` hands the branch back to its session with the
   findings as the correction, so the session that wrote the line is the one that removes it.
4. No special case at the cap: at `ROUND_CAP` `lifecycle.hold` already stops climbing, marks the
   correction `at_cap` for the ADJUDICATE row and flags the operator on a second hold at the cap
   (B-0048). A redaction hold goes through that rule like any other hold — do not add a branch for
   it.
5. `tests/test_harvest.py` gains `RedactionTests`, per §3.3.
   `test_the_hold_hands_the_findings_back_as_the_correction` asserts the run's `correction` has
   `kind: redaction` and the formatted findings, and that neither contains the matched text.
