§2.1's first two rows, and D4. The quickstart is rewritten around `tools/quickstart.sh` and
promises only what a test proves; concepts is written from nothing. Neither page carries config —
that is `docs/operator.md`'s, and `docs/README.md`'s one-place rule is why.

**Files**

- `docs/quickstart.md` — rewritten over the moved `getting-started.md`
- `docs/concepts.md` — the prose behind Task 3's headings
- `tests/test_quickstart.py` — `BOUND_S = 1200`, replacing three literals (PD8)
- `tests/test_docs.py` — `QuickstartBoundTests`

**Steps**

1. `tests/test_quickstart.py`: `BOUND_S = 1200` at module level with a one-line comment saying
   what it bounds, and the three existing `1200`s (`:67`'s subprocess timeout, `:98`'s and `:99`'s
   assertions) read it. Nothing else in the file changes — §1.2 keeps the script and its run out of
   scope (PD8).
2. `docs/quickstart.md`: zero to the sample product ticking and one item landed. Prerequisites;
   `bash tools/quickstart.sh`, what each of its nine steps prints and which artefact to open after
   it; then the same walk against a real product, pointing at F-0107's `asf install`. It names
   `tools/quickstart.sh` **once** and never restates its steps — a second copy of the script's
   steps is the drift this card exists to remove (§1.2).
3. The page states the bound as minutes derived from `BOUND_S`, and says what proves it
   (`tests/test_quickstart.py`, on CI hardware). The card's "15 minutes" is the intent and the page
   does not print it as a promise; it prints the number a test asserts (D4).
4. `docs/concepts.md`: the record and its two copies; Epic / Feature / Story / Task / Bug and what
   each is for; typed intent versus derived state; the tick and its eight steps — read off
   `asf.tick.steps.STEPS`, which is `record, health, groom, wave, prs, harvest, batch, daily` in
   this worktree; the wave, the groom, the harvest and the gate; approvals. One diagram in prose,
   and **no config** — a key named here would be a second home for it.
5. `QuickstartBoundTests` in `tests/test_docs.py`: the minutes `docs/quickstart.md` states equal
   `tests.test_quickstart.BOUND_S`, read from the module and not from a copy of the number.
6. `asf docs check` stays clean: both pages' links resolve under check 2, and neither introduces a
   heading that another page's fragment misses.
