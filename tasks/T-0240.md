§2.2: the module that documents itself as the single owner of every environment ASF hands a child
strips three variables where the private copy beside it strips seven (D4). `hermetic.GIT_HOOK`
grows to the seven, `hermetic.git_env` becomes the one function that removes them, and the two
copies plus the detached harvest's hand-built environment go. No behaviour change on the push
path — the list it strips is the same list — and a strict widening everywhere else.

**Files**

- `asf/hermetic.py` — `GIT_HOOK` at seven names; `git_env(base=None)`
- `asf/gitpush.py` — `_HOOK_VARS` and its loop deleted; `push` builds through `hermetic.git_env`
- `asf/harvest/harvest.py` — `clean_env` a one-line delegation; `GIT_HOOK_VARS` kept as the alias
- `asf/tick/step_harvest.py` — `spawn_background`'s environment from `hermetic.build`, its
  `pkg_parent` from `hermetic.package_parent()`
- `tests/test_hermetic.py` — `BuildInvariants` and `OneBuilderTests` over the widened list; a
  `GitEnvTests`
- `tests/test_gitpush.py` — the child environment asserted equal to `hermetic.git_env(base)`

**Steps**

1. `asf/hermetic.py`: `GIT_HOOK` becomes the seven names §2.2 prints, with §2.2's `#:` comment —
   naming the `pre-push` quarantine as the reason, and `asf.gitpush` as the list that has been the
   complete one in production. `build()` already strips `GIT_HOOK + CALLER_IDENTITY` in one loop
   (`asf/hermetic.py:151`), so it gains the four new names with no edit.
2. `git_env(base=None)` beside `package_parent()`: `dict(os.environ if base is None else base)`
   minus every member of `GIT_HOOK`, returned. Its docstring is §2.2's — the whole environment for
   a `git` child, where `build` is for a child that runs *code*. It strips no caller identity and
   no config: a `git` process reads neither, and stripping `PATH` would break the child.
3. `asf/gitpush.py`: `_HOOK_VARS` (`:30`) and the `for var in _HOOK_VARS` loop (`:57`–`:59`)
   deleted; `run_env = hermetic.git_env(env)` in their place. `gitpush` gains one import of
   `asf.hermetic`, which imports nothing of `asf` — no cycle. `push_args`, the timeout, the
   process group and `TIMED_OUT` are untouched.
4. `asf/harvest/harvest.py`: `clean_env` becomes `return hermetic.git_env(env)`, its docstring
   kept word for word — it is the reason the rule exists and it stays written where harvest can
   read it. `GIT_HOOK_VARS = hermetic.GIT_HOOK` (`:68`) stays as the alias its readers use.
   `sh` and `sh_timed` are untouched and gain the four variables through `clean_env` (§1.3).

   `hermetic.build(worktree=hermetic.package_parent())` plus the two values it genuinely sets —
   `ASF_HOME` and nothing else; `--product` is already on the argv. The `import asf` and the
   `os.path.dirname(os.path.dirname(...))` go: `package_parent()` is that value and gains its
   second caller. The detached harvest stops inheriting the tick's `ASF_PRODUCT` and every hook
   variable, which is `CALLER_IDENTITY`'s whole purpose.
6. `GitEnvTests`: with all seven set in the base, `git_env(base)` has none of them and keeps
   everything else; `git_env()` reads `os.environ`; the return is a new dict, not the base mutated.
