## Description
stories: S-18109

after: Task 1, Task 2

The spec's §2.10, and the ledger prune of §4. The one Story that needs both halves of the Feature:
the fold is in `asf/chores.py`, the table and the event are the `watch` step's probe part. Its
`writes:` intersect Tasks 1, 2 and 3, so the feeder runs it after them (PD14) — which is what
`after:` says. Nothing goes near `asf/metrics/metrics.py`: F-0099 is changing `scorecard_rows` and
`render_daily` this week, and the controller's two numbers are read by one person once a week
(§1.4).

**Files**

- `asf/chores.py`
  - `measure(root, cfg, days=7, now=None)` → `{'peak', 'peak_last_week', 'now', 'per_day': {...},
    'reads'}`: the controlling account's window folded over `metrics/events/*.jsonl`'s
    `controller_usage` lines through `metrics.read_stream(root, 'events')`
    (`asf/metrics/metrics.py:280-289`), and the chore counts over the ledger's day files. Divides
    by nothing when either source is empty.
  - `render_controller(m, account)` → the five lines of §2.10, and `None` when
    `controller.account` is unset — there is no account to measure and the table is not written.
  - `prune(now=None)` → day files older than `LEDGER_DAYS_KEPT` deleted, the count returned; an
    unreadable directory is 0, not an error.
- `asf/tick/step_watch.py` — `part_probe` gains three things, all inside the hourly throttle it
  already holds: one `controller_usage` event through `ctx.event` carrying `account`,
  `seven_d_pct` and `five_h_pct` from the same quota read the table uses; `tables/controller.md`
  through `write_changed`; and `chores.prune()`, the one part that already runs on a throttle and
  already writes there (§4). A `controller.account` that is unset skips all three with one line —
  no event, no table, and the doctor's own rows are unaffected.
- `tests/test_chores.py` — `MeasureTests`.

**Steps**

1. `measure`: the peak is a fold over the stream, so a missing day is a gap and not a zero, and
   `reads` is how many lines it folded — the number §2.10's table prints as
   `highest of 38 reads`. `peak_last_week` is the 7 days before the window, read the same way.
2. `per_day` over the ledger: `{'commands', 'covered', 'repeated'}` as a 7-day mean, from
   `read_days(days)` and `classify`, and `scriptable` as the top rows of `covered` by count.
3. `render_controller`, matching §2.10's five lines exactly, and returning `None` with no account.
4. `prune`.
5. `part_probe`: the quota read happens once and feeds the event, the table and nothing else. The
   event is appended before the stamp is written, so a probe that dies half way is due again rather
   than silently skipping a week of reads.
6. `MeasureTests`, including the pair PD19 names: a tick whose probe was due appends exactly one
   `controller_usage` line, a tick whose probe was not due appends none.
7. Re-run Task 1's fence (`tests.test_tick_steps`, `tests.test_tick`): the probe part grew, and the
   tests that stub `doctor.run` now need the quota read stubbed too.

**Acceptance**

returns this week's peak, last week's peak, the current read and the per-day chore counts, counts
how many reads it folded, and divides by nothing when either source is empty;
`tables/controller.md` carries the five lines of §2.10 after a tick whose probe was due, and the
`controller_usage` event is in the day's event stream once per tick.
