The spec's `### asf/feeder/rows.py`. Two kinds, one row function called before `feature_rows`, and
three existing functions taught that a delivery speaks for its members.

**Files**

- `asf/feeder/rows.py`
  - `DELIVERY_PLAN = 'DELIVERY → PLAN'` and `DELIVERY_CODE = 'DELIVERY → CODE'` beside the other
    kind constants (`:41-54`), both added to the module docstring's kind table (`:10-33`) and to
    `KIND_ORDER` (`:411`) after `RESHAPE` — i.e. at 4, with the fallback becoming 5.
  - `delivery_rows(items, product, busy, running, landed_shas=None)` — the spec's pseudocode,
    exactly. Tier 2 and `feature_id = lead` on every row it emits (PD10). Branches through
    `branch_for(product, 'plan', lead)` and `branch_for(product, 'code', lead)`, never a literal
    prefix. The `WAITS ON delivery <lead>` row per non-lead open member carries
    `waits_on='delivery'` and `brief_kind='task'`, as the spec writes it — it never launches, so
    its brief kind is never rendered.
  - `feature_rows` (`:312`): skip a Feature carrying `delivers:` or `delivered_by:`.
    `bug_rows` (`:209`): the same skip in its filter chain. `task_rows` untouched.
  - `running_footprints` (`:296`): after the Task loop, also yield `(lead, union)` for every open,
    busy card carrying `delivers:`, the union being the ordered union of its open members'
    `writes:` — so an unrelated Task waits on a running delivery exactly as on a running Task.
  - `hold_unlanded` (`:454`): for a `DELIVERY_PLAN`/`DELIVERY_CODE` row, the `after:` scan reads
    the lead **and** every member of `delivers:`, holding the row on the first unlanded
    predecessor any of them names.
  - `candidates` (`:476`): `rows += delivery_rows(items, product, busy, running, landed_shas)`
    immediately before the `feature_rows` line (`:497`), so the union is in `running` when Tasks
    are placed.
- `tests/test_feeder.py` — `DeliveryRowsTest`, on the in-memory index dicts the module already
  builds.

**Steps**

1. The two constants, the docstring table, `KIND_ORDER`.
2. `delivery_rows`, in the spec's branch order: no open members → nothing; a blocked open member →
   one `WAITS ON <blocker>`; lead in `busy` → no launching row; stage `card`/`plan-draft`/
   `plan-review rN` → `DELIVERY → PLAN`; stage `plan-approved`/`building …` → the union, then
   `footprint.first_conflict(union, running)` → `WAITS ON <other>`, else `DELIVERY → CODE` and
   `running.append((lead, union))`.
3. The `WAITS ON delivery <lead>` rows for the non-lead open members, emitted in every branch —
   the spec's `plus,` clause is unconditional.
4. The three skips and the `running_footprints` addition.
5. `hold_unlanded`'s member scan.
6. The call in `candidates`.
7. `DeliveryRowsTest`.
