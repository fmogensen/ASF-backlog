**Steps**
1. `write_new_item(..., acceptance=(), sections=None, shape=None)`:
   - The body is `## Description`, then each `sections` entry as `## <heading>\n- <item>…\n\n`,
     in the map's order, then `## Acceptance` with one `- [ ] <item>` per item (or today's single
     `- [ ] ` when there are none), then `## Non-goals`, `## History`, `## Children` and
     `## Backlinks` as today.
   - An empty `sections` value (for example `{'Features': []}`) writes no heading.
   - The History line gets `f" — shape: {rule} → {type}"` appended when `shape` is given (PD1).
   - With the defaults, the output is byte-identical to today's.
2. `file_bugs.py:199` passes `shape=('signature', 'bug')`, and `why` stays `'file-bugs'`.
3. `check.py`: after the per-item checks, add a size pass. It covers every open item (`is_open`)
   whose `## History` section (`parse_sections`) has a line matching `shape.SHAPE_LINE_RE`, and
   checks the item against the spec's table:
   - bug: `signature` is non-empty.
   - task: `writes` is a non-empty list.
   - story: `## Acceptance` has at least one `- [ ]`/`- [x]` line with text after the box.
   - epic: `## Features` has at least 2 `- ` bullets, or `derived[iid]['children']` holds at least 2 items of type `feature`.
   - feature: neither `writes` nor `signature`.

   Each finding is `add(rec, find_line(rec, 'id'), …)`, with the spec's text and the parenthesis
   taken from `shape.SIZE`. Items with no shape line are skipped (D6), and so are items that are
   not open.
4. `CheckShapeTest`: every case the spec lists. The items are written with `write_item` and a
   body whose History carries the shape line. `test_file_bugs_writes_the_shape_line` reuses
   `tests/test_file_bugs.py`'s CI-line fixture (`write_ci_line`, two failures on `main` within
   24h), runs `asf file-bugs`, and checks the History line and that `asf check` reports no
   shape finding.
