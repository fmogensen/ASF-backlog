The spec's **In** 2 and its design §2 and §3, less the `record`, `tags` and `hooks` acts: the
lay-down, the act loop, the command's remaining flags, and the first two acts. `lay` is the whole
reason the rehearsal is not another tidy fixture — a real pinned venv and the real dispatcher in
front of it, with **no stub `asf` anywhere on the laid-down `PATH`** (P16 names the e2e harness's
instant `asf` stub as exactly what a rehearsal must not use), and S-79605's third acceptance line
is what proves it.

**Read this Task from `docs/plans/f-0306.md`, not from this card.** Three decisions bind it and
reach no card: **PD1** (the ratchet is already landed in `tests/test_id_pattern.py`, so the act
must be stricter than that file's `ALLOWED` table and the module carries its own scanner),
**PD12** (`manifest` is not selectable and always runs first) and **PD6** (`asf plugin check` is in
the Gate as a proof, not because anything regenerates).

**Files**

- `asf/rehearsal.py` — `lay(snapshot, root, product='rehearsal')`: two bare origins seeded from the
  snapshot, the two checkouts, `home/` with `products/<p>.yaml` and `config.yaml`, the refs of
  `manifest['refs']` created as the manifest says (an annotated entry with `git tag -a -m <its
  annotation>`), a real venv pinned in `state/<p>/install.json` and the real dispatcher written at
  `home/.local/bin/asf` through `asf.dispatch.install` (`asf/dispatch.py:197`). `Act(name, what,
  run)` and the ordered `ACTS` tuple; `rehearse(snapshot, root=None, acts=None, out=print)` laying
  the snapshot down **once per act** from a template copied per act (the `tests/gitfixture.py`
  `Template` shape of P16, `:22`), removing each root on the way out unless `--keep`, returning
  `[(name, ok, detail)]`. The `manifest` act (`manifest_holds`) and the `ids` act. A lane is
  `lane-N` wherever one is needed, and the module still spells no branch prefix: the `worker/<id>`
  branch name the `ids` act feeds `lane.item_of` comes from `conventions.branch_prefixes`.
- `asf/cli.py` — `--act NAME` (repeatable), `--keep`, `--json` on the existing parser; the exit
  codes of §3 (0 every act passed, 1 a red act, 2 a usage or lay-down error); `--json` printing
  `{"snapshot": …, "acts": [{"name", "ok", "detail"}], "ok": bool}`.
- `tests/test_rehearsal_ids.py` — **new**. The act's scanner pinned equal to
  `tests.test_id_pattern.FOUR_ONLY` by comparing `.pattern` (PD1); the act's detail naming the four
  sites; the truncation case reported **as a truncation with the answer it got** (`score._JOB_RE`
  on `coder-t-78306` → `'coder-t-7830'`), not as a miss; `--act` selection and the three exit
  codes; `lay` leaving no stub `asf` on the laid-down `PATH`.

**Steps**

   laid-down `PATH` is a stub.

   session row, `ingest._MERGED_INTO`, `runtime._ITEM_RE` and `score._JOB_RE`, then the P1 ratchet.
   It is **red** when it lands, and its detail names the four sites and the truncated answer.
5. Do **not** touch the four sites, `tests/test_id_pattern.py` or its `ALLOWED` table. Out, by C1:
   each is its own Bug card filed from the first red run.
