The spec's §4's two surfaces. When this lands, a held Story or Feature carries
`waiting on your check: #869, #871 — asf checked --item F-0119` in its `evidence:` block, and every
tick prints one `NEEDS OPERATOR` line per held item, immediately after the `deploy:` lines (D9). An
item whose landings are not yet inside `prod_sha` is named by neither: it is waiting on the deploy,
not on a person, and saying otherwise would send an operator to look at something that is not there.

Read **PD1** (`deploy_pass` has no root — where it gets one and why the argument is the answer),
**PD2** (the tick reads the record this same tick's `record` step wrote; it does not derive again)
and **PD15** (one constant, three readers) before starting.

**Files**

- `asf/record/ingest.py` — one line appended to a held Story's or Feature's `lines`, at the three
  sites Task 2 already computes `awaiting` at.
- `asf/tick/step_harvest.py` — `deploy_pass(product, out=print, root=None)` and its one caller.
- `tests/test_ingest.py` — **new class** `TheWaitIsOnTheCard`.
- `tests/test_checked.py` — **new class** `TheTickLine`.

`asf/evidence/checked.py` is **not** on this list: `WAITING_PREFIX`, `waiting_line` and `held`
landed in Task 1 for exactly this reason.

**Steps**

1. `asf/record/ingest.py`: at each of the three sites, when the computed `awaiting` is non-empty,
   append `checked.waiting_line(iid, awaiting)` to that item's `lines` before `settle` is called —
   so the line sits with the other evidence lines and `_ingest_fields` writes it into the machine
   block exactly as it writes the rest. It goes **above** the `rule:` line, which `ingest_into`
   appends last (`:1101`). No new History line: the existing
   `state Resolved → Closed (rule: children-closed)` already records the transition the tick
   unblocks, and the spec's `## Records` says so.
2. `asf/tick/step_harvest.py`: `deploy_pass(product, out=print, root=None)`. After
   `deploy.tick(product, out=out)` returns — inside the `try`, so a deploy fault still short-circuits
   to its one loud line and the held items are not named against a deploy that did not read — and
   only when `root` is not None, call `checked.held(root)` and emit, per item id in sorted order,
   `f'NEEDS OPERATOR: {iid} is on prod and waiting on your check — asf checked --item {iid}'`. With
   `root` None the function's output is byte-for-byte today's. Extend the docstring by one clause.
3. `asf/tick/step_harvest.py`, in `run`: `deploy_pass(product, out, ctx.record_root() if
   ctx.has_record else None)` — the same guard `:178` already uses for `record_items` (PD1).
4. A product on `auto`, one that deploys nothing, and one whose landings are not yet deployed all
   get no lines, and none of that is decided here: `held` finds nothing because ingest wrote nothing,
   because `awaiting_tick` returned `[]`. One decision, in one function, read by both surfaces
   (PD15) — do not add a second `prod_mode` test in `deploy_pass`.
5. `tests/test_ingest.py` `TheWaitIsOnTheCard`: a held Story's card gains

   after the tick — run the ingest twice, ticking between, and assert the line is gone from the

   an item on `auto` gets none; a PR-less landing renders as a 9-character sha; and the `rule:` line
   is still last.
