**Files**

- `asf/channels.py` — `advance(product, root, event=None, out=print, now=None,
  run=subprocess.run)`: publish edge at the newest green tag, then promote stable when all four
  rows are met. It returns **0** always — a remote, record or log it cannot read is one line and a
  zero exit, because a channel that does not move today is not a failed tick (D-0010, P13) — and it
  publishes nothing at all for a product whose repo is not the factory's own source. Its two lines
  are the ones §1 prints, naming the version and what moved or held, and each publication is one
  `ctx.event` line: `kind` `channel`, with `channel`, `tag`, `commit`, `previous`, and `rows` for

  channels, the candidate, and the four rows with their evidence — shared by the table and
  `--json` so the two cannot drift.
