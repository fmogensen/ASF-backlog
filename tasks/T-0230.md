## Description
stories: S-29055
writes: asf/groom/groom.py, tests/test_groom.py
after: Task 1

§2.7. An item stopped by its budget waits for a ruling, and the groom file is where a ruling is
already recorded, with its attribution and its History line. One question per over-budget open
card, three answers — raise it, close it, reshape it — and `approvals.groom` is the autonomy
level, unchanged: unset, the line waits for the operator; `auto`, the `GROOM → ADJUDICATE` session
answers it like any other. No new approval class (§1.3): a product that lets the factory rule on a
budget must not thereby let a session buy a subscription.

**Files**

- `asf/groom/groom.py` — `OVER_BUDGET_QUESTIONS`, `groom_over_budget_section`, its `EXTRA_SECTIONS`
  entry (`:679`) and its line in the sections builder (`:821`, `:854`); `ANSWER_BUDGET`, its
  `_parse_answer` branch (`:86-118`), its applier branch beside `unblock`'s (`:355-377`), and the
  unrecognised-answer text (`:330`).
- `tests/test_groom.py` — `OverBudgetQuestionTests` (T6).

**Steps**

1. `OVER_BUDGET_QUESTIONS = ('Over budget', 'over_budget')` appended to `EXTRA_SECTIONS`, so
   `_SECTION_BY_TITLE` and `_line_sections` attribute the lines to `over_budget` with no further
   change.
2. `groom_over_budget_section(canonical, product)` built like `groom_refused_section` (`:580`):
   `product is None` → no lines; one `_card_line` per open card whose `budget.spent` over its
   `cost:` block is over; a card that is Closed, removed or marked `reshape` is skipped, as the
   refused section skips them. Rendered only when it has lines.
3. The line's `why` is §2.7's: both measures, whose budget it is (`product default` / `item`, from
   `Spent.budget.source`), and the three answers — ``raise it (`budget 12` or `budget 12 $25`),
   close it (`no: <why>`) or reshape it (`reshape: <how>`)``.
4. Asked once in the sense the spec means: an answer changes the card, and a card no longer over
   budget is not asked again. No state file, no marker.
5. `ANSWER_BUDGET = re.compile(r'^budget\s+(?P<sessions>\d+)(?:\s+\$?(?P<usd>\d+(?:\.\d+)?))?$',
   re.I)` → `_parse_answer` returns `('budget', {'budget_sessions': n, 'budget_usd': x})`, the
   `usd` key present only when the answer named it.
6. The applier's own branch, beside `unblock`'s: one `frontmatter.write_typed` of the one or two
   fields, one History line `- <date> groom: budget → 12 sessions / $25 <who>`, one `groom_answer`
   event (`field: budget`, `section: over_budget`, `by: operator | adjudicator | rule:<name>`),
   and a no-op when the card already carries those values — `--apply` stays idempotent.
7. The unrecognised-answer text (`:330`) gains `budget <n> [$<usd>]` to its list of words.
8. `tests/test_groom.OverBudgetQuestionTests`: T6's sentences — one line per open over-budget card
   naming both measures and the three answers, and none for a card under its budget, closed or
   already carrying the raise; `budget 12` writing `budget_sessions: 12`, one History line and one
   `groom_answer` event; `budget 12 $25` writing both; a second `--apply` changing nothing;
   `no: not worth more` closing the card as any `no` does; `budget soon` skipped with the "not an
   answer the grammar knows" line naming `budget <n> [$<usd>]`; under `approvals.groom: auto` the
   adjudicate session's `adjudicator: budget 12` applied and attributed; the section rendered only
   when it has lines, and `_line_sections` attributing its lines to `over_budget`.
