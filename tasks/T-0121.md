## Description
stories: S-15356
writes: asf/metrics/metrics.py, tests/test_metrics.py, tests/test_continuation.py
after: Task 4

§2.7 and §4's stream. The card's third acceptance is a seven-day reading, and today it cannot be
taken at all: a `correct` session derives to `code` (PD4), so the fixers are counted as coders.
This Task makes the stream carry the two facts the reading needs — the kind and the `resumed` —
and prints the row.

**Files**

- `asf/metrics/metrics.py` — `RECORD_KINDS['correct'] = 'fix'` (`:49`);
  `SCHEMAS['sessions']['resumed'] = (('str', 'null'), None)` (`:120`, PD5); `session_event`'s
  `ev` gains `'resumed': record.get('resumed')` (`:931`); `scorecard_rows` gains the `fixers`
  row beside `agents` (`:446`).
- `tests/test_metrics.py` — the new mapping and the new row.
- `tests/test_continuation.py` — `MetricTests` (§3.6).

**Steps**

1. One line in `RECORD_KINDS`. `session_kind` reads a record's own row kind before it derives
   from the job name (`:889`), so this alone re-types every `correct` session, cold or
   continued, and nothing else moves: `derive_kind` is untouched and `KIND_PREFIXES` gains no
   pattern.
2. The schema key, in the same diff as the field (PD5) — without it `_apply` raises
   `SchemaError` on every session event, continued or not.
3. `session_event` carries `resumed` straight off the record's `resumed` field, as it already
   carries `session` off `session` (P10). A cold fixer's record has none, so the event's is
   `None`.
4. The `fixers` row per §2.7, over the `sessions` stream: sessions of `kind: fix`, split by
   whether `resumed` is set, against the `merges` the `ticks` stream already sums —
   `| fixers | 11 fixer sessions, 6 continued | 5 cold → 0.8 cold fixers per merged PR |`. With
   no fixer sessions at all the row is **absent**, not a zero: a factory that fixed nothing has
   nothing to report, and a printed zero would read as an achievement.
5. `scorecard_rows` keeps its positional signature `(ci, sessions, ticks, conv=None)` — it is
   already handed `sessions` — so every existing caller and test is unaffected.
6. `MetricTests` per §3.6: `metrics.session_kind({'kind': 'correct', 'job': 'correct-f-0039'})`
   → `fix`; a continued run's `sessions` event carrying the writer's `session` and its
   `resumed`, and a cold fixer's carrying neither; `scorecard_rows` emitting the `fixers` row
   over a seven-day stream with 11 fixer sessions, 6 of them continued, against 6 merges →
   `0.8 cold fixers per merged PR`; the row absent with no fixer sessions at all; and
   `tests/test_metrics` green with the new mapping.
