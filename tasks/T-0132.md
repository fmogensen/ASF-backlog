## Description
stories: S-18606
writes: asf/workers/spawn.py, asf/workers/pool.py, asf/tick/step_wave.py, tests/test_session_identity.py, tests/test_workers.py
after: Task 3

§2.6's first half and D11. The brief's `role`, `role_sha` and `effort` reach the workers' `Row`,
and `spawn` writes `role`, `role_sha` and a `rules` object on the launch line. `lifecycle.fold`
needs no change: a launch line opens a run, so a field written once at launch is on the same
folded run as the result (B-0041) — "recorded with its result" needs no second write.

**Files**

- `asf/workers/pool.py` — `Row.__init__` gains `role=''`, `role_sha=''`, `effort=''` (`:103-119`);
  `Row.from_dict` reads all three (`:129-134`), so a JSON row round-trips.
- `asf/tick/step_wave.py` — `worker_row` copies `brief.role`, `brief.role_sha` and `brief.effort`
  onto the `Row` beside `model` (`:158-168`).
- `asf/workers/spawn.py` — `standing_sha(product)` = the first 12 hex of sha256 over
  `preamble.rules_block(product, getattr(product, 'main', 'main'))` (PD9); `rules_digest(product)`
  → `{'standing': …, 'cards': …, 'n': …, 'sha': …}` per D11, where `cards` is the sha over the
  sorted `<rule-id> <card-sha>` lines of the enforced rule cards
  (`asf.rules.rules.load_rules(product.backlog_dir)` + `partition`, `asf/rules/rules.py:65-99`),
  `n` their count, and `sha` the digest over both parts; a record with no `index.json` and a
  record with no rule cards both give `cards: None, n: 0` and a `sha` over the standing part
  alone — `RulesError` is caught, never raised at a launch. `spawn.spawn` adds
  `'role': row.role, 'role_sha': row.role_sha, 'rules': rules_digest(product)` to the record
  (`:305-311`) and passes `effort_args(row.effort, cfg)` into the `Job`'s `extra_args`.
  `asf.rules.rules` and `asf.briefs.preamble` are imported inside the two functions.
- `tests/test_session_identity.py` — `RoleRecordedTests` (new, §3/T7).
- `tests/test_workers.py` — the fake-runtime launch fixtures extended where they assert the
  registry record's keys.

**Steps**

1. `pool.Row` and `Row.from_dict`: the three fields, defaulting to `''`, so every hand-built row
   and every parsed text row in the suite still constructs.
2. `step_wave.worker_row`: three more keyword arguments from the `Brief`, read with `getattr(...,
   default='')` in the shape the existing `add_dirs` line uses (`:168`).
3. `spawn.standing_sha` and `spawn.rules_digest` per PD9 and D11. The standing text is
   recomputed, never parsed back out of the brief; the two are equal by construction because both
   call `preamble.rules_block` with the same product and the same `main`.
4. `spawn.spawn`: the three keys on the record, written beside `model` and `brief`, and
   `extra_args=effort_args(row.effort, cfg)` on the `Job`. Order of operations is unchanged — the
   digest is computed before `runtime.run`, so a refused effort label fails the launch at the same
   point a refused model label does.
5. `RoleRecordedTests` per §3/T7, over the suite's fake runtime: a spawned session's registry line
   carries `role`; `role_sha == roles.load(<role>).sha`; `rules.standing` equals the sha of the
   standing-rules block found in the *written brief file* (the proof of PD9); `rules.cards` and
   `rules.n` match the enforced cards of the fixture record; `rules.sha` is the digest over both.
   A record with no rule cards gives `cards: None, n: 0` and still a `sha`. Editing the role file
   between two launches (a temporary `$ASF_ROLES_DIR`) changes `role_sha` and no other key.
