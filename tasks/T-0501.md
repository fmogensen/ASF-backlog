  (`:196-199`)

**Steps**

1. `asf/cli.py:196` — the `set` subparser's `help=` becomes `## The design`'s two-line string, byte
   for byte: `"write typed fields of a card through the parser (a Task's writes:/after: with =, += and -=)"`.
2. `asf/cli.py:198` — the `assignments` positional gains `## The design`'s `help=`, byte for byte:
   repeatable; a Task's list fields `writes:` and `after:` also take `FIELD+=VALUE` to add and
   `FIELD-=VALUE` to remove, and `VALUE` may be `[a, b]`. No `epilog` is added — no subparser in the
   module uses one (P9, D10). Nothing else in the file is touched.
3. `docs/guide/operating.md:317-321` — replace the drift section's first sentence with
   `## The design`'s diff, byte for byte. Both halves of the false claim go together (D11, P12): the
   new text says every console command that writes the record commits and pushes what *it* wrote,
   names all five, and moves "your own edits stop in your checkout" to where it is actually true.
   The `## Your checkout drifts from the tick's clone` heading itself does not change.
4. `docs/guide/operating.md` — insert `## Widening a Task's footprint` immediately after that
   section and before `## The direct lane, small Features, and comparing them` (D12), copying
   `## The design`'s markdown byte for byte: the one-command opening with the indented
   `asf set T-0338 writes+=asf/tick/widen_footprint.py`, the paragraph on the two list fields and
   the three forms, the two-bullet "two refusals are the record protecting itself" list, and the
   closing paragraph that most widenings are the tick's, from a REPORT's `needs writes:`, and the
   command is for the card in front of you. Every claim in it is Task 1's behaviour, which is why
   this Task waits (PD8).
5. `SetHelpTests` in `tests/test_cli.py` — built from the real parser, not a hand-kept string, using
   the module's `cli.build_parser()` idiom (`:277-279`) and `tests/test_readme.py:400-408`'s walk of
   `_SubParsersAction` to reach the `set` subparser. Assert its `format_help()` names `writes`,
   `after`, `+=` and `-=`, and that `set --help` exits 0. Reaching the subparser off the real parser
   is what makes a later edit to `p_set` that drops a form turn this red.
6. `FootprintWideningProseTests` in `tests/test_readme.py` — read `docs/guide/operating.md` by
   `REPO_ROOT`-relative path, the module's existing idiom (P13). Assert: it carries an
   `asf set … writes+=` line; it says `asf set` publishes rather than stopping in the checkout; it
   names the `I3` refusal; the `## Widening a Task's footprint` heading exists; and the sentence of
   P11 — `asf set` and your own edits change your checkout and stop there — is **gone**, asserted as
   a `assertNotIn` on a distinctive span of it so the correction cannot silently regress.
