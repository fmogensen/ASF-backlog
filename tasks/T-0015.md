`tests/test_groom_policy.py`, `tests/test_views.py`.

**Steps**
1. `render_digest(root, date, canonical, groom_text, answers_done_texts, attempts, cap)`:
   - **Answered by rule** and **Ruled by the adjudicator (groom-<date>)** come from each card's
     `## History` lines dated `<date>`, with the reasons taken per PD5.
   - **Spoken for** holds the `[x]` lines and, under the cap, the open questions as `GROOM → ADJUDICATE` (PD6).
   - **For you** holds the barred lines as `NEEDS OPERATOR: <id> — … approvals.<key> is <value>`,
     plus the answers files' `NEEDS OPERATOR` lines and the open questions past the cap.
   - The summary line has §2.7's four counts. An empty section prints `(none)`. The last line is
     `asf.cli.stamp('groom')`.
   `write_digest` writes `groom/<date>-digest.md` wholesale.
2. `cmd_groom` calls `write_digest` on every gated run, including one where everything is empty.
3. `status.py`: a `groom_cell(root, product)` row `Groom`. It reads the newest `*-digest.md` summary
   and prints `<date>: N by rule, N ruled, N for you`, or `not_configured('approvals.groom')`.
4. Tests: `DigestTests` (T11). It adds the digest half of
   `PolicyPassTests.test_second_run_same_date_changes_nothing` (T4) and of `ApprovalBoundTests` (T7:
   the barred Epic appears under For you, naming `approvals.new_epic`). A `StatusViewTests` case covers both `Groom` cells (T12).
