   2 usage or missing list) and its final `check_generic: clean` line. Its body becomes

   ${EXTRA_FILE:+--names "$EXTRA_FILE"}`, run from the repo root, with its own messages around it.
   The one difference a reader sees: a finding prints `file:line: name (tools/forbidden-names.txt)`

   `tests/test_check_generic.py` — keep working unchanged (D11).

   `python3 -m asf.redact --pre-commit` — the staged lines against every source, including the pool
   accounts and the private list, which the tree check does not load without `--extra`.

   `hermetic.GIT_HOOK`). Per P4 that preamble is symmetry with `pre-commit`, not a suite this hook
   runs; it is harmless because `--pre-push` reads refs from stdin and `git rev-list`, never the
   index. The hooks `asf hooks install` writes do **not** carry it — see D12.

   `test_a_finding_prints_file_and_line_not_the_word`.
