**Steps**

1. `actions`: `Launch` gains `base: str = ''` and `base_sha: str = ''` (spec §2.2). A new
   `@dataclasses.dataclass NoteStack` with `item_id`, `base`, `base_sha` — the record write.
   `describe` says `… stacked on <base> @<sha[:9]>` for a `Launch` carrying a base, and
   `stack <item> on <base> @<sha[:9]>` / `unstack <item>` for `NoteStack` (an empty base is the
   clear). `ORDER` gains `NoteStack` **before** `A.Launch`, next to `A.NoteItem`: the launch must
   find the stack on the card.
2. `decide.stacked_builds(facts, config, judged, states, parked, launches)` — a new pure function
   beside `early_plans`, which is its model (`P3`). `[]` at once when `not config.stack`. It walks
   the items in `launch_order` and requires all of: the type is in `BUILDABLE`, visible, not parked
   (`D3`); the derived state is `State.NEW`; `open_edges` holds **exactly one** id (`D6`); that id
   is an item on the record, not parked, not Stuck, with derived state `State.REVIEW` or
   `State.LANDING` (`D2`); it has an open, non-conflicting PR whose branch is `kernel_owned` under
   `config.work_branch`/`fix_branch` and whose base is the trunk, which is the depth check
   (`D5`, dropped above `max_depth` 1); the item has no live session, no open PR of its own, and an
   empty `stack`; its `writes` overlap no Building item's and none `launches` just took. The seat
   guards are `early_plans`': `open_prs(judged, on_pr) > cap`, and no free seat once the launches
   already planned are subtracted.
3. Per eligible item, in this order: `A.NoteStack(iid, base_branch, base_sha)` then the
   `A.Launch('build', …)` with `base=base_branch, base_sha=base_sha` on the item's own work branch
   — `base_branch` the predecessor's PR branch, `base_sha` its `head_sha`. Neither `judged` nor
   `states` is touched: the card stays New (`D1`).
4. Wire it where `early_plans` is wired, on the seats `_launches` left free:
   `if not facts.paused and config.stack:` … `launches += stacked`, `actions += <the NoteStacks>`.
   One `notes` line per stacked item, `STACK <item> on <base> @<sha[:9]>`.
5. `apply`: a `NoteStack` handler writing the card through `record.write_fields` with
   `{P.STACK: <'<base>@<sha>'> or None}` — `None` whenever either half is empty, so the clear
   removes the key (`K3`). In `apply.Launch`, put the base on the launch metadata
   (`meta['base']`, `meta['base_sha']`, when `a.base`), which reaches `_spawn` (Task 3) and the
   ledger row through `_meta`. A stacked launch returns before the `P.STATE` write, exactly as the
   `early` arm does: the card stays New.
6. Extend `tests/kernel/test_stacked_builds.py` with `class StackedLaunch`: one case per acceptance
   line on `S-135956`'s card, all 26, in three groups — the launch and what it leaves untouched
   (both predecessor states, base and fork carried, `NoteStack` ahead of `Launch`, the card still
   New, the edge still open, `_count_blocked` unchanged, the `after` spell ending `building`); every
   refusal in step 2's list, each breaking exactly one precondition of the launching fixture; and
   the two-seat and purity cases.
