**Files**

- `asf/workers/spawn.py` — `make_worktree`
- `tests/test_workers.py`

**Steps**

1. `make_worktree(product, job, branch)` calls `hooks.ensure_git_hooks(product)` **first** — after
   the `repo_dir` check and before `lifecycle.may_launch` or any of its four paths: the worktree of
   an ended run it takes over as it stands (B-0025, B-0051), a worktree on a held branch already on
   origin (B-0046, B-0048), a local branch with no worktree that is reused when it carries nothing
   and refused with its commit count when it does (B-0025), and a fresh branch off the trunk. One
   call before the branching is what covers all four: no path may reach `git worktree add` without
   the hooks in place (D10).
2. Missing hooks are written. A foreign hook in the way raises `SpawnError` carrying the same
   `NEEDS OPERATOR` line `ensure_git_hooks` produced, and the wave does not launch that row.
3. `tests/test_workers.py` gains `SpawnHookTests`, per §3.4 — including the two reuse paths, which
   are the ones a naive placement after the branching would miss.
