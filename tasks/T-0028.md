## Description
stories: S-7150, S-7151, S-7152, S-7153, S-7154, S-7155
writes: asf/workers/quota.py, asf/workers/pool.py, asf/workers/__init__.py, tests/test_workers.py

**Files**

- `asf/workers/quota.py` — `DEFAULT_STOP`, `BAND`, `FREE`/`COOLDOWN`/`STOP`, the rebuilt
  `guards_from_config`, the new `band`; `DEFAULT_GUARDS` and `under_guard` deleted; the module
  docstring's GUARD paragraph rewritten to describe the band.
- `asf/workers/pool.py` — `REASON_COOLDOWN`, `Pool.band`, the default in `Pool.__init__`, the
  band split in `pick_account`; the `Pool` class docstring.
- `asf/workers/__init__.py` — `cmd_quota`'s table.
- `tests/test_workers.py` — `TestQuota` rewritten, `TestPick` extended, `TestWave.run_wave`
  extended plus one wave test, `TestQuotaCli` added, the `cmd_quota` import.

**Steps**

1. `asf/workers/quota.py`. Replace `DEFAULT_GUARDS` with the four module constants of the
   spec's design block (`DEFAULT_STOP`, `BAND = 5`, `WINDOW_KEYS` unchanged, `FREE, COOLDOWN,
   STOP`). `WINDOW_KEYS`' insertion order is `why`'s window order and does not move.
2. Rebuild `guards_from_config(cfg)` to return `{'stop': {...}, 'cooldown': {...}}` in the
   spec's five numbered steps, in that order. Drop the early `return out` (D1). The flat pass
   iterates `WINDOW_KEYS`, so `stop` and `cooldown` are never read as window names (D2). Last:
   derive every unnamed cooldown as `max(0, stop[w] - BAND)`, then clamp every cooldown with
   `min(cooldown[w], stop[w])` — an inverted pair means "no band", not an error, because there
   is no config-validation path here and a raise from a pick would take down a wave.
3. Write `band(usage, guards) -> (state, why)`: `None` → `(STOP, 'quota unreadable')`; then a
   full pass over `WINDOW_KEYS` against `guards['stop']`, returning the first window at or above
   it; then the same pass against `guards['cooldown']`; else `(FREE, '')`. Two passes, not one —
   a stop in a later window must beat a cooldown in an earlier one. Keep the existing
   `f'{uk} {float(v):g} ≥ {t:g}'` shape of `why` and its `≥` (D5).
4. Delete `under_guard`. `QuotaSource`, `FakeQuotaSource`, `CommandQuotaSource`, `NoQuotaSource`
   and `source_from_config` are untouched. Rewrite the module docstring's GUARD paragraph: two
   thresholds per window, 95 / 90 by default, at or above the cooldown one job at a time, at or
   above the stop none, an unreadable account `stop`.
5. `asf/workers/pool.py`. Add `REASON_COOLDOWN = 'quota cooldown — one job at a time'` beside
   the other reason constants. `REASON_NO_QUOTA`'s text does not change.
6. `Pool.__init__`: `self.guards = guards or quota_mod.guards_from_config({})` (D3). Add
   `Pool.band(account)` returning `quota_mod.band(self.usage(account), self.guards)`.
7. `pick_account`: replace the single `guarded = [...]` / `if not guarded` / sort with the
   spec's band split, in the same place, after the caps and the S1 reserve. Free accounts win
   over cooling ones whatever their load; among free accounts the old sort `(load, name)` is
   untouched; a cooling account is eligible only at `load == 0`; a cooling account that is
   already carrying its one job sets `held`, and `held` chooses `REASON_COOLDOWN` over
   `REASON_NO_QUOTA`. Nothing else in `pool.py` moves — `load`, `under_caps`, `lane_load`,
   `take` and the ledger are as they are.
8. `asf/workers/__init__.py` `cmd_quota`: the last column header becomes `Band` (the separator
   row keeps its six `---`), and the cell is `free`, or `f'{state} — {why}'` when it is not.
   `u = u or {}` still guards the two percent lookups, so an unreadable account prints
   `| ? | ? | stop — quota unreadable |`.
9. `tests/test_workers.py`: `from asf.workers import cmd_quota, register` (D8). Replace
   `TestQuota`'s `test_default_guards_and_config` and `test_under_guard` with the four new
   methods below, keeping `test_command_source_parses_one_json_line` exactly as it stands
   (D4). Add the seven `TestPick` methods. Give `TestWave.run_wave` its `usage`
