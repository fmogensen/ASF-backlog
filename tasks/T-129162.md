The spec's **In** 3 and its design §3: `stability` joins `SETTABLE['feature']`, `SETTABLE['epic']`
and `BOOL_FIELDS`. That is the whole change, and it is deliberately the whole change — the
`true`/`false` refusal before the card is touched (`asf/record/new.py:69-70`, `_parse_sets`), the
`asf set` path (`asf/record/setfield.py:148`, through the same `_parse_sets`) and `asf check`'s
per-card type validation (`asf/record/check.py:312-316`, which already loops over `BOOL_FIELDS`)
all come from machinery that exists, with no edit outside this file (PD9). The field reaches the
feeder with no index change (PD8), and what it *means* is Task 2's.

This Task delivers S-80305's last bullet — `stability` is settable on both types, parses `true`
and `false` as a boolean, and `asf new --set stability=yes` is refused before the card is written.
S-80305's other four bullets are the exemption's behaviour under a freeze and are **Task 2's**.
