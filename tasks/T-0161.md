## Description
stories: S-17405
writes: asf/savings/signals.py, tests/test_savings.py
after: Task 3, Task 4

§2.4. One detector per row of the card's table, all six returning the same `Signal`, all six reading
the same window, and each carrying the number that would close it. Nothing here files anything —
`signals()` returns every class, over its threshold or not, and the caller decides.

**Files**

- `asf/savings/signals.py` — **new**: `Signal`, `signals(streams, conv, day)`, one detector per
  class, and `DETECTORS` — the `{class: detector}` dict §2.8's re-read looks a card up in.
- `tests/test_savings.py` — `SignalTests` (new, §3.4).

**Steps**

1. `Signal` as §2.4 fences it: `cls`, `subject`, `reading`, `threshold`, `window_days`,
   `expected_pct`, `title`, `sentence`, `evidence`, and the `over` property (`reading > threshold`,
   so a reading exactly at its threshold is not over).
2. `expected_pct = round(100 * (reading - threshold) / reading)` — computed, never chosen (§1.4).
3. The six detectors of §2.4's table, each reading `savings_for(conv, …)` for its threshold and the
   window `savings_for(conv, 'window_days')` ending at `day`:
   `brief_tokens` (per brief kind, median `in_tokens` over all sessions' median, `preamble_ratio`);
   `gate_minutes` (median `gate_minutes` per landed change, `gate_minutes_max`);
   `rounds` (per kind, mean rounds per landed change, the kind that rounds most, `rounds_per_landing`);
   `step_duration` (per tick step, today's median `seconds` over the window's, `step_duration_ratio`);
   `model_spend` (per kind, median `usd` per landed change over the median of the per-kind medians,
   `spend_ratio`); `failure_class` (per `gates.signature`, occurrences in the window,
   `failure_class_count`).
4. Signal 2's card text follows `conventions.harvest_gate`: *split the suite by footprint* when it
   is `combined`, *one gate per tick* (B-0040) when it is `per-branch`.
5. Signal 4 reads the `steps` list on each `ticks` line — a declared key since Task 1 (P6). A step
   seen fewer than three times in the window is not read.
6. Signal 5 names the cheapest model by re-reading the same landings: per `sessions.model` on a
   landed lane of that kind, its median `usd` per landed change. A kind whose landings all ran on
   one model proposes nothing. The model id is a **value read from the stream** and appears in no
   tracked file of this repository (§1.2) — the tests use fixture ids, never a vendor's, so
   `tools/check_generic.sh` stays green.
7. The floor: with fewer than `min_landings` landings in the window, classes 2, 3 and 5 read but do
   not propose, and the pass prints `savings: <n> landings in <w> days — under the floor of <f>;
   nothing proposed from the landing join`. Classes 1, 4 and 6 do not read the landing join and are
   unaffected.
8. `SignalTests` per §3.4, one test per class: the reading and `expected_pct` over hand-built event
   lists; each class exactly at its threshold is not over; the floor silences 2, 3 and 5 and leaves
   1, 4 and 6 reading; signal 2's text follows `harvest_gate`; signal 4 ignores a step seen twice;
   signal 5 proposes nothing when one model ran every landing of the kind; signal 6 counts a
   signature across days and not within one gate line.
