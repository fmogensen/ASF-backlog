  `f"legacy:{rec['started']}:{n}"` for a status file written by an older `asf`. `:78`'s counter

  `key=log.keyed('stall', tick, job)`, `item`, `text=reason` (PD8). `:113`, `:150` and `:267`
  (`held`, `ruling`) gain a `key`.
- `asf/tick/widen_footprint.py` — `:110`, `:164`, `:339`, `:351`, `:434` gain a `key`: the job for
  a `held`, `f"{job}:{item}"` for a `widened` or `reshape`, the item for a `reverted`.
- `asf/approvals.py` — `:1019`'s counter becomes one `refusal` event per open hook refusal,
  `key=log.keyed('refusal', tick, f"{item}/{cls}")`, `item`, `fields.class`, `fields.path`
  (PD8). `:1036` and `:1042`'s `held` / `hold-resolved` gain `key=hold`. The `ctx.counts`
