   `ITEM_TOKEN_RE` verbatim, and its six strings (`PRED_OPEN`, `PRED_DONE`, `HOLDER_SESSION`,
   `HOLDER_PR`, `HOLDER_STUCK`, `HOLDER_NONE`).
2. `predecessor_question(text)` → the one item id the question names, or `''`: `PREDECESSOR_RE`
   fires and `ITEM_TOKEN_RE` finds exactly one distinct token. Two tokens or none is `''` (D-0013).
3. `predecessor_answer(dep, open_, session, pr)` → the answer or `None`: `dep` is the named item
   (`None` → `None`, an id the record does not hold is the console's); `open_` is whether the
   predecessor's change is still off the trunk; the holder clause is appended in order — a live
   `Session` for that item by branch, else an open `PR` by number and branch, else `dep`'s own
   `Stuck` by **owner role** and capped reason (**PD12**), else `HOLDER_NONE`. Both answers end
   with the holder, because "is it still open" and "who owns this" are one question in the ledger.

   same predicate kind 3's hold rule and the `after:` edges already use, so "still open" means one
   thing across the kernel rather than two — and the holder from `facts.sessions` (alive, for that
   item) and `facts.prs` (open). Returns `(answer, resolvers.PREDECESSOR, ())` or `None`. Called
   from `_auto_answer` after `_card_read`, last of the four.
5. `asf/kernel/settings.py`, `asf/kernel/model.py`, `asf/kernel/ports.py`: `resolve.predecessor:
   true` with §2.10's comment, `'predecessor': (True, bool)` in `SPEC['resolve']`,
   `Config.resolve_predecessor: bool = True` with its sentence, and the `ports` line.
6. `asf/kernel/questions.py`: the `predecessor` line in `classify`'s new block, **last** of the
