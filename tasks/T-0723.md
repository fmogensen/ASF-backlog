and refuse anyway — ASF must not pretend its allow rule won. After this Task one `asf doctor` row
names, by name, every derived rule the composed settings still refuse and the file the deny came
from, and `asf worker-permissions offer --product <p>` prints in full the list the next session
will get.

declares eight. Read **PD2** before touching the CLI: the registration goes in `asf/init.py`, not
`asf/cli.py`; the surface row is not optional, because `surface.apply` raises without it; and
`tests/test_surface.py:50`'s hard count of 50 becomes 51 or every parser test fails. Read **PD9**
before `denied_anyway` — a doctor writes nothing.
