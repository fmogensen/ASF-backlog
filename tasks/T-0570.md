The spec's `## The design` §2 whole, in one commit. After it lands, a Story traced to a reference
that declares `evidence: test` holds at `Resolved / ref-untested` until every one of its
`## Acceptance` bullets has a landed `Proves:` claim on a sha CI went green at or after, and closes
`Closed / ref-tested` when they do. Every other Story in every product — and every legacy
`matrix_path` row, which Task 1 synthesised as `evidence: merge` — matches neither new rule and
falls through to exactly today's five.

**Read PD7 before writing `ref_proven`.** The Story loop already has a `review_claims` local in
scope and it is the **wrong** collection: `review_proven` is review-derived, `ev['proves']` is
`landed_proves` and only the second one is on the trunk. Then **PD8** (build `_green_after` lazily,
the way the Bug loop below already does, and guard for `product=None`) and **PD9** (the two fields
are **one** `GROUPS` entry — two would take the sweep to 327 680).

**Files**

- `asf/evidence/closing.py` — two fields on `Ev` (`:57`), `ref_evidence: str = ''` and
  `ref_proven: bool = False`, with the spec's `#:` comments; two `story` rows inserted into `RULES`
  (`:97`) **immediately before** `('story', 'tasks-closed', …)` at `:107`, verbatim from the spec;
  and the module docstring's rule table (`:11-17`) gains the two rows in the same position, because
  that table is the readable copy of `RULES` and a table that disagrees with the tuple is worse than
  no table. `('any', 'reconciled', …)` at `:98` is **not** moved — C10, it still leads everything.
  `state_of` (`:144`) is not edited: first match wins is already the mechanism, and position is the
  whole of this change.
- `asf/record/ingest.py` — inside `derive`'s Story loop (`:541-743`): one `green_after = None` local
  before the loop (PD8); the reference resolution per Story; the two values passed into the existing
  `closing.Ev(...)` construction at `:549-552`; and one appended evidence line,
  `reference <id> row <ref_row>: <n>/<m> acceptance lines proven green`, beside the existing
  `acceptance …` line at `:739-742`. `settle` (`:660`) is the only place a state is chosen and is
  **not** edited — P12, and the AST fence is in this Task's Gate. The Bug loop below (`:745-…`) is
  untouched; `_green_after` (`:459`) is called, not changed.
- `tests/test_closing.py` — `ref_evidence` and `ref_proven` added to `SAMPLE` (`:11`) and **one**

`asf/evidence/evidence.py` is read and not written (PD11): `landed_proves` (`:1302`) and

**Steps**

1. The two `Ev` fields, with defaults. The defaults are the compatibility story: every existing
   `Ev(...)` construction in `asf/` and `tests/` keeps its meaning, and `ref_evidence == ''` is
   what makes both new rules fall through for every Story that is not a traced reference row.
2. The two rules, from the spec verbatim, inserted before `tasks-closed`. Get the position right
   before getting anything else right — a rule that merely *adds* a way to close changes nothing,
   because `tasks-closed` leads the `story` rules today (P9) and would answer first. The
   `ref-untested` rule is the one that has to be reachable: it is what holds an unproven row at
   `Resolved`.
3. The docstring table rows, in the same position as the tuple rows.
