The spec's design §8, and the card's outcome 7 — which is **the condition, not the change**: the
two-level risk flag is extended *"only if measured incidents justify it"*, so 0.3 ships the
measurement and the trigger and whatever the measurement then justifies is its own card, with this
ledger as its evidence. Two inputs decide high risk today — a path glob the item's `writes` or the
PR's files hit, and a diff over `large_lines` (`asf/kernel/settings.py:252`,
`'risk': {'high': ((), 'globs'), 'large_lines': (800, int)}`) — three consequences hang off the
verdict, and **nothing anywhere counts how often the thing those consequences prevent actually
happened**: `grep -rn "incident" asf/kernel/*.py` is empty at this head (`P11`, `PC7`).

**What the ledger counts is what the rule exists to prevent, not what the rule did** (`D22`). A
count of flagged PRs would rise by widening the globs, which is the decision the trigger is supposed
to inform. The question the ledger must answer is: *of the incidents that happened, how many would a
wider flag have caught* — and that needs the incident, its attribution, and the verdict the flag
gave **at the time**. So `flagged` is the risk verdict that PR got when it was open, and `why` is
the two terms that produced it; neither is re-derived at read time.

**No risk threshold, glob or consequence changes in this Story** — S-131017's last acceptance line,
and the reason `asf/kernel/settings.py` is on Task 1's `writes:` and not on this one's.

**Read this Task from `docs/plans/f-0334.md`, not from this card.** The card's prose is cut at 4000
characters (`PC8`). Two decision rows bind it and are on no card: `PC14` (`asf/kernel/mainmoves.py`
is the precedent for the ledger and both `asf/cli.py` sites) and `PC12` (`decide.py` is shared with
Tasks 3, 4, 5 and 8; `loop.py` with Tasks 4, 5, 6 and 7; `cli.py` with Task 6; and
