§2.3's first two adapters and §1.1's one extraction. The two pure levers arrive together because
§3.3 is one fence over both, and because they are the same statement: a judged tool with one entry
point, scored over a pair, with no clock and no file in sight.

**Files**

- `asf/briefs/build.py` — `detect_kind(row)`, and `build`'s line 495 calling it (PD15).
- `asf/evals/adapters.py` — `matcher_fires`, `brief_kind_fires`, both in `ADAPTERS`; `kwargs_of`.
- `evals/manifest.json` — the `matcher` and `brief-kind` entries of §2.1, byte for byte.
- `evals/matcher/*.json` — at least one `fire` and one `no-fire`; §2.1's own worked example is
  `002-branch-token-of-another-item.json` and is shipped as written.
- `evals/brief-kind/*.json` — `001-row-brief-kind-wins.json` (`fire`, `want.kind`) and
  `002-unknown-kind-refuses.json` (`fire`, `want.error`).
- `tests/test_evals.py` — `MatcherLeverTests`, `BriefKindLeverTests`.

**Steps**

1. `asf/briefs/build.py`: `detect_kind(row)` beside `normalize_kind` (`asf/briefs/build.py:163`),
   returning `normalize_kind(getattr(row, 'brief_kind', '') or getattr(row, 'kind', ''))` — the
   expression that is at `:495` today, moved, with a docstring saying which field wins and why the
   *choice* is the judged thing (PD15). Line 495 becomes `kind = detect_kind(row)`. `normalize_kind`
   keeps its name, its signature and its `BriefError` (P5).
2. `asf/evals/adapters.py`, `matcher` — pure, no I/O: `kwargs_of(given)` passes only
   `match_event`'s own keywords (`task`, `branch`, `pr`, `title`, `body`, `files`, `prs`, `pr_info`,
   `prefixes`, `asf/record/match.py:170`), and any other key in `given` beside `items` is a
   **loader** error, not a silently ignored key — so the check belongs in `set.load`'s validation
   for an adapter that declares its keys, and the adapter asserts it a second time. `fired` is
   `set(ids) == set(want['ids']) and (want.get('reason') or '') in (reason or '')`. `want.ids` may
   be `[]`: with `expect: fire` that is the assertion that a branch is attributed to nothing.
3. `asf/evals/adapters.py`, `brief-kind` — pure: `given.row` is a dict, handed to `detect_kind`
   through a tiny stand-in carrying only the two attributes it reads, so a task never builds a
   feeder `Row` (`asf/feeder/rows.py:169`). `want` is `{"kind": …}` (fires when the returned kind
   equals it) or `{"error": …}` (fires when `BriefError`'s message contains the text) — which is
   how a must-fire task proves the detector *refuses* an unknown kind instead of guessing one.
   A `BriefError` is not a `Broken`: refusing is an answer.
4. `evals/manifest.json`: the two entries of §2.1 — `matcher` judging
   `asf.record.match.match_event` with `implements: ["asf/record/match.py"]`, and `brief-kind`
   judging `asf.briefs.build.detect_kind` with `implements: ["asf/briefs/build.py"]`.
   `evals/exemptions.json` is **not** touched: Task 1 shipped it empty and these two levers land
   with their pairs (PD5).
5. The matcher's tasks — four, from `match_event`'s own rules and each with a `why` that says what
   would be wrong if it flipped: an id token in a branch matching the item it names (`fire`);

   `want.reason: "no rule matched"` (`fire`). Ids in a fixture are item ids of the fixture's own
   `given.items` map and of nothing else.
