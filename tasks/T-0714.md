The spec's §1 and §2 in full — `reserve_plan`'s `held` argument and the seconds-until-free term in
its pick key, `ReservePlan.held` and its `text()` suffix, `held_by_pr`, `reserve_plans` threading it,
and `_held` built in `tick_reserve` and `cmd_reserve` — plus the one piece of §5 that Story 1 cannot
work without: `Queue.hold_s` and the `hold` figure in the `expect` cache. **That inversion is
deliberate (PD3's sibling reason):** `held_by_pr(runners, busy, jobs_of, hold_s, now)` takes
`hold_s` as an argument, `_held` passes `lambda wf: q.hold_s(wf)`, and C4's `except Exception: return
{}` would swallow the `AttributeError` — so shipping Story 1 before `hold_s` exists would ship a fix
that silently degrades to today's plan and tests green. `hold_s` is named in Story 4's bullet and is
delivered here; **Task 4 does not re-add it** and Story 4's coverage is unaffected.

Read **PD5** (what the hold is, and why it is not the run's `created_at`), **PD12** (`TestMeasure`
already exists and already tests the estimate) and **PD13** (P1, P2, P3, P14 re-read) before
starting.
