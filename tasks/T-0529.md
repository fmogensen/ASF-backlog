The spec's first and third **In** bullets and `## The design` §`asf/workers/lifecycle.py`. One guard
at the top of `judge` — `if ev.alive: return None` — and with it the whole point of the card: a
result record while the process is up is *still working*, and the pid is the only evidence that tells
the last result from the first (D1). It waits on Task 1 because the seat this stops handing back is
handed back by a process going away, and Task 1 is what makes it go — and because both write
`asf/workers/health.py`.

**PD1 is this Task's real work.** The guard itself is two lines; the two callers the spec's *"Nothing
else in the module moves"* overlooks are a `None` written onto the ledger and a `None` rendered in
every view, and PD2 measures that the suite catches neither. Read PD1, PD2 and PD13 first.
