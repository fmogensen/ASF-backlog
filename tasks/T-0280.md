§2.6. One section, one answer grammar, one place the operator reads "over budget" (D8): the Epic's

in the shape `index_reader` reads (P9). The ruling gains one form and no vocabulary: `budget $750`,
the `$` required, because without it the grammar is ambiguous with `budget <n>` sessions and the
wrong reading silently writes the wrong field (D9). A sessions answer against an Epic is refused
out loud rather than typing a field `budget.of` deliberately ignores (§1.3).
