The spec's eighth and ninth `## In` bullets and the tail of `## The design` §§2 and 4: `_timed`
puts `seconds` on every process `push` returns, the timed-out line names which of the two keys to
raise, and `publish` puts the branch push's seconds on its success line. This is the Task that
replaces C9's refused percentile with a number an operator can read: *"a week of these knows whether
900 is generous, tight or absurd for this product"*.

Nothing the factory decides changes here. `health.py:533` routes a publish line on
`' refused: ' not in line` and the suffix is on the **success** line, so no judgement, hold,
correction or ledger field moves.

Read **PD5** (`started` is the first line of `push`), **PD6** (what F-0164 does to the same two
functions, and why an appended suffix composes with it), **PD7** (the two assertions that cannot
stay equalities) and **PD13** (why the rehearsal's clock fence cannot see this) before starting.
