already derives `groom-operator-<date>` from a `<date>.<half>.answers` name with no code change
(`asf/groom/groom.py:1279-1286`, re-verified — PD13), and the groom step already calls
`apply_pending_answers` (`asf/tick/step_groom.py:20`). The whole seam is one new function, one
call, one widened pattern and one deletion.

**Files**

- `asf/groom/answers.py` — `_ANSWERS_FILE_RE` becomes an alias of `asf.groom.groom.ANSWERS_FILE_RE`
  (`:16`, PD9); `_newest_answers_file` is deleted (`:19-32`, PD10); `pending_answers_files` returns
  `(path, date)` pairs (`:35-42`); `carry_checkout_answers(product, out=print)` is new, after
  `carry_staged_answers` ends (`:90`, PD13); `apply_pending_answers` gains the one call at `:103`
  and takes its `date` from the pair instead of slicing `'.answers'` off the basename (`:106-107`)

- `tests/test_tick_steps.py` — declared for the return-shape change only. Measured: its one
