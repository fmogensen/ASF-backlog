The spec's `## The design` §2 in full: the stamp gains a format of its own (`asf-daily <date>`), one
reader tells ASF's own stamp from a foreign one from none, `daily_due` is due for anything that is
not ASF's own stamp of today, and the doctor gets a warning row quoting what it could not read.
After this lands, the incident the card was filed for cannot recur: another job's file at
`daily.stamp` is no longer evidence that ASF ran.

C6 is the sharp edge and it is deliberate — a bare `YYYY-MM-DD` is foreign, with no grandfathering,
because grandfathering it would grandfather the exact byte sequence that caused the incident. The
cost is one extra daily run per product on the day this lands.

Read **PD4 before running anything** — two assertions in `tests/test_tick.py` pin the old format
byte for byte, a third passes for a new reason, and that file is on this Task's `writes:` for
exactly that. Read **PD12 before opening `docs/guide/operating.md`**: one paragraph there is this
card's and the paragraph below it belongs to F-0144, whose replacement text is already right.
**PD7** has the row's insertion point and the two positional assertions it must not disturb.
**PD20** has two stamp shapes the spec's table does not name.
