**Steps**

1. `_token(line)` reads the id token of an open-question line — the same group
   `groom_policy.OPEN_QUESTION_RE` (`asf/groom/policy.py:24`) already names — and `_split_lines`
   partitions on `inbox_mod.TOKEN_PREFIX` (`asf/groom/inbox.py:181`), exactly as §2.4 fences it.
2. `groom_rows` keeps every existing gate unchanged and applied to the pair: no `groom_state`,
   `groom_policy.groom_auto` off, or no open question at all → `[]`. Then, per half:
   - the **judgement** row is today's `GROOM_ADJUDICATE` verbatim — `brief_kind='groom'`, job key
     `groom-<date>`, `answers_file` `<date>.answers`, `item_id` the existing `oldest`,
     `open_questions` the judgement lines — suppressed by a live `groom-<date>` session or by its
     own `attempts`/`new` cap, and **not minted** when its half is empty;
   - the **clerical** row is new: `kind=GROOM_CLERK`, `brief_kind='groom-clerk'`, job key
     `groom-clerk-<date>` (through `KIND_JOB_KEY`), `branch` the groom row's,
     `answers_file` `groom_state['clerk_answers']`, `item_id` the first `inbox:` token of its half,
     `open_questions` the intake lines — suppressed by a live `groom-clerk-<date>` session or by
     its own `clerk_attempts`/`clerk_new` cap, and not minted when its half is empty.
   Both carry the same `groom_date` and `groom_file`.
3. `step_wave.groom_state` counts launches of `groom-clerk-<date>` into `clerk_attempts`, runs
   `_not_yet_put` against that job for `clerk_new`, and carries
   `clerk_answers = <state>/groom/<date>.clerk.answers` — `rows.py` is pure and builds no path
   from `env.state_dir` (P10). The existing `attempts`, `new`, `answers` and `oldest` keep their
   meaning and their job.
4. `KIND_ORDER[GROOM_CLERK] = 2`, the groom row's own weight, so the pair sorts together.
   `hold_unlanded`'s `keeps` becomes `r.kind in (GROOM_ADJUDICATE, GROOM_CLERK) or …` — the clerk
   row speaks for a day, not for the item it names.
5. `candidates` replaces `gr = groom_row(...)` with `rows += groom_rows(...)`.
6. `rows.py:337`: `STARVED_SPEC` mints `brief_kind='spec-amend'`. `CARD_SPEC` at `rows.py:334`
   still mints `brief_kind='spec'`. Same branch, same item, same reason string — one word.
7. `GroomSplitTests` and `SpecAmendRowTests` as §3's T3 and T7 describe them.
