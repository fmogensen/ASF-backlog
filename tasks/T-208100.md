**Steps**

1. `asf/kernel/resolvers.py`: `CARD_READ = 'card-read'`, §2.5's `CARD_READ_RE` verbatim,
   `MAX_QUOTED = 20`, and `CARD_SECTION` as §2.5 words it.
2. `card_read_question(text)` → `'acceptance'`, `'gate'` or `''`: `CARD_READ_RE` fires and the
   text names Gate → `'gate'`; it fires otherwise → `'acceptance'`, which is the card's own
   wording for this kind and what a session cannot proceed without; it does not fire → `''`. A
   question that says a *file* could not be read, naming no card section, does not fire — the
   regex's third group is the fence, and the test asserts it.
3. `card_read_answer(it, section, lines)` → `CARD_SECTION % (it.id, section, text)` or `None`:
   `lines` are the section's own lines, cut to `MAX_QUOTED` with a last line saying how many were
   left when it cut, each one indented so the answer reads as a quotation. `None` when `lines` is
   empty — the card really is missing the section, which is a Definition-of-Ready failure for a
   person and not a fact to recite (D-0008).
4. `asf/kernel/decide.py`: `_card_read(it, text, facts, config)` beside `_finding_head`, reading
   the lines with the parsers the Definition of Ready already uses (P7) — `D.acceptance_lines(it.body)`
   or `D.gate_lines(it.body)`, `dor` being imported as `D` at `:211` — and returning
   `(answer, resolvers.CARD_READ, ())` or `None`. Called from `_auto_answer` after `_write_grant`.
5. `asf/kernel/settings.py`, `asf/kernel/model.py`, `asf/kernel/ports.py`: `resolve.card_read:
   true` with §2.10's comment, `'card_read': (True, bool)` in `SPEC['resolve']`,
   `Config.resolve_card_read: bool = True` with its sentence, and the `ports` line.
6. `asf/kernel/questions.py`: the `card-read` line in `classify`'s new block, third of the four
   (**PD4**).
7. Create `tests/kernel/test_question_card_read.py`, one case per acceptance line of S-205271,
   and beyond them: a card whose Acceptance holds more than `MAX_QUOTED` lines answered with the
   cap and the count of what was left; a card whose body has a `**Gate**` fence but no
   `## Acceptance` answered for `gate` and left to the console for `acceptance`; and with
   `resolve_card_read=False` the question reaching the console unchanged.
