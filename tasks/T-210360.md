**Steps**

1. `asf/kernel/decide.py`: `hold_main_red(items, judged, facts, config)` beside the three holds
   at `:842`, `:1024`, `:1070`, computed at `:497-499` where the others are. It returns
   `{item: why}` for every **fresh** launch only — a Ready Task or Bug with no open PR
   (`needed.fresh`) — and holds nothing else: reviews, fix rounds, rebases, the merge train and
   `MergePR` are untouched, because the fix for a red trunk lands as a merge and a factory that
   cannot merge cannot recover (D-0006). It stands while the trunk is red and `blame.verdict`'s class
   is not `guilty`, and returns `{}` on a green or unread trunk, on `guilty`, and with
   `config.main_red_hold_launches` off.
2. The spell's own Bug is **exempt**, and so is any Bug whose body carries the spell marker
   (`mainline.bug_key`'s `main-red: …`) — the Bug is the launch that ends the hold, and a hold
   that holds its own cure is the stall that was a person on 2026-10-13 (D-0007).
3. Plumb it the way `overlap` is plumbed, which is the whole of the hold's visibility: the notes
   join at `:529-531`; each held item gets `skipped[iid] = queued[iid] = 'main-red'` the way
   `:2203` sets `'overlap'`; `IDLE_REASONS` (`:303-313`) gains
   `'main-red': 'trunk red: launches held'` so `_idle` at `:2268` prints it; and `'main-red'`
   joins the two tuples that read `queued` — `_why_no_breach_action` (`:2869-2876`) gains its
   line, and `_stalled`'s exemption list at `:2987` gains it, **without which a hold the kernel
   took on purpose is reported as an item stalled in limbo.**
4. The wait class (D-0008): `'main-red'` joins `WAIT_CLASSES` **and** `WAIT_TARGETS` with a default of
   `30m` (`asf/kernel/settings.py:260-265`, K19 — the two tables differ and a class in one only
   can never breach), the documented `waits: targets:` block at `:117-121`, and
   `waits.classify`'s Ready branch (`asf/kernel/waits.py:138-142`) in the shape `overlap` already
   has: `if (queued or {}).get(iid) == 'main-red'`, before the `seat` fallback. Then a hold past
   its target is a breach on the tick line rather than a silence — which is what made the 8
   "launches paused" ticks unattributable even in hindsight.
