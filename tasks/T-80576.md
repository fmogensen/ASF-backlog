The spec's **In** 4 and its design §5: `release.ci_steps.rehearsal` defaulting to the job's step
name, the criterion in the `1.0` gate, and the same key in `release_preview.STEPS` so the `preview`
gate reads it too — because a pre-release is a version offered. Needs nothing landed: the criterion
reads a **step name** off the forge, not the command.

**Read this Task from `docs/plans/f-0306.md`, not from this card** — the record cuts a Task card's
description at 4000 characters and this section is longer, so the card stops inside the Gate and
the Acceptance does not reach it at all.

**PD2** and **PD3** bind this Task and reach no card. The criterion is **13**, not 12 — criterion 12
is *PR CI healthy* and 11 is *Self-tuning live*, both appended in `compute` (`asf/release.py:876-877`)
— and the Story's "criterion 12" below is read as *the rehearsal criterion*. And the single shared
`step_run_steps` call at `:794` must **not** be given the rehearsal pattern: the step lives in
`release.yml` while the three existing ones live in `tests.yml`, `ci_runs` (`:350`) does not filter
by workflow, and one shared call returns one run's steps — so adding the key to the shared list
would make criteria 4 and 6 read *the step did not run* on every trunk push.

- `asf/release.py` — `DEFAULTS['ci_steps']` gains `'rehearsal': 'release rehearsal'` (`:80`);
  `config`'s `configured` gains `'rehearsal': bool(req.get('rehearsal') or steps.get('rehearsal'))`
  (`:139`); `gather` keeps its shared `step_run_steps` call to the three `tests.yml` patterns and
  adds a second call with `[cfg['ci_steps']['rehearsal']]` into its own fact key (`:794`, PD3); the
  criterion itself, in the shape of criteria 4 (`:485-496`) and 6 (`:514-523`) — `n/a` with *"not
  configured (release.requires.rehearsal, release.ci_steps.rehearsal)"* when the product configures
  neither the step nor the `requires` id, else the newest finished trunk run that ran the step, that
  step green and `requires.rehearsal` landed, with evidence naming the run, the step and its
  conclusion; appended in `compute` after `pr_ci_criterion` (`:877`); and the module docstring's
  numbered list gains it as 13.
- `asf/release_preview.py` — `STEPS` gains `'rehearsal': 'release rehearsal'` (`:31-36`) and the

- `docs/products.example.yaml` — the key documented in the commented `release:` block (`:623-660`),
