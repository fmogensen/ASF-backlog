The spec's `## The design` §1 in full, and nothing else. After this lands, `groom/<date>.md`, the
cards the groom edited, the cards intake minted and the `index.json` they imply are on origin
before the next step runs — so a tick that dies in `wave`, or whose `finish` cannot take the record
lock, loses nothing, where today the next tick's `ensure_clone` resets it away.

Three things are load-bearing. The snapshot is taken **before** `_groom` runs, so the paths
committed are exactly the paths this call changed and an earlier step's writes stay for `finish`
(P11, C3). The publish runs on every exit of `_groom`, the `return 1` on parse errors included,
where it finds nothing changed and does nothing. And `snapshot` returning `None` off a checkout
(P10) is the whole reason the 240 existing groom tests, which build their record with `make_repo`,
are untouched.

Read **PD15 before renaming the function** — the body is byte-for-byte unchanged, and a reflowed
line is a reviewer reading the wrong diff. Read **PD10** before editing `asf/cli.py`,
**PD16** before building the new fixture, and **PD19** before writing the four-subject test.

**Files**

- `asf/groom/groom.py` — today's `cmd_groom` (`:1138`) renamed `_groom`, **its body byte-for-byte
  unchanged** (PD15), and above it the spec's two new functions verbatim: `_commit_message(args)`
  and the four-line `cmd_groom(args, root)` wrapper with its docstring. The `from asf.record import
  publish` stays inside the wrapper, as the spec writes it — `asf.groom.groom` gains no
  module-level import.
- `asf/cli.py` — the groom branch (`:491-494`) becomes the spec's three lines, `_published` call
  gone; and `_utc_today()` (`:428-430`) deleted, its only caller having been that line (PD10).
  `_published` itself and its two other callers are untouched.
- `tests/test_groom_publish.py` — **new**, the spec's §1 acceptance table, six tests, on
  `tests/test_new_publish.py`'s bare-origin shape with the fuller record seed and the product-less
  call PD16 specifies.
- `tests/test_groom_every_tick.py` — the two tick-path tests of the spec's second acceptance table,
  in a new class over `StepsTestCase` (imported there already, `:21`). Nothing existing in the file
  moves.

**Steps**

   in it: not a line break, not a comment, not the three `return`s (PD15). Nothing else in the
   package calls `cmd_groom` by a name this rename would miss — the three callers
   (`asf/cli.py`, `asf/tick/step_groom.py`, `asf/groom/answers.py`) all import the public name and
   all keep working through the wrapper.
2. **`_commit_message(args)`** — the spec's body verbatim: the UTC date when `args.date` is unset,
