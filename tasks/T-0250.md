## Description
stories: S-0004
writes: asf/views/ci.py, asf/ci_pool.py, tests/test_views_ci.py, tests/test_ci_pool.py
after: Task 2

§2.3 and D4: bare `asf ci` stops being an argparse error and becomes the view S-0004 names. The
three sub-commands keep their names, their flags, their exit codes and their tables.

**Files**

- `asf/views/ci.py` — new: `render(root, product, source=None, days=7)`, `as_json(...)`, `cmd_ci`
- `asf/ci_pool.py` — `register`: `required=False`, `--product`, `--days`, `--json`,
  `set_defaults(run=cmd_ci)` on the `ci` parser itself
- `tests/test_views_ci.py` — new
- `tests/test_ci_pool.py` — bare `asf ci` is no longer exit 2

**Steps**

1. `asf/ci_pool.py:1220`'s `add_subparsers(dest='ci_command', required=True)` becomes
   `required=False`. On the `ci` parser add `env.add_product_arg(p)`, `--days` (int, default 7)
   and `--json`, then `p.set_defaults(run=cmd_ci)` importing `cmd_ci` from `asf.views.ci` inside
   `register` to keep the import cheap. The three sub-parsers already set their own `run=`, which
   overrides the parent's default when a sub-command is given — assert that, it is what keeps
   `asf ci reconcile` on `cmd_reconcile`.
2. `asf/views/ci.py` `render`, three blocks under `header.head('ci', product.name, <clause>)`:
   - **the pool** — `ci_pool.load_pool(product)` and `ci_pool.class_rows(pool)`: a
     `| Class | Declared | Live |` table, plus `ci_pool.plan(...)`'s pending steps as a count
     (`3 label changes pending — asf ci reconcile`) and `ci_queue.phantom_lines(product)` (PD6).
     A product with no `ci.pool` prints the one line `cmd_reconcile` prints at
     `asf/ci_pool.py:1163` and stops there.
   - **the queue** — `ci_queue.live_line(product, source=source)`: the line's length, its head,
     what the head waits on, and the free runners per class. When the host is unreadable
     `live_line`'s own `_snapshot_clause` supplies the clause and its `(as of tick HH:MM)` tag
     comes with it — the view reads the pair the `asf status` CI row already reads and learns
     nothing new about the host. `mode off` says so and stops, as `cmd_queue` does.
   - **the green rate** — `metrics.read_stream(root, 'ci', metrics.days_back(<today UTC>, days))`
     (PD13): runs, green %, red, and the newest three red workflow/branch pairs.
3. Every host read goes through `live_line`'s `source=` and `ci_pool`'s injected backend, both of
   which already take a fake; `render` takes `source=None` and passes it straight down, and
   constructs no `GitHubSource` of its own.
4. `--json` prints `{pool, queue, ci}` on stdout and nothing else — the header and the stamp are
   already suppressed by `_stamp_tail` (§2.2) and by `render`'s own JSON branch.
5. `cmd_ci(args)` resolves the product, resolves `root` for the metrics stream the way the other
   views do, calls `render` or `as_json`, prints, and returns 0. A CI view is a reading, not a
   verdict: it has no red exit.
6. `tests/test_views_ci.py`, §3.3: bare `asf ci` exits 0 and prints the table where it exited 2
   before; against a product with a `ci.pool` and a fake backend the pool block names each class
   with its declared and live count and the pending label-change count, the queue block names the
   line's length and its head's wait with a `source=` fake, and the green-rate block over three
   `metrics/ci` lines says `2 of 3 green`; against `ci: {provider: none}` (the sample product's
   shape) the view prints one line per absent half, exits 0, and starts no process — constructed
   with a backend whose every method raises, it still answers; with an unreadable host the queue
   clause comes from the tick's snapshot and carries `(as of tick HH:MM)`; `--json` parses and
   carries the three blocks.
7. `tests/test_ci_pool.py`: the assertion that bare `asf ci` is an argparse error becomes the
   assertion that it is the view, and the three sub-commands' exit codes are asserted unchanged.
