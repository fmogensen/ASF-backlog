The spec's §3. Two rows move out of `### The record`, a `### The groom digest` subsection takes

**Files**

- `docs/guide/troubleshooting.md` — the two groom-digest rows leave `### The record`; a new
  `### The groom digest` subsection after it
- `tests/test_readme.py` — one new `GroomDigestProseTests`

**Steps**

1. Delete lines 88 and 89 of `docs/guide/troubleshooting.md` — the two `(groom digest)` rows — from
   the `### The record` table. What is left there is the schema-migrate row and the rule-check
   timeout row, which are about the record and not about a digest.
2. After that table and before `## The redaction hooks`, add the spec's subsection, its lead
   sentence and its four-row table, verbatim:

   ```
   ### The groom digest

   `groom/<date>-digest.md`'s **For you** holds only actions the approvals matrix holds at
   `human-now` — typically none to five a day. A question a rule can answer is answered, a question
   an adjudicate session can answer is queued to one under **Spoken for**, and the groom's own
   bookkeeping goes to **Housekeeping**. None of those is yours.

   | line | meaning | fix |
   | --- | --- | --- |
   | `NEEDS OPERATOR: <item> — <why>; approvals.<key> is not auto.` (**For you**) | a groom policy would have decided it, but the action class the answer crosses is not `auto`. The only line a rule can put in **For you** | answer it in the groom (`/asf:groom`), or map `approvals.<key>` to `auto` |
   | `NEEDS OPERATOR: <what> — <the question>` (**For you**) | an adjudicate session ruled the question was not its to answer — money, credentials, or an action that cannot be undone — and escalated it with a recommendation | answer it in the groom (`/asf:groom`) |
   | `- <item> <why> — (spoken for: the next GROOM → ADJUDICATE)` (**Spoken for**) | today's adjudicate sessions are spent; the question is queued for tomorrow's, not parked on you | nothing |
   | `- <item> <why> (<section>)` (**Housekeeping**) | the groom's own bookkeeping past the day's adjudicate cap: an inbox card still undecided, a Feature with no Stories, a Story with no Tasks after plan-approved. Not an operator decision | nothing — the next groom asks again |
   ```

   The sentence the old row 89 carried — *"the adjudicate sessions for today are spent and the
   question is still open"* — appears nowhere on the page afterwards. Since `3cc3741b8` a spent cap
   sends that question to **Spoken for** or to **Housekeeping**, never to **For you**.
3. `tests/test_readme.py`: one `GroomDigestProseTests(unittest.TestCase)`, in
   `ProductResolutionProseTests`' idiom — a `_read(path)` that opens under `REPO_ROOT`, and a
   `_section(text, heading)` anchored to the string `### The groom digest` rather than to a line
   number. Its terminator is `lines[i].startswith('#')` (**PD7**), not `'###'`.
4. Four methods:
   - `test_the_subsection_exists_and_states_the_bound`: the slice is non-empty and names
     `human-now` and `none to five a day`;
   - `test_it_names_the_two_sections_that_replaced_the_stale_row`: the slice names `Spoken for` and
     `Housekeeping` — the first mention of **Housekeeping** in any page of `docs/`;
   - `test_it_still_carries_the_barred_row`: the slice contains
     `approvals.<key> is not auto.`;
   - `test_the_stale_spent_cap_row_is_gone`: the **whole page** does not contain
     `the adjudicate sessions for today are spent and the question is still open`. Assert over the
     page, not the slice — the point is that the sentence is nowhere, not that it moved.
5. Leave `docs/guide/operating.md` alone. Its `:307` sentence is loose but not false (P16), and
   F-0128's Task 4 claims that file (P18).
