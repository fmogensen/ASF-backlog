## Description
stories: S-37359

after: Task 3

The spec's §2.4's second half — the two wait limits and the guard's line — and nothing else. After
this lands, the head guard's question (*has this entry waited longer than a whole run takes?*) is
asked against how long a run actually takes on this fleet rather than against 20, and the admission
line says which limit it used and where the number came from.

D17 is the argument: *"on a fleet where a run takes 8 minutes, 20 is too patient, and where it takes
70, 20 fires on every entry."* The lower clamp is today's constant, so no product's limit can get
**shorter** than what is tuned now; the upper stops one pathological run from disabling the guard.

**Read PD7 before writing the fallback.** The two readers are called with one argument from
`ci_queue.py:3330-3331` and `:3503-3504` — inside the PR-admission loop and inside `LiveLine` — and
neither has a record root or a stream. `measured=` is the test seam; the *production* fallback reads
the p50 off `ci-census.json`'s `measure` block, where Task 3's `refresh` wrote it, through
`ci_census.run_wall_p50_min(product)`. Do not read the `ci` stream from either reader: it is a
per-candidate loop. **Read PD11** for the verdict string as it stands today and where the limit and
its source go inside it.

Two things are load-bearing. **No admission arithmetic moves** (O8): the fit, the head guard, the
backfill and the starvation guard are already measured and already pace off free runners — this Task
changes two *wait limits* they read and nothing else, and `decide`'s verdicts over a fixed set of
entries, needs and free counts must be **identical to today's for every limit value**. **`FIELDS`
keeps both keys and both keep meaning "override the measure"** (P7): a product that tuned
`head_wait_max_min` still wins, and its row says so.

  `DEFAULT_PR_WAIT_MIN` and `DEFAULT_HEAD_WAIT_MAX_MIN` (`:296-297`). `pr_wait_min(product,
  measured=None)` and `head_wait_max_min(product, measured=None)` (`:521-535`) keep their declared
