**Steps**

1. Three pure functions in `asf/record/slice.py` beside `levels`/`cut`/`fits`, in that module's
   style — plain lists in, plain lists out, no record read — with the spec's §2 docstrings:
   `feature_stories(metas, fid)`, the Feature's Stories in the Feature's own order (`rank`, then
   id), excluding a retired or removed one; `story_owner(metas, tid, order)`, the first Story of
   `order` that `asf.proves.stories_of_task` says the Task covers, else `None`; and
   `cut_by_story(order, owner)`, returning `[(story, [tid, …]), …]` — one slice per owning Story,
   the Stories in `order`'s order, each slice's Tasks in plan order.
2. `story_owner` reads the **Feature's** order, never the Task's own `stories:` order: the spec's
   `P7` measures that `proves.stories_of_task` returns the order a plan session typed, so a
   cosmetic edit to a `stories:` line would otherwise re-cut a plan. `stories_of_task` also
   appends a Story `parent:` to what the line names, which is what makes `S-137006`'s third line
   true with no special case.
3. `cut_by_story` applies **no limit**: `slice_max_tasks` and `slice_max_files` become a line, not
   a cut (spec `D4`). `cut`, `fits`, `levels` and both limits are untouched, byte for byte (spec
   `## Out`).
4. `plan_slices` (`:198-210`) grows a third element and reads the unit: `[(lead, members, story)]`,
   with `story` the Story under `conventions.delivery: story` and `None` under `feature`. Under
   `story` the body is
   `cut_by_story(free, {t: story_owner(metas, t, feature_stories(metas, fid)) for t in free})`
   and the `whole`/`fits` path at `:208-209` is not reached at all; under `feature` it is today's
   `cut(...)` with `story=None`. The `len(free) < 2` early return at `:204-205` and the
   `len(s) >= 2` filter at `:210` both stay: a Story with one free Task forms no delivery and keeps
   its own Task lane (spec `D7`).
5. The three existing callers move to the three-tuple: `deliveries`' loop at `:256-257` and the
   assertions at `tests/test_feature_delivery.py:168` and `:569`. `grep` over the tree finds no
   fourth outside `docs/`.
6. `deliveries`' slice line (`:286-290`) gains the Story position and three clauses and loses one.
   Under the story unit: `slice: {fid} {story} {n}/{len(slices)}: {lead} delivers {members}` — the
   Story between the Feature id and the counter, which the counter keeps (`K3`); **no** `(proves
   …)` union clause (`K4`); one `({member} also proves {other})` clause per member whose `stories:`
   reach a Story of the Feature other than the slice's own; and, over a limit, one clause carrying
   the limit's name, the measured number and the threshold — `over slice_max_tasks (9 > 6)`,
   `over slice_max_files (22 > 15)` — with the slice still written whole. The existing `after …`
   and `— N started, no shared path: …` clauses keep their shape and position. Under `feature`: no
   Story position, none of the three new clauses, the union clause kept, and the line
   **byte-identical** to the trunk's.
