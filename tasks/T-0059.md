## Description
stories: S-14707
writes: asf/record/core.py, asf/feeder/rows.py, asf/conventions.py, docs/products.example.yaml, tests/test_feeder.py, tests/test_readers.py, tests/test_backlog.py, tests/test_conventions.py

A thin card gets interrogated without a person asking. The feeder still writes nothing and reads
nothing but `index.json`. Runs after Task 2 (`rows.py`) and Task 6 (`conventions.py`).

**Files**

- `asf/record/core.py` — `compute_derived` and `build_index_data`
  (`asf/record/core.py:189-212,253-260`).
- `asf/feeder/rows.py` — `CARD_ENRICH`, `thin`, the `word == 'card'` branch of `feature_rows`
  (`asf/feeder/rows.py:308-312`), and the module docstring's row table.
- `asf/conventions.py` — `DEFAULT_THIN_ACCEPTANCE = 1`, `DEFAULT_THIN_DESCRIPTION_WORDS = 40`, the
  two fields, `THIN_CARD_KEYS` and its `_BLOCK_KEYS` entry (PD3).
- `docs/products.example.yaml` — `conventions.thin_card`, commented out at its defaults.
- `tests/test_feeder.py` — new `EnrichRowTest`, on the file's existing index-dict fixtures.
- `tests/test_readers.py`, `tests/test_backlog.py` — the golden `index.json` gains the two keys.
- `tests/test_conventions.py` — the `thin_card:` block and its two defaults.

**Steps**

1. `compute_derived` adds two keys per item beside `children` and `backlinks`: `acceptance_items`,
   the checklist lines under `## Acceptance` with text after the box, and `description_words`, the
   words under `## Description`. `build_index_data` copies both into the entry. Both are generated;
   no card is edited to carry them, and nothing is back-filled (spec, *Out*).
2. `CARD_ENRICH = 'CARD → ENRICH'`, and `thin(item, conv)` → `acceptance_items < conv.thin_acceptance
   or description_words < conv.thin_description_words`, missing counted as 0.
3. In `feature_rows`, where `word == 'card'`: a thin Feature carrying no `enriched:` gets
   `Row(tier=2, kind=CARD_ENRICH, item_id=fid, feature_id=fid, action=LAUNCH, brief_kind='idea',
   branch=branch_for(product, 'enrich', fid), reason='thin card: no acceptance list to spec from',
   tree_file=tree_file_for(product, fid))`; every other card gets the unchanged `CARD → SPEC` row.
   The two are never both emitted for one Feature, so `KIND_ORDER` is untouched. The `busy` and
   `blocked` guards above the branch are unchanged and cover the new row exactly as they cover the
   old one.
4. The docstring's row table gains the line.
5. The two conventions and their yaml spelling, through the `_BLOCK_KEYS` table.

The enrich session writes only the record — its worktree's branch is never pushed, as the groom
session's is not — so no harvest lane changes.
