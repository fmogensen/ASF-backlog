The spec's `## The design` §4, plus the two guide lines and the one guide example PD11 and PD12 found.
`docs/guide/product-config.md:592-597` still tells an operator that a card whose title reads as money
is answered by nobody but them, and that an auto-filed Bug is exempt from the title recognisers.
Neither has been true since `3cc3741b8`, and an operator turning on `decide_feature: auto` reads that
paragraph to find out what will be left for them.

**Files**

- `docs/guide/product-config.md` — the class table's two decide rows (`:520-521`), the
  `approval_signals` example (`:526-534`), the `groom: auto` switch paragraph (`:566-571`), the
  `decide_by_approval` policy row (`:589`), and the bound paragraph (`:592-597`)
- `docs/products.example.yaml` — the `decide_feature`/`decide_bug` lines (`:351-352`) and the
  commented `approval_signals` block (`:357-364`)
- `docs/guide/operating.md` — `:489`, one clause
- `docs/guide/connectors.md` — `:74`, one clause
- `tests/test_decide_feature_docs.py` — new module: `TheBoundTheGuideDescribesTests`,
  `TheExampleDeclaresAreasTests`

**Steps**

1. `docs/guide/product-config.md:592-597`, the paragraph that is wrong. The two stale sentences go:
   the title recognisers, and the auto-filed-Bug exemption from them (an auto-filed Bug carries no
   `area:`, so it needs no exemption). What replaces them is the bound that exists — a new Epic
   (`new_epic`), plus any class the product has declared `areas:` for — with the line shape it
   produces, `____ (barred: approvals.<class>)`, and the digest line it becomes, `NEEDS OPERATOR:
   <id> — <why>; approvals.<class> is not auto.`. The sentences either side stay: the CI facts, and
   `groom.policies.<name>: off`.
2. The same paragraph says a title decides nothing, in one sentence, because that is the question an
   operator arrives with: deciding a card crosses no approval class, the approvals hook guards the
   work the decision leads to, and a Feature is barred only by an `area:` the operator declared.
   `3cc3741b8`'s ruling said in the guide, so the next operator does not file it again.
3. `:589`, the `decide_by_approval` row, and `:520-521`, the two class-table rows: each gains the
   sentence the spec's P17 says is missing — the class does nothing unless `approvals.groom: auto` is
   also set, since without that gate `cmd_groom` runs no suppression, no policy pass and no digest.
   The `groom: auto` switch paragraph at `:566-571` gains the reverse pointer: with it on, the decide
   classes are what let the groom decide a card rather than ask about it.
4. The `approval_signals` example at `:526-534` gains an `areas:` line (PD12), and beside it the
   spec's own `spend_money` block — `paths: ['billing/*']`, `areas: ['billing', 'pricing',
   'payments*']` — with one sentence on each of the three keys, on case-insensitive glob matching
   (D8), and on the two classes a product may **not** declare areas for and why (D5: an area declared
   under `decide_feature` would bar the card from the very policy that class switches on). The
   `asf approvals` row the spec draws is quoted so the operator can recognise the output.
5. `docs/products.example.yaml`: the commented `approval_signals` block (`:357-364`) gains an
   `areas:` line under `spend_money` and its header comment's key list becomes
   `paths/commands/areas`; the `decide_feature`/`decide_bug` lines (`:351-352`) gain the pointer that
   both need `groom: auto` beside them and that `approval_signals.<class>.areas` is what leaves a
   card to the operator. The file must still parse into a `Product` —
   `tests.test_env.TestExampleConfigsParse` and `env.validate_product_text` both read it.
6. `docs/guide/operating.md:489` — `approval_signals` (commands and paths) becomes `(commands, paths
   and areas)`. `docs/gui
