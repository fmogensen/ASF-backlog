## Description
stories: S-37300
writes: asf/feeder/footprint.py, asf/feeder/rows.py, tests/test_shared_paths.py
after: none

The spec's §`asf/feeder/footprint.py` — one reader, one direction — in full, and nothing else.
`footprint.shared_globs(product)` is the accessor every later Task calls; `is_shared` loses its
second `fnmatch` direction (P9, D2); `rows.py`'s two sites become the accessor.

**What changes for a product when this lands.** Only one thing, and it is the hole: a Task declaring
`writes: ['*']` or `['**']` stops being exempt from every overlap there is. Every product declaring
a literal lockfile (`uv.lock`, `package-lock.json`, `**/package-lock.json`) gets byte-identical
answers from the feeder — measured in PD13. `tests/test_lane.py:2216`'s case is untouched and is in
the Gate as the proof.

**Files**

- `asf/feeder/footprint.py` — `shared_globs(product)` added above `is_shared`, carrying the spec's
  docstring; `is_shared`'s body becomes `return any(fnmatch.fnmatch(glob, s) for s in shared or ())`
  with the spec's docstring naming D2 and the `writes: ['*']` case. `overlaps`, `first_conflict`,
  `globs_overlap` and `_literal_head` are untouched, and the module stays pure — `shared_globs`
  reads attributes and mappings, never the filesystem (the module docstring's promise).
- `asf/feeder/rows.py` — `:1020` and `:1346`, `_conventions(product).get('shared_paths') or ()` →
  `footprint.shared_globs(product)`. `footprint` is already imported in the module.
- `tests/test_shared_paths.py` — **new**, a plain `unittest.TestCase` per class and never a subclass
  of a harness in another module: `SharedGlobsTests`, `IsSharedTests`. It is the file Tasks 2, 4 and
  5 extend (PD1), so it opens with a module docstring naming F-0187 and the four classes to come.

**Steps**

1. `shared_globs(product)`: `()` for `None`; a `Product` answers through `product.conventions`; a
   `Conventions` and a plain mapping answer through `.get('shared_paths')`; the result is
   `tuple(...)` of the non-empty entries. Tolerate all three shapes as D1 says — the lane holds a
   `conv` mapping, the groom step and the widen tick hold a `Product`, and `Conventions` is what
   `product.conventions` is — and take the attribute before the mapping so a `Conventions` dataclass
   answers from its field rather than its `extra`. One function, no import of `asf.conventions`
   (the module must stay importable by the lane, the groomer and the record without pulling the
   product loader in).
2. Narrow `is_shared` to one direction. Keep the docstring's two worked examples (`uv.lock` against
   `uv.lock`; `apps/web/package-lock.json` against `**/package-lock.json`) and the sentence naming
   what the second direction bought and what it cost (D2), so a later reader cannot restore it as a
   "fix".
3. Rewrite the two `rows.py` sites. Nothing else in `rows.py` moves; the argument each site passes
   to `footprint.overlaps`/`first_conflict` is the same tuple it passed before.
4. `SharedGlobsTests`: the same tuple for `env.Product('p', {'conventions': {'shared_paths': [...]}})`,
   for that product's `.conventions`, and for the bare `{'shared_paths': [...]}` mapping; `()` for
   `None`, for a product with no `conventions:` block, and for one whose `shared_paths` is `[]`.
5. `IsSharedTests`: the five rows of the Story's fence — the two True cases, `'*'` and `'**'` False,
   `'*.lock'` False — plus `overlaps(['*'], ['src/a.py'], shared=['uv.lock'])` returning
   `('*', 'src/a.py')`, which is the regression the card's exemption would otherwise open, and
   `is_shared('x', ())`/`is_shared('x', None)` False so the empty default is pinned.
