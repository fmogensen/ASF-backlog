The spec's **In** 4 and the `stable_rows`/`s1_in_window` half of design §1 (D-0006, D-0007, D-0008, D-0009; P14,
P15, P21). Four rows in one order — the cadence, the dwell, the rehearsal, the S1 window — reusing
`release.Criterion` (`:102`), because the operator question is never *may it promote* but *what is
it waiting for*. A row is never dropped when its evidence is missing: a missing rehearsal check is
**unmet**, and only the literal `off` makes it `n/a`.

**Read this Task from `docs/plans/f-0308.md`, not from this card** — the record cuts a Task card's
description at 4000 characters and this section is longer, so the card stops before the Acceptance.

Two decisions bind this Task. **PD10**: `ci_verdict(url, commit, checks=[name])` is the rehearsal
row, and its `'<name> has no run'` is already the unmet evidence — no special case. **PD13**: the
rehearsal check does not exist on the trunk and this Task does **not** wait for it; the row is
built to be unmet and the acceptance proves the unmet row. `F-0306` is named here only to say what
this Task is *not* holding for.

**Files**

- `asf/channels.py` — `stable_rows(product, root, log, candidate, now, cfg=None,
  run=subprocess.run)` returning `[Criterion]` in the spec's order, and `s1_in_window(root, since,
  now=None)`. The four rows, with the evidence §1 writes for each: **cadence**, `now -
  log['stable']['at'] >= stable_every_days` days, met with `no stable release yet` when there is
  none; **dwell**, the newest tag in `log['edge_log']` whose publication is at least `edge_dwell_h`
  hours old — read from the log and never from the tag's own date (D-0005), so a tag never published
  to edge is never a candidate however old it is; **rehearsal**, `release.channels.rehearsal_check`
  on the candidate's commit through `ci_verdict(..., checks=[name])`, green met and anything else
  not, the literal `off` making it `n/a` with the setting named in the evidence; **no_s1**, both
  reads of D-0009 — `s1_in_window` over the record's typed `stage_since`/`updated` fields, and
  `release_preview.open_defects(root, ('S1',))` (`:278`) for an open S1 of any age. A `candidate`
  of `None` makes every row unmet with one evidence line saying why there is no candidate.
  `## History` is never parsed: its dates are days and the window is hours (D-0009, P14).
- `tests/fixtures/channels/**` — the record fixture for the S1 window: four Bug cards, the four
  answers D-0009 has to tell apart — an S1 whose typed fields fall inside the window, an S1 raised and
  closed before it, an open S1 from a month ago, and an S2 inside it. Each carries `severity:` as
  a typed field, which the record requires of a Bug (`asf/record/check.py`), and ISO
  `stage_since`/`updated`.
- `tests/test_channels.py` — `StableGateTest` and `S1WindowTest`, one case per checkbox line of
  S-81206, including the two the fence names: the rehearsal row unmet without the check, and the
  S1 window itself. All four rows met promotes and the head moves; any one unmet promotes nothing
  and that row's evidence names what it is waiting for.

**Steps**

1. `s1_in_window` against the fixture record first — it is the only row with a record read, and
   the four-card fixture is what makes its four answers distinguishable.
2. The cadence and the dwell against `log(**rows)` at a frozen `now`, including the no-stable-yet
   case and the never-published-to-edge case.
3. The rehearsal row against `FakeGh`: green, missing, and the `off` setting.
4. `stable_rows` whole: all four met, then each one unmet alone, asserting the evidence string.
5. The Gate, in the foreground.
