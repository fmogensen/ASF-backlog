The spec's §*The design* §2 and §3: `PREFETCH_WIDTH`, `harvest.prefetch`, `Lane.prefetch_facts` and
`Lane.prefetch_checks` — the per-branch `git` reads and the gate's PR checks issued eight wide
*before* the sequential loop reads them back out of T-0671's store.

The prefetch is never load-bearing, and that is the property this Task is judged on rather than any
speed: it produces nothing anyone reads, it swallows every ordinary failure, and skipping it
entirely leaves the pass computing exactly the same thing at exactly T-0671's speed.

It runs after T-0671 and never beside it: both write `asf/harvest/harvest.py` and
`asf/harvest/lane.py`, and a prefetch has nothing to fill before the store exists.

Read **RD2** (what `pr_graph` already took from the card's finding #1, and what is left for the
checks warm), **RD3** (the warm passes `head=`, or it warms a key the gate will not read), **RD4**
(the predicate, at its current line), **RD6** (`prefetch` must consume its results or it swallows a
rate limit), **RD8** (what the two waves must cover, measured) and the surviving **PD7** (a lock
across the subprocess ships a prefetch that passes its own concurrency test and saves nothing),
**PD8** (two callables in one wave may never share an argv) and **PD9** (no slug, no warm) of
`docs/plans/f-0177.md`.

**Files**

- `asf/harvest/harvest.py`:
  - `PREFETCH_WIDTH = 8`, with the spec's docstring — 8 is what `metrics.ci_from_api` already uses
    for concurrent `gh api` calls (**asf/metrics/metrics.py:1693**, P6/D8).
  - `prefetch(calls, width=PREFETCH_WIDTH)` — `pool.map` over zero-argument callables in a
    `ThreadPoolExecutor`, every answer discarded, `contextlib.suppress(Exception)` per call,
    returning the number of calls made. **The map's results are consumed inside the `with` block**
    (RD6): `gh_limit.RateLimited` is a `BaseException` (**asf/gh_limit.py:40-42**) and must leave
    `prefetch` with the pass, which a discarded lazy generator would prevent. Each worker re-enters
    `mutation_guard.active()` when the submitting thread had it on (D9, P5): read
    `mutation_guard.is_active()` once on the submitting thread, and wrap each call so the worker
    opens `active()` when it was on. The docstring says **never load-bearing** in those words, says
    why the guard line is there for a pool that submits only reads today, and says why a rate limit
    is the one thing it does not swallow.
