§2.2 and §2.8's first half: the three constants that write a signature become references to the
classifier's patterns, and the `ticks` stream learns the dict the classes are counted in. Both
halves are the same statement — the tree stops spelling what `asf.failure` owns — and the second
must land before §3.4 can assert `ctx.counts['failures']['credential']` (PD5). No behaviour
changes in either: §2.1's patterns are byte-identical to the ones being replaced (PD17), and a
`ticks` line without `failures` still validates.
