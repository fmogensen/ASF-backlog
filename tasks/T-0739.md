The spec's §*The design* 3, and nothing else. After this Task every plan the factory writes states
the heading the record reads, and a test refuses a drift to a form it does not read.

**Read PD4 before writing the test.** `H1_ALIAS` matches the colon form and returns `F` — a test
that only asserts "`H1_ALIAS` matches" passes on `# F-0123: a title`, which is precisely the drift
the test exists to catch. Read **PD5** for what this line does and does not change: it is the
convention for plans written from here on; the 146 plans on the trunk whose H1 is `# Plan <ITEM> —`
are not re-headed, and nothing reads them by header.
