**Steps**

1. `SCOPES = ('record', 'feeder', 'lane', 'flow')` — appended, the three existing entries and
   their order untouched.
2. Add `FlowContext` (`items`, `rows`, `inflight`, `occupancy`, `product`, each defaulting to
   empty rather than raising), `_flow_only`, `check_i16` and
   `INVARIANTS.append(Invariant('I16', 'flow', _flow_only(check_i16)))`, exactly as §2.2 writes
   them. `I16` and not `I15`: `docs/plans/f-0092.md` reserves `I15` for the budget invariant and
   that Feature is live (§1.2 P6, §1.3 C4). The guard is what keeps `run({})` empty (P13).
3. Add `FLOW_MODES = ('report', 'off')` — no `refuse` mode: a sink is a standing state, not a
   write (§1.3 C6) — and `CANDIDATE_KEYS`, the eight arguments `plan_inputs` and `candidates`
   share (P12).
4. `flow_mode(product)`: `flags.flow_conservation` as one of `FLOW_MODES`; `report` when unset or
   unreadable, `off` on a yaml `off` (False), on `None`, or on any other value.
5. `flow_context(product, items, inputs, inflight)`: `candidates` over `items` with
   `decision_limit=0` and only the keys in `CANDIDATE_KEYS`, so a `plan_inputs`-shaped mapping
   that also carries `held`, `gate`, `bandwidth` and `failing` does not raise. Every fact read
   soft and read-only.
6. `flow_report(product, items, inputs, inflight, out=print, now=None)`: one
   `INVARIANT I16: <item> — <why>` line per sink and one `invariants-report.jsonl` record each
   (`{'at','invariant':'I16','mode':'report','item','message'}`) through `asf.state.store`, which
   is already registered at `asf/state/registry.py:90`. Returns the findings. Drops no row,
   writes no card, changes no item, launches nothing. Soft like every other check point: a raise
   costs one `INVARIANT flow: not checked (<error>)` line and `[]`; a `store.append` that raises
   leaves the lines printed and the findings returned. `off` prints nothing, writes nothing and
   returns `[]`.
7. In `tests/test_invariants.py`, the asserted registry set at `:382-383` becomes the eleven ids
   including `I16` — in the **same** commit as the registration (K4). Keep that test asserting
   that `I6`, `I9`, `I12` and `I13` are not registered and that `TEST_ONLY` is still exactly
   `{I6, I12, I13}`: R13 is unchanged, and the law is a tick check because the card says so.
8. Extend `tests/test_flow_conservation.py` with `I16Registered`, `TheCutIsNotASink` and
   `TheCheckPoint`. `TheCutIsNotASink` plans the same fixture items twice — `candidates(...,
   decision_limit=0)` and `plan_rows(..., capacity=1)` — and asserts the *relation*, strictly more
   sinks over the cut plan than over the candidates, not the figure, so it survives a change to
   the cut. `TheCheckPoint` runs against a temporary `ASF_HOME` with `env.ASF_HOME` patched, the
   pattern `tests/test_invariants.py:762` already uses — never the operator's home.
