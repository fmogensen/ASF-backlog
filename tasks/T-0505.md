**Steps**

1. `docs/guide/operating.md`: after the `--by-lane` paragraph that ends at `:339`, the spec's two
   blockquoted paragraphs as ordinary guide prose, carrying every fact they name — that
   `asf scorecard --all` prints one row per configured product plus a total; the six numbers it
   prints; that beside each is last week's value and the change; that it reads each product's stored
   weekly snapshot (`state/<product>/scorecard.jsonl`, written by that product's own daily step) and
   not its record, so it works for a product whose clone is not on this machine; that a product whose
   daily step has never run reads `never measured`, not zero; that the comparison is this ISO week
   *so far* against last week whole, and the line says how far into the week it is (`day 3/7`); and
   that the same line is the first line of `metrics/daily/<day>.md`, under the title, naming how many
   products it covers and never which.
2. Match the surrounding section's voice and list style rather than pasting the spec's `>` quoting.
   `state/<product>/scorecard.jsonl` and `metrics/daily/<day>.md` are both already written in the
   tree (P19) and neither is a product convention, so `check_conventions.sh` is unaffected — but it
   runs in the Gate anyway, as does `check_generic.sh`, because a guide paragraph is the easiest
   place in this card to write a product name by accident.
3. `ScorecardAllProseTests(unittest.TestCase)` in `tests/test_readme.py`, beside
   `ProductResolutionProseTests` (`:482`), reading from the checkout and not a fixture — reuse that
   class's `_read` shape over `REPO_ROOT` (`:15`, `:487-489`). PD12: `grep -rn operating.md tests/`
   is empty today, so this is the suite's first reader of that document. Three cases, one per clause
   of the eighth fence: the guide names `asf scorecard --all` and what it prints; it says what the
   delta is measured against (this week so far vs last week whole, and the `day n/7` marker); and it
   says the same line is the first line of the daily rollup and names a count of products rather
   than their names.
