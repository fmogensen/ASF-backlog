## Description
stories: S-29757
writes: asf/views/failures.py, asf/cli.py, tests/test_failure.py
after: Task 2, Task 6

§2.8's second half: relaunches and failures by class, per day and per product, from the `ticks`
dicts where they exist and from the `sessions` stream's own `result` texts where they do not — so
the card's third acceptance line is one command and not a wait (D12). No git, no network, no
`Product`: a reader over two streams in a record root.

**Files**

- `asf/views/failures.py` — new; the reader and `cmd_failures(args, root)` (PD6)
- `asf/cli.py` — `add_parser('failures')`, `--days`, `--json`, `--product`, and two lines of
  dispatch inside the existing view block
- `tests/test_failure.py` — `ReportTests`

**Steps**

1. `asf/views/failures.py`, shaped on `asf/views/tokens.py`: a `_day_rows(root, stream, day)` that
   tolerates a missing file and an unparsable line, `days(n)` for the window, and a builder
   returning `{day: {product: {relaunches, by_class, by_result}}}`. The `relaunches` column is the
   `ticks` stream's own sum; the four class columns are the `failures` dicts summed for the days
   that have them; and for **every** day, including those recorded before this change,
   `failure.of_signature(lifecycle.outcome_class(row['result']))` over the `sessions` stream is
   the `by result` half of the table.
2. The header and the table in the house form (`asf/views/header.py`, `asf.tables`), §2.8's shape:
   `FAILURES — <product>`, then `day  relaunches  credential  quota  transport  work`. A day with
   no ticks prints nothing for that day rather than a zero — the no-events rule
   (`asf/views/readme.py:128`: "`value` stays None, so 'none measured' is not 'measured zero'").
   A stopped lane is named as `lane-N` and never as an account.
3. `--json` is the same numbers as one object with sorted keys, on stdout and nothing else.
4. `asf/cli.py`: `p_failures = sub.add_parser('failures', help=…)` with `--product`,
   `--days` (`type=int, default=7`) and `--json`; `'failures'` added to the view-command tuple at
   `:531` so it resolves a record root through `resolve_record`, and two lines of dispatch beside
   `tokens`'. Nothing else in `cli.py`.
5. `tests/test_failure.py`: `ReportTests`, §3.8 sentence by sentence — `asf failures` through
   `cli.main` with `ASF_HOME` pinned to a temp dir over a fixture record of three days of `ticks`
   lines with `failures` dicts, one day of `ticks` lines *without* one, and a `sessions` stream of
   `result` texts; one row per day per product; the `relaunches` column equal to the stream's own
   sum; the four class columns summed from the dicts; the pre-change day classified from the
   `sessions` stream through `outcome_class` → `of_signature`; `--days 2` reading two days;
   `--json` parsing, carrying sorted keys and the same numbers; no git and no network.
6. The schema cases §3.8 also names (`failures: {'wat': 1}`, `{'work': -1}`, a line with no
   `failures` key, a line carrying every class, and `import_sessions` producing a validating
   line) are Task 2's and already green — `ReportTests` does not repeat them, and §3.8's fence
   runs `tests.test_metrics` alongside, which is where they live.
