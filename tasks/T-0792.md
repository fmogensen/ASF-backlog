**Steps**

1. **`judge`** — the ladder top to bottom, each verdict a short branch: `moved`, `stale`, `no-run`
   (`WAITING_CI`, `gate run not started yet`), `pending`, `timeout`, `unknown`, `red`, `green`. The
   `run_wait_min` bound and the `no-workflow` verdict are **cut**: leave `no-run` unbounded and leave
   room above `pending` for them, so inserting them later reorders nothing.
   `slug = lane.slug or lane_mod.repo_slug(lane.product)` (P5). Every forge read goes through
   `H.gh_json`/`H._gh` (P20).
2. **Green** — `land`: `lane.ci_admits(member, 'trunk')` first (PD10), `MERGING` on every member at
   the gate sha, the ancestry re-read, one
   `gitpush.push(['-q', 'origin', f'{sha}:refs/heads/{trunk}'], …, refs_only=True)`, `fresh_trunk()`,
   `H.record_gate(lane.state_dir, branches, sha, True, seconds, None)` — six args, no keyword —
   then `MERGED` at that sha, `lane.delete_branch(f)`, the landed line,
   `lane.results[branch] = 'landed'`, and the ref deleted through
   `lane.ref_push(':refs/heads/<ref>', …, lease=…, done=lambda: lane.ref_gone(ref))` (P21). A refused
   push leaves the gate claimed and the members `WAITING_CI`. **No job log is read here.**
3. **Red** — the failed job, its log, the three pure readers, then in order: the trunk-alone read
   (the newest completed `push` run of the workflow on `origin/<trunk>` at the trunk sha,
   `release.ci_runs`' shape; red there → every member `WAITING trunk-red` in `gate_one_set`'s words,
   nobody blamed, the ref dropped); one member → `send_back(lane, f, 'gate', <first failing
   line>, <files>)` with the run's URL; more than one → the halves written to the gate's `split`, the
   first cut on the **trunk alone** next pass; an unreadable log → the text degrades to `gate red:
   <job> — <run url>` and the files to `()`, verdict unmoved. One `record_gate(…, False, seconds,
   line)` per concluded gate, red included.
4. **`pass_for`** — replace T-0631's interim branch with `judge` and its outcomes; the
   `lane.dry_run` guard stays ahead of everything.
5. **`Verdict`** — one test per clause of the spec's `**Green lands, red holds**` paragraph (listed
   in `links.plan`). The stub's trap: an argv stub answering an unknown `gh` call with a non-zero rc
   reddens the whole class — enumerate every call.
6. Run the Gate.
