The spec's `## The design` §2 and §3 in full, and nothing else. After this lands, F-1129's row
cannot launch on `cloud/spec-F-1129` when B-1382 closes: the health step sees the hold before the
wave does, finds the branch on no remote, in no worktree and under no local ref, releases it, raises
one `dropped` event and prints one line — and the item falls back to the `CARD → SPEC` row it should
have had all along.

Two things are load-bearing. **The order of the three questions in `branch_is_gone` is the cost
model**: the pass's one `ls-remote` first (P8), then two local git calls only for a branch origin
does not carry, which on a healthy product is almost none. And **`sha is None` returns False** —
that is the failure case `RemoteHeads` documents (a glob, an unplaceable checkout, a failed
`ls-remote`), measured returning None for both in PD3, and nothing is ever dropped on an answer the
snapshot could not give.

Read **PD4 before choosing the call site** — it is after `hold_failed_corrections` for a reason that
is the tick's step order, and a drop deferred to the next tick does not stop the launch it exists to
stop. **PD6 before running anything** — three tests in `tests/test_tick_steps.py` pin this step's
output exactly, and the `if not branch` guard is what keeps them green. **PD5 before the third
question**, and **PD8 before the fixture** — the base harness has no repo, so a `BranchIsGone` test
written on it passes while proving nothing.
