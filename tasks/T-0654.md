## Description

The spec's `## The design` §1, §2 and §4, and nothing else. After this lands, an operator with two
plan sizes writes `provider: plan-small` on one account and `provider: plan-large` on another plus
a `quota_guards.window_usd` block, and the same heavy spec is priced at 40% of one account's 5h
window and 10% of the other's — where today one pool-wide scalar prices it the same on both. Every
sample line records the kind and all three windows, so the next reading can be attributed. **No
placement behaviour changes in this Task**: `Pool.headroom` still reads `five_h_pct` alone. That is
deliberate — the scale lands and is proved first, and Task 2 is the one that starts spending it.

Three things are load-bearing. **`shares`, `cost` and `source` do not move** (PD5): `share` is a
new method beside `cost`, not a replacement for it, and `estimate` gains `usd` without emptying
`shares`. **A derived long-window share is rounded to one decimal and never clamped to 1** (PD6) —
the arithmetic Task 2's green `tests.test_workers` depends on, written here. And
**`five_h_usd_from_samples` keeps its name, its signature and its meaning** (D9): it becomes a
wrapper that returns `five_h_usd_by_provider(samples, runs)['default']`, or — when no sample
carries a `provider` at all — the single pool-wide median it returns today, which is what `:102`
and `:110` assert.

**Files**

- `asf/workers/headroom.py` — three module-level additions and the arithmetic. `WINDOW_RATIO`
  computed from `quota.WINDOW_LENGTH` and `quota.WINDOW_KEYS` exactly as §2 writes it, with its
  comment, placed beside `DEFAULT_COST` (`:55-61`); it needs `from asf.workers import quota` — check
  the module's existing imports first and do not create an import cycle (`quota` imports neither
  `headroom` nor `pool`). `CostTable.__init__` gains `window_usd=None` and `usd=None`, keeping
  `shares`, `five_h_usd` and `allowance` in their current positions and meanings, and keeping
  `source` on `shares` (PD5). The new `CostTable.share(kind, model, provider, window)` — §2's four
  clauses in §2's order, with PD6's rounding on the third. `estimate(runs, window_usd=None,
  allowance=…, five_h_usd=None)` — the old keyword stays where it is, the new one is added, and the
  body keeps its existing `shares` loop and adds the `usd` medians beside it.
  `five_h_usd_by_provider(samples, runs)` → `{provider: usd}`, the current
  `five_h_usd_from_samples` body with the interval grouping keyed by the sample's `provider`
  (absent → `'default'`), and `five_h_usd_from_samples` reduced to the wrapper D9 describes.
  `record_samples(usage, providers=None, now=None)` — the line grows `provider`, `seven_d_pct` and
  `seven_d_model_pct` per §2's shape; an account whose reading is `None`, or whose `five_h_pct` is
  `None`, still writes no line, exactly as today (`:307-309`). `table_from_config(cfg, now=None)`
  reads `quota_guards.window_usd`, maps each run's `account` through the config's
  `{account: provider}` before grouping, and passes both keywords to `estimate`. The module
  docstring (`:7-20`) gains the per-kind scale in one sentence; it currently says the dollars are
  one pool-wide figure.
- `asf/workers/pool.py` — `DEFAULT_PROVIDER = 'default'`; `Account.__init__` gains
  `provider=None` as its last keyword with §1's three-line comment and `self.provider = str(provider
  or DEFAULT_PROVIDER)`; `Account.from_dict` gains `provider=d.get('provider')`; `Pool.providers()`
  exactly as §1 writes it. **`Pool.headroom`, `take`, `untake`, `pick_account`,
  `pick_cloud_account`, `full_reason`, `band` and `banded_seats` are not touched in this Task** —
  every one of them is Task 2's, and `Account.__repr__` stays as it is.
- `asf/env.py` — `validate_worker_pool` gains §1's three-line check, in the shape of its `home`
  neighbour (`:315-317`
