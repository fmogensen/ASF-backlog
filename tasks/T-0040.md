   product is `None`. The three `default='fix/'` call sites (lines 199, 204, 226) drop the
   argument; line 226's `'fix/' if kind == 'fix' else None` becomes just `branch_for(product,
   kind, iid)`, since `Conventions.prefix` already falls back per kind.
3. `inbox.py`: `process_inbox(root, canonical, date, default_bug_parent=None, intake_dir=None)`;
   `intake_dir or Conventions().intake_dir` names the directory. The `why` written into the
   card's History (`created (inbox)`) stays the literal word — it names the *stream*, and
   `groom.inbox_origin_ids` (`asf/groom/groom.py:117-121`) reads it back. The docstring says so.
4. `groom.py:300` passes `intake_dir=product.conventions.intake_dir`. The `inbox` section key
   (`asf/groom/groom.py:240`) is unchanged — it is a section name, not a path (spec, Out).
5. `init.py`: `STREAM_FOLDERS` loses `'inbox'`; `cmd_init` (line 220) creates
   `conventions.intake_dir` beside the stream folders. `asf/record/check.py` is **not** touched —
   PD5 records what that costs. `SPECS_GLOBS` / `PLANS_GLOBS` take `DEFAULT_SPECS_DIR` /
   `DEFAULT_PLANS_DIR` (already imported from `asf.conventions`) as their first candidate, the
   heuristics after (D10, PD12).
6. `match.py`: the docstring's `.sdd-input/reviews/spec-free-plan-r2.md` becomes
   `<reviews_dir>/spec-free-plan-r2.md` and `"worker/free-plan-t3"` becomes
   `"<code prefix>free-plan-t3"` — in the example at line 22 as well as the rule at line 17.
   No code changes.
7. Tests: `test_groom.test_intake_dir_is_a_convention`,
   `test_feeder.test_branch_for_reads_the_products_prefix` (including
   `inspect.signature(rows.branch_for)` carrying no `default`), and
   `test_tick_steps.test_prs_step_prefixes_come_from_the_product`, exactly the three the spec
   names.
