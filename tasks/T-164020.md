   `set_typed` call, as now — the lead never holds `delivers:` without the Story it is for, and the
   record stage's guard sees the whole change in one write. No key at all under `delivery: feature`,
   where `story` is `None` and there is no one Story to name (spec `D5`).

   for an id the record does not hold **or** holds with a type other than `story` (two acceptance
   lines, one finding text — the spec's §4 gives that one wording for both); and
   `{lid}: delivers_story: {sid} is not a Story of {fid}` where `fid` is the Feature the lead's own
   Tasks belong to, walked from `canonical` the way `slice._feature_of` does. The existing
   `delivers:`/`delivered_by:` findings keep their text and their order. A record whose leads carry
   no `delivers_story:` gets nothing from any of them.
