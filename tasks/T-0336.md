## Description
stories: S-31458
writes: asf/metrics/import_sessions.py, asf/conventions.py, tests/test_metrics.py
after: none

§2.5 whole. 120 lines of regex that guessed a tick's boundaries from a nine-minute window, and the
only reason the factory's prose was ever a format somebody could parse. Independent of the whole
events spine: it touches no module Tasks 1–4 write, so it starts in wave 1.

**Files**

- `asf/metrics/import_sessions.py` — delete `LOG_LINE`, `CUT_LINE`, `WAVE_ID`, `WAVE_LINE`,
  `QUOTA`, `TICK_WINDOW`, `STALL_LINE` (`:48-54`), `parse_log` (`:59`), `batch_prs_from_log`
  (`:79`), `wave_centres` (`:89`), `ticks_from_log` (`:108`), the `'legacy-log'` member of
  `FORMATS` (`:56`), its branch in `import_file` (`:246-252`) and the `--log` flag (`:286`).
  `sessions_from_results` loses its `recs` parameter (PD22) — it used it only for `LAUNCH <task>`
  start times and already falls back to the brief's mtime — and `import_file` loses the `log`
  argument and the `recs = parse_log(...)` preamble. The module keeps `legacy-results`,
  `launch_ledger` and `sessions_from_results`; the docstring loses its `legacy-log` section and
  its `--log` line, and the `WAVE HHMM` and `LAUNCH <task>` sentences at `:24` and `:29` go with
  them.
- `asf/conventions.py` — the module docstring's exemption paragraph (`:5-8`) is updated to say
  that what remains foreign in `asf.metrics.import_sessions` is the **results** file: the
  exemption in `tools/check_conventions.sh:30` stays, and the reason it stays is now the one
  sentence that is still true.
- `tests/test_metrics.py` — `LOG` (`:876`) and `SAMPLE_LOG` (`:890`) go; the `Backlog.recs`
  helper and the six parser tests in `:944-1005` go (PD22 — six, not five);
  `test_import_sessions_reads_a_legacy_runner_log` goes;
  `test_import_sessions_reads_a_legacy_results_file` keeps its shape and drops its `log=log`
  argument and the runner-log file it wrote for it. New: `ImportSessionsSurfaceTests`, asserting
  the module's surface is what remains — the four names gone, `'legacy-log'` not in `FORMATS`,
  `--format legacy-results` still importing a results file, and `--log` no longer a flag the
  parser accepts.
- `tests/fixtures/` — nothing to delete: `grep -rl` over the fixtures at this head finds no log
  sample; both literals were inline in `tests/test_metrics.py`.

**Steps**

1. `ImportSessionsSurfaceTests` first, red, naming the four functions and the format.
2. The deletions in `asf/metrics/import_sessions.py`, module first and docstring with it, then
   `sessions_from_results`' signature and `import_file`'s.
3. The test deletions and the one changed call, then `asf/conventions.py`'s docstring.
4. `bash tools/check_conventions.sh` by hand: the exemption still covers the file, and the
   remaining literals are still the results file's.
5. Note for the record: deleting `SAMPLE_LOG` also removes the last `docs/superpowers/…` and
   `worktree-m-batch-…` strings in `tests/`, two of `tools/forbidden-conventions.txt`'s own
   patterns (PD22).
