The spec's `## In` bullets one through seven and `## The design` §§1–3: two documented constants, two
yaml keys, one kind-aware resolver, `conv=` on `gitpush.push`, and the eleven call sites that
resolved their own budget passing the conventions instead. After this Task the flag that skips the
product's hook is the flag that sizes the clock, in one place, and a twenty-first push cannot
disagree with itself.

Two behaviour changes are worth naming before the diff, because both are the point: `lane.py:2179`
(drop copies) and `:2236` (sign-off) are **hooked** pushes that today get 120 s by default and after
this get 900 — they stop being killed mid-hook. The four hooked pushes that read no conventions at
all (PD4) take 900 for free. Every hookless push keeps 120 s exactly, now read from its own key.

Read **PD2** (why this Task is not two), **PD3** (what `check_conventions.sh` does and does not
prove), **PD4** (the fallback must pass the kind), **PD8** (the one test edit, and why it is only
the key), **PD9** (borrow the fixture, do not subclass it), **PD10** (the fence's file walk) and
**PD11** (health's import line) before starting.
