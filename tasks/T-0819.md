The spec's `## The design` §1 and §2 in full, and its `tests/test_precedent.py` whole. When this
lands, the factory can read a product's `docs/decisions/` as a register with titles, statuses and
paths, a REPORT can carry a `precedent:` line that `report.parse` reads as a field of its own, and
`park_note` and `history_note` can turn that line into the two sentences Tasks 3 and 4 will write.
Nothing in the factory behaves differently: no brief prints the register, no park reads the note,
no card carries the clause. That is the point — this Task is a reader with no caller yet.

Read **PD1** (why the field is here and not in Task 2 — 5 of 6 `ClaimTests` red without it),
**PD7** (the `ADR nnnn — ` prefix the spec's own illustration strips and its regex does not),
**PD8** (one id per file, and the three regexes read off `asf.record.decisions`), **PD11** (the
bound is `entries[:BRIEF_MAX]`, not `[-BRIEF_MAX:]`), **PD13** (`entries` applies the retire rule
itself, with `index_reader.retired`) and **PD17** (a card row ends `· the record`) before starting.

`asf/evidence/precedent.py` is pure and total by contract, exactly as `decisions.docs_ids` is: no
git, no network, no clock, and an unreadable directory, an unreadable file or a file with no title
is skipped and never raised. No register is no precedent, never an error — a brief is built for
every feeder row on every tick, and a product whose `docs/decisions/` cannot be read must still
get a brief.

- `asf/evidence/precedent.py` — new. Module docstring in `rulings.py`'s shape (what went wrong,
  what is now read, where); `HEAD_BYTES`, `BRIEF_MAX`, `STALE_STATUS`, `TITLE_RE`, `HEADING_RE`
  (with PD7's optional prefix), `STATUS_RE`, `CITES_RE`, `NO_PRECEDENT_RE`; `entries`,
  `brief_section`, `claim`, `named`, `park_note`, `history_note`. No export, no `asf/surface.py`

- `asf/workers/report.py` — `'precedent'` appended to `FIELDS` after `'superseded_by'`, the same
  name appended to `FIELD_RE`'s alternation in the same place, and one accessor `precedent(text)`
  beside `ruling(text)`. `needs_input`, `ruling_fields`, `failure` and `unpushed` untouched
- `tests/test_precedent.py` — new, the spec's module: `RegisterTests`, `BriefSectionTests`,
  `ClaimTests`
- `tests/test_report.py` — the spec's two `f0262` cases

**Steps**

   the one amendment PD7 decides: `HEADING_RE = re.compile(r'^#\s+(?:ADR\s*\d{3,}\s*[—–-]\s*)?(?P<t>\S.*?)\s*$', re.M)`.

   status is **printed, never dropped**, so the tuple is the list of words the brief's closing
   sentence warns about, not a filter. Say that in its comment, or a later reader will add the
   filter C5 forbids.
