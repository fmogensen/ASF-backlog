**Steps**

1. `asf/kernel/briefs.py`: delete `FINDINGS_ANSWERS_FLOOR` (`:65`) and its one use in `build`
   (`:553-554`, the `room = max(limit - spent, FINDINGS_ANSWERS_FLOOR)` line and the
   `cap_sections([list(item.answers)], room)` call that follows it). `cap_sections` (`:375`)
   keeps its own shape and its other callers; the answers simply stop going through it.
2. Add `answers_section(answers, limit)` → the rendered lines, pure: answers **newest first**;
   each one **whole**, never truncated and never ellipsised; the newest always rendered whatever
   `limit` says — the brief grows rather than cut it; older ones included newest-first while they
   fit whole, and an older one that does not fit **dropped**, not cut. It returns the section's
   lines and the count left out, so its one caller logs and the function stays pure.
3. `build` calls it once and uses the result at both existing sites: the main section at
   `:619-620` and the light brief's own at `:565-566` — so a light review's answers are newest
   first and whole too. The log line becomes `brief <item> <kind>: N older answers left out (the
   newest N kept whole)`.
4. The findings section is unchanged and stays uncapped (`:616-618` and the comment at
   `:545-547`): this Task moves no finding and changes no finding's text. Keep the
   `findings alone are %d chars, over the %d cap` log line as it is.
5. `decide.ANSWER_MAX = 600` (`asf/kernel/decide.py:351`) is **not** touched — it bounds the
   relaunch attempt marker on the card, not the brief, and the brief quotes `item.answers`. It is
   outside this Task's `writes:` on purpose; step 6's case proves the two are independent by
   reading the marker and the brief from the same answer.
6. Create `tests/test_brief_answers.py` with one case per acceptance line of the Story. Among
   them: three answers rendered newest first; a 600-character answer and a 2 000-character
   answer each whole, byte for byte; no rendered answer ending in an ellipsis the brief added;
   the newest whole with `max_appended_chars` set far below its length; an older answer that does
   not fit left out rather than cut; the one log line with its two counts; one answer and no
   limit rendering exactly that answer; no answers rendering no `## Operator answers` heading at
   all; a findings set over the cap still whole beside the answers; the relaunch marker short
   (`decide.ANSWER_MAX`) while the brief's copy of the same answer is whole; the section present
   on a fix round, on a correction and on a document round; and `answers_section` the same
   answer for the same arguments.
