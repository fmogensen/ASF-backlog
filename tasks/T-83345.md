One new module in `asf/evidence/`, beside `rulings` and `precedent` in the family that answers
"what the record already settled" (C3), and the flag that turns it on. Pure but for two reads: the
card (through `rulings.standing`) and the register (through `precedent.entries`). It writes one
line, in `file_rulings`' own shape, and nothing else. No caller yet — Task 3 is the caller — so
this Task's whole proof is its own test module plus the two landed modules it must not disturb.

**Files**

- `asf/evidence/rulepass.py` — new. The module docstring is the spec's `§1` block verbatim.
  Then, in order:
  - `FLAG = 'rule_pass'`, `JOB = 'rule-pass'`, `EVENT = 'rule_pass'`, `ON_WORDS` reused from
    `asf.harvest.mechanical` (imported, not copied — one list of what "on" means).
  - `enabled(product)` — `mechanical.enabled`'s body with `FLAG` swapped: `product.flag(FLAG,
    'off')`, falling back to `product.conventions.flag`, `True` or a word in `ON_WORDS`.
  - `Dispute` — frozen dataclass, the spec's five fields: `item`, `branch`, `finding` (tuple),
    `entries` (tuple of `(label, text)`), `where`.
  - `COMMIT_SHAPE`, `WORDING`, `FORMATTING` — compiled case-insensitive alternations over the
    spec's own word lists, and `COSMETIC = (('commit-shape', COMMIT_SHAPE), ('wording', WORDING),
    ('formatting', FORMATTING))`; `CLASS_WHY` — one sentence per class, each **ending in a full
    stop** (**PD9**).
  - `LICENCE`, `MONEY`, `SECURITY`, `CUSTOMER`, `CORRECTNESS`, `COVERAGE` and
    `NEVER_WAIVE = (LICENCE, MONEY, SECURITY, CUSTOMER, CORRECTNESS, COVERAGE)` — the spec's
    lists, the first four the adjudicate brief's own four classes (P14).
  - `classify(text)` — `''` the moment any `NEVER_WAIVE` pattern matches; else the first
    `COSMETIC` class that does; else `''`.
  - `Ruling` — frozen dataclass, six fields: `rules`, `text`, `cites`, `finding`, `why`, `where`
    (**PD4**).
  - `standing(product, item)` — the card's rulings from the record clone and from
    `backlog_dir`, each through `rulings.standing` handed a stand-in whose only attribute is
    `backlog_dir`, concatenated (**PD6**).
  - `cosmetic(dispute, label, text)` and `precedent(product, items, dispute, label, text)` —
    `(rule, why, cite)` or `None`, the spec's two rules.
  - `rule(product, dispute, items)` — every finding or none (C5).
  - `history_line(ruling, now=None)` — the spec's exact shape, `JOB` in the job slot.
  - `waived(product, item, finding)` — the stamp of the rule-pass ruling already over this key,
    or `''`.
  - `dispute(product, row, items)` — **PD11**'s two positive tests, `None` otherwise.
  - `apply(product, row, items, out, root=None, dry_run=False)` — total; `root` is the record
    root the ruling is filed in, and no `root` means no write (**PD5**).
- `asf/conventions.py` — `'rule_pass'` added to `KNOWN_FLAGS` (`:403`), with the one-line comment
  the neighbouring entries carry (**PD14**).
- `docs/products.example.yaml` — one commented line directly under `mechanical`'s (`:346`), in
  the same one-line style, naming the default: off (**PD14**, **PD20**).
- `tests/test_rulepass.py` — new. Every S-78054 acceptance line, over built `Dispute`s and a
  fake product with a `docs/decisions/` folder — the fixture shape `tests/test_precedent.py`
  already uses. No `screen` cases here: those are Task 3's, in this same module.
- `tests/test_env.py` — `'rule_pass': 'on'` into `_PLANNED_FLAG_VALUES` (`:761`), which
  `:837` asserts against `KNOWN_FLAGS` (**PD14**).
