## Description
stories: S-32556
writes: asf/feeder/rows.py, tests/test_feeder.py
after: none

The spec's §6: one helper, two call sites (D11), so the S1 lane and the Feature lane can never disagree
about what a flag means. The row launches nothing and costs no slot, so by P8 `hold_unlanded` leaves it
alone and by P9 a flagged S1 stops cutting the tier-2 rows behind it. Nothing here waits on Task 1 or
Task 2: by P6 `belongs_to` reaches `index.json` with no plumbing, and this Task's fixtures write the
field themselves.

**Files**

- `asf/feeder/rows.py` — `foreign_row`, its call in `bug_rows`, its call in `_one_feature_rows`
- `tests/test_feeder.py` — `ForeignCardRowTests`

**Steps**

1. `foreign_row(product, item, tier, feature_id, brief_kind, kind)` beside `console_amend_row`
   (`asf/feeder/rows.py:1032`), byte for byte as §6 gives it — docstring, `NEEDS_DECISION` action, the
   `asf move <id> --to <name> (or --remove)` reason, `waits_on='decision'`. It reads `belongs_to` off
   the item and nothing else: no git, no gh, no filesystem (P7).
2. `bug_rows`: the first branch of the per-Bug ladder, ahead of `busy` (D12) — after the severity and
   open/decided filter that selects the Bug at all, before `if b['id'] in busy`. A card that is not this
   product's work is waiting on a person, not on a session, and a `WAITS ON session` row would hide the
   one row the operator needs to see.
3. `_one_feature_rows`: at the head, after `fid`, `stage` and `word` are computed and **before** the
   stalemate and `busy` branches — but not for `word in ('plan-approved', 'building')`, which keeps
   handing the Feature's Tasks their own `PLAN → CODE` rows (PD5, the spec's Out). `tier=2`,
   `brief_kind='spec'`, `kind` the Feature's own row kind.
4. `tests/test_feeder.py`: `ForeignCardRowTests` — a flagged S1 Bug is one `NEEDS DECISION` row naming
   `asf move` and its `waits_on` is `decision`; `hold_unlanded` leaves it alone even with an unlanded
   `after:`; with one free slot and two tier-2 rows behind it, `tiers.select` now emits the tier-2 rows
   (P9's measurement, as the row that used to cut them no longer launches); a flagged Feature at
   `spec-approved` yields the `NEEDS DECISION` row and no spec session; a flagged Feature at `building`
   yields its Tasks' rows unchanged; an unflagged card's rows are byte-identical to today's.
   `NoS1S2BugIsInvisible` keeps passing: a flagged S1 is still never silent, it now says something else.
