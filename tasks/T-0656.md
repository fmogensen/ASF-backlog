The spec's `## The design` §2's `DEFAULT_MINUTES` and `minutes`, and §3's reset credit — the `held`
factor Task 2 left at `1.0`. After this lands, an account at 93% of a 5h window that resets in six
minutes is the place a fifty-three-minute spec goes, because only the eleven percent of the run
landing before the reset is charged to the current reading and the rest lands in a window that will
be empty. Today that is the one account the pool refuses while it loads a 60% account further.

Two things are load-bearing. **`held` is clamped to `[0, 1]` and is `1.0` when the source names no
reset** — a reset already past gives `0` and the launch costs the current reading nothing, a reset
beyond the run's end gives `1` and is identical to naming none, and both ends are asserted. And
**the credit is linear in the run's span** (D7), which errs toward holding the launch back because
real burn is front-loaded; do not model a curve and do not treat any reset inside the run as full
relief, which would place a run on an account it drains before the reset arrives.
