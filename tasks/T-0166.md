§2.3. The cause strings harvest and health already write become named constants in the module that
writes them, and `mechanical(kind, text)` is the one place that says whether a correction is a
chore. No string on disk changes, so every ledger line already written classifies. Nothing calls
`mechanical` yet — Task 3 does.

**Files**

- `asf/workers/lifecycle.py` — `GATE`, `CONFLICT`, `MERGE`, `NAMING`, `DIED`, `CAUSES`,
  `NAMED_TEST_RE`, `MECHANICAL`, `mechanical()`, and `TIMED_OUT` moved here (P6), beside
  `UNPUSHED`/`PUSHED_AFTER_STOP`/`EMPTY` (`:88`, `:589-591`); the module docstring gains the
  vocabulary and the one sentence that says what mechanical means.
- `asf/harvest/harvest.py` — `TIMED_OUT = lifecycle.TIMED_OUT` (`:135`, so `timed_out_line` and
  every existing reader are unchanged); `hold_with_correction`'s two `kind == 'gate'` tests
  (`:886`, `:890`) and the `hold(entry, 'conflict', …)` / `hold(stacked[0], 'gate', …)` call sites
  (`:956`, `:973`) take the constants; `lane_refusal`'s two returned kinds (`:757`, `:761`) take
  `lifecycle.MERGE` and `lifecycle.NAMING`.
- `asf/workers/health.py` — already passes `lifecycle.PUSHED_AFTER_STOP` and `lifecycle.UNPUSHED`
  (`:278`, `:310`, `:317`); only the module docstring's cause list is touched.
- `asf/tick/step_health.py` — the two `'died'` literals (`:91`, `:173`) take `lifecycle.DIED`.
- `tests/test_lifecycle.py` — `MechanicalCauseTests` (new).
- `tests/test_harvest.py` — `HoldCauseTests` (new, the structural half).

**Steps**

1. The constants of §2.3 in `asf/workers/lifecycle.py`, in the hold section beside `UNPUSHED`:
   `GATE`, `CONFLICT`, `MERGE`, `NAMING`, `DIED`, and `CAUSES = (GATE, CONFLICT, MERGE, NAMING,
   DIED, UNPUSHED, PUSHED_AFTER_STOP, EMPTY)` — `EMPTY` included per P7.
2. `TIMED_OUT = 'gate timed out'` moves here with the B-0082 comment it carries in harvest, and
   `asf/harvest/harvest.py:135` becomes `TIMED_OUT = lifecycle.TIMED_OUT`. Nothing else in harvest
   changes about it; `timed_out_line` is untouched.
3. `NAMED_TEST_RE` exactly as §2.3 fences it, with its comment naming the two runner shapes;
   `MECHANICAL = (UNPUSHED, PUSHED_AFTER_STOP, CONFLICT, MERGE, NAMING, EMPTY)`.
4. `mechanical(kind, text='')` as §2.3 fences it, with P6's guard first:
   a `text` whose stripped form starts with `TIMED_OUT` is never mechanical; then
   `kind in MECHANICAL` → True; then `kind == GATE and bool(NAMED_TEST_RE.search(text or ''))`.
   The docstring says why the guard is there and names B-0082.
5. Harvest and step_health pass the constants where they pass literals. The values are identical,
   so no ledger line, no fixture and no correction text changes — that is what makes step 7's
   sweep a structural test and not a behavioural one.
6. `MechanicalCauseTests` in `tests/test_lifecycle.py`: `mechanical()` over every member of
   `CAUSES` with an empty text; `NAMED_TEST_RE` over a unittest `FAIL: test_x (tests.test_y.C.test_x)`
   line and a pytest `FAILED tests/test_y.py::test_x` line → True, and over
   `check_generic: forbidden name found`, `ruff: E501 line too long` and
   `gate timed out after 900 s: python3 -m unittest tests.test_all` → False (the last is P6's
   guard, and is the case the regex alone gets wrong).
7. `HoldCauseTests` in `tests/test_harvest.py`: walk `asf/harvest/harvest.py`,
   `asf/workers/health.py` and `asf/tick/step_health.py` with `ast`, collect the `kind` argument of
   every call to `lifecycle.hold`, `hold_with_correction` and the local `hold` closures, plus every
   `(kind, text)` tuple `lane_refusal` returns, and assert each resolves — as a string constant or
   as an `Attribute` on `lifecycle` — to a member of `CAUSES`. A new cause string that skips the
   vocabulary fails here, not in production.
