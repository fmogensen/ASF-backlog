The spec's design whole — §1.1 through §1.5, §2, §3 and §4 — because **PD1** measures that no cut of
it leaves two Tasks that can each pass their own fences, and because the only path two candidate
Tasks would not share is the one line in `asf/proves.py` that **PD2** measures as inseparable from
§1.4. Nothing in `asf/` imports the new module until §3's six lines of parser land, so the ordering
inside the commit is free; what is not free is landing §1.4 without §2 (**PD2**) or §3's row without

**Files**

- `asf/record/reconcile_proofs.py` — **new file.** `CLAIM_KEYS` and the frozen `Row` (§1.1);
  `read_rows(path)` (§1.2); the seven verdict constants, `TICKING`, the frozen `Verdict` and
  `reconcile(canonical, ev, rows, on_trunk, body_of)` (§1.3); `apply(root, canonical, verdicts,
  stamp)` (§1.4); `cmd_reconcile_proofs(args, root)` (§1.5). The docstrings the spec writes are the
  module's, and its own signatures are binding — with the two reader names of **PD4** and **PD5**,
  which change no signature. The module is under `asf/record/`, so `tools/check_record_writers.sh`
  refuses any `open(…, 'w')` in it: the one write goes through `asf.record.writer.write_card`.
- `asf/proves.py` — one line: `PROVED_ENTRY_RE` at `:310` gains `(?:ingest|reconcile)`, with the
  spec's §2 comment. `UNTICK_ENTRY_RE` (`:312`) is **not** touched — it discards a line number
  whoever proved it, so `asf untick` reverses a reconciled tick exactly as it reverses an ingested
  one. Nothing else in the file moves.
- `asf/cli.py` — the four `add_argument` lines and the parser, beside `audit-proofs` (`:322-327`);
  the five-line dispatch in `audit-proofs`' own shape (`:813-818`), with the lazy

  dispatch does it, and `_published(…, 'record: reconcile-proofs --apply')` on the writing half only
  (`:692-701`).

- `tests/test_reconcile_proofs.py` — **new file.** Eight classes, named by the five fences:
  `VerdictTests`, `PreferenceTests`, `AdmitTests`, `LedgerReaderTests`, `RefusalTests`,
  `IdempotenceTests`, `HistoryEntryTests`, `CommandTests`. Built on
