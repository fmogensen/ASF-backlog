The spec's first three **In** bullets and `## The design` §1 and §2, plus the first two of §4's three
new tests and the third inside `GateLedgerTests`. One keyword, `timing=True`, decided once in
`gate_one_set` from `lane.dry_run` and threaded through four functions the way `ledger` already is.

This is the whole product change. It is small — one f-string, four signatures, nine call sites — and
every risk in it is in the tests: read **PD1** and **PD2** before writing a line of
`tests/test_harvest.py`, and **PD3** before writing a line of `asf/harvest/lane.py`.

Nothing here is a convention and no new module is added, so `check_conventions.sh` and
`check_generic.sh` are nets rather than obstacles — both are in the Gate (P12).

**Files**

- `asf/harvest/harvest.py` — `product_gate`'s signature (`:850`), its docstring (`:851-855`) and the
  `timed` closure's one line (`:870-872`)
- `asf/harvest/lane.py` — `red_on_trunk` (`:2553`), `gate_groups` (`:2562-2563`), `confirmed_group`
  (`:2599`), and `gate_one_set`'s one decision plus its two calls (`:2744`, `:2779-2780`, `:2785`)
- `tests/test_harvest.py` — a new `GateTimingLineTests` after `PRODUCT_REPOS` (`:2704`); one new test
  in `GateLedgerTests` (`:2707`)

**Steps**

1. `asf/harvest/harvest.py:850` — `def product_gate(tmp, conv, asf_repo, out=None, only=None,
   timing=True):`. The keyword is `timing` and **not** `timed`: the inner closure at `:870` is already
   called `timed` and a parameter of that name would shadow it (the spec says so at `:131-132`).
2. The docstring's last sentence (`:855`) gains the spec's §1 text byte for byte — the `timing=False`
   clause naming the rehearsal, the line it prints, and where the real duration still goes
   (`gate_groups`' own clock). Keep the existing first sentence and the `only` sentence untouched.
3. The closure body (`:871-872`) becomes the spec's two lines byte for byte:
   `secs = f'{time.monotonic() - started:.0f}s, ' if timing else ''` then
   `out(f'gate: {count[0]} modules, {secs}{verdict}')`. The closure keeps its name; all three call
   sites (`:882`, `:888`, `:890`) are unchanged (PD5). `started = time.monotonic()` at `:867` stays
   exactly where and as it is (PD6).
4. `asf/harvest/lane.py` — the four signatures, each gaining `timing=True` **last**, after the
   keyword it already ends on: `red_on_trunk(…, modules=None, timing=True)`;
   `gate_groups(…, trunk_green=False, ledger=None, timing=True)`;
   `confirmed_group(…, announce=True, ledger=None, timing=True)`.
5. The nine call sites of **PD3**, each passing `timing=timing`: `:2556`
   `H.product_gate(tmp, conv, asf_repo, out, only=modules, timing=timing)`; `:2575`
   `H.product_gate(tmp, conv, asf_repo, out, only, timing=timing)`; `:2584`
   `red_on_trunk(tmp, trunk, conv, asf_repo, out, red, timing=timing)`; `:2593` **and** `:2596`, the
   bisection's two recursive `gate_groups` calls (**PD4** — both, or a bisecting rehearsal prints a
   clock in the lines it bisects into); `:2603-2604` `gate_groups(…, ledger=ledger, timing=timing)`
   inside `confirmed_group`.
6. `gate_one_set` (`:2744`) is the **only** place the value is decided: `timing = not lane.dry_run`,
   beside the existing `announce = …` at `:2750`. Then `:2779-2780`'s nine positional arguments gain
   a tenth, `timing`, and `:2785`'s `red_on_trunk(tmp, trunk, conv, asf_repo, out)` gains
   `timing=timing`. Nothing else in the file reads it.
7. `gate_groups`' docstring gains one sentence for `timing`, in the shape its `ledger` sentence
   already has. `red_on_trunk`'s one-line docstring and `confirmed_group`'s are left as they are —
   they pass it on and do not decide it.
8. `tests/test_harvest.py`, **after** `PRODUCT_REPOS` at `:2704` (PD1): `class
   GateTimingLineTests(unittest.TestCase)` with a docstring naming D4 — a rehearsal and a journal are
   different documents and one token is where they diff
