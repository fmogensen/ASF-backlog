**Steps**

1. `classify` gains `refusal=None` as its **last** keyword and appends `refusals.clause(refusal)`
   to the `:467` arm and to no other (§3). The docstring's "Pure: no gh, no git, no clock" stands
   and is now load-bearing: `classify` reads no file, so the test may pass a `product` whose state
   directory does not exist (C8). `timed out`, `stalled` and "never appeared" stay bare — `sync`
   branches on those prefixes (`:673`) and a clause there would have to be parsed back off.
2. `REPORT_MISSING = 'without the report commit'` as a module constant, so the one arm that earns
   a log read is named rather than matched on a sentence fragment at the call site (§3).
3. `remote.evidence(run, s, elapsed, now, client=None, refusal=None)` and
   `cloud.classify(view, report, elapsed, s.timeout_min, tid, refusal=refusal)` at `:583`, per
   **PD1**. One argument, trailing and defaulted, so every other caller is byte-identical.
   Measured: `tests/test_remote.py:460` pins the bare sentence and keeps passing — its fixture has
   no ledger and no record refusal.
4. In `sync`, after `tok = run['pid']`, `refusal = refusals.last(product, job, run)` — once per
   run, before the arms, where `product`, `job` and `run` are all in hand. Pass it to
   `remote.evidence` at `:638` and, as `refusal=refusal`, to `classify` at `:649` (which takes
   `s.timeout_min`, not `timeout` — **PD1**). The `no workflow run` arm at `:640` takes none.
5. §3's leg-5 block, placed after the three arms and **before** the `stalled` handling, so the
   reason `cloudpid.record` stores at `:701` carries the clause: `if status == DEAD and refusal is
   None and REPORT_MISSING in why:` then `refusal = refusals.recognise(_run_log_tail(run, s,
   remote_client, gh))` and `why += refusals.clause(refusal)` when it answered. Measured safe
   against the `stalled` reassignment at `:671`: `beat` is only taken when `status == WORKING`, so
   a DEAD reason from the arms is never overwritten.
6. `_run_log_tail(run, s, remote_client, gh)` with §4's docstring byte for byte — the dead run's
   own output as text, or `''`, through the runtime already in hand, never raising: `remote.
   is_remote(run)` → `remote.run_log_summary(run, s, remote_client)`, unchanged (P11, and **PD7**
