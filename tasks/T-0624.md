The spec's §3, and §4's `awaiting_tick` — the predicate and its own mirror, in one landing, because
a helper that must return exactly the landings `_in_prod` refuses cannot be written in a different
diff from `_in_prod` without drifting from it. When this lands, a `manual` Feature closes on the
tick, a childless `manual` Feature is gated on its own landing instead of hard-`False` (D5), and a
child that landed as a direct trunk commit is ticked by its landing sha (D4). Nothing yet *says* an
item is waiting — that is Task 3, which is why `asf/tick/step_harvest.py` is not on this list.

Read **PD4** (three call sites, at other lines than the spec's, and one passing both a child list
and an `own_sha`), **PD5** (the Story site cannot reach the childless path and is left alone),
**PD3** (the awaiting landings ride on `_Derived`, not a seventh return slot) and **PD6** (the two
test literals that must change, and why they are the proof) before starting.

**Files**

- `asf/record/ingest.py` — `_in_prod` gaining `own_pr` and reading through `checked.matches`; the
  two Feature call sites passing `own_pr`; `awaiting_tick`; `_Derived.awaiting` and `settle`'s
  `awaiting=` argument.
- `tests/test_ingest.py` — the two `checked={…}` literals; **new class** `TheGateCanBePassed`; the
  two new cases inside `AutoDeployIsTheProdTick`.

   childless branch returning False without a reached `own_sha` and otherwise

   `_child_landing` and returning False on the first `not checked.matches(ticked, pr, sha)` (D4);
   the `_merged_in_prod` tail unchanged. `prod_mode` is never `'ci'` — do not write a third branch
   for a value prod cannot hold (PD13).
3. Rewrite `_in_prod`'s docstring's last clause to the spec's sentence: *"`manual` — a person also
   ticked each child's landing into the product's checked list (`asf checked`)."* Keep every other
