§2.5: a step in the manifest, a weekly stamp beside the daily one, a `--improve` force flag, and a
thin `run(ctx)`. Runs last (P5): it calls Task 2's `cmd_improve`, and it is the Task that makes
`improve` a name the example's clock may use.

**Files**

- `asf/tick/step_improve.py` — new. `run(ctx)`.
- `asf/tick/steps.py` — `STEPS` gains `'improve'` after `'daily'`; `ASF_CALLABLES['improve'] =
  'asf.tick.step_improve:run'`; `improve_stamp_path`, `weekly_due`, `write_improve_stamp` beside
  the daily three (`asf/tick/steps.py:24-35`, `:137-160`); the module docstring's step list.
- `asf/tick/tick.py` — the two lines in `_run_steps` (`asf/tick/tick.py:292-294`, `:313-314`) and
  the `--improve` flag beside `--daily` (`asf/tick/tick.py:404`).
- `docs/products.example.yaml` — the daily clock becomes `steps: [daily, improve]`, with a comment
  saying the step's own stamp is what makes it weekly (P9).
- `tests/test_tick_steps.py` — the new cases, and the two assertions a new step moves (P11).
- `tests/test_tick.py` — the `--improve` force path.

**Steps**

1. `steps.improve_stamp_path(product)` → `os.path.join(env.state_dir(product), 'improve.stamp')`.
   `steps.weekly_due(product, force=False, today=None, every_days=7)`: true when `force`, when the
   stamp is missing or unparseable, or when `today - stamp >= every_days` days.
   `steps.write_improve_stamp(product, today=None)` writes the date, one line.
2. `steps.STEPS` gains `'improve'` after `'daily'` and `ASF_CALLABLES` gains its callable, so
   `resolve` falls a product that declares `steps:` without it back to `asf` — no product config
   changes (`asf/tick/steps.py:56-62`).
3. `tick._run_steps`: `if step == 'improve' and not steps.weekly_due(product, getattr(args,
   'improve', False)): print(f"tick: step improve not due until <date>"); continue`, and
   `if step == 'improve' and step_rc == 0: steps.write_improve_stamp(product)` — the two lines the
   daily step already has, in the same two places.
4. `asf/tick/step_improve.py`: `run(ctx)` builds the namespace `cmd_improve` wants, calls it
   against `ctx.record_root()` with the product, writes `ctx.event('improve_run', ...)` and one
   `ctx.event('improve_proposal', ...)` per proposal (§4's three event shapes), and returns its rc.
   No push and no commit of its own — the page it wrote goes to the trunk in `tick.finish`'s
   commit.
5. `docs/products.example.yaml`'s daily clock (P9). `sample/product.yaml` is not touched (P10).
6. `tests/test_tick_steps.py:120` and `:140` grow by one step, and `:637`'s neighbour assertions
   gain `daily → improve` (P11).
