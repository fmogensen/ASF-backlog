The spec's §7 and the card's last acceptance clause: a tag is cut only when `upgrade-matrix` is
green, and each release's notes list its migrations. Plus PD15's guide, which today states as the
product's contract the two behaviours this Feature just changed.

**Files**

- `asf/release.py` — `DEFAULTS['ci_steps']['upgrade_matrix']`, `KEYS`, the `upgrade_matrix`
  `Criterion` in `evaluate`
- `asf/metrics/metrics.py` — `render_notes` gains the `### Migrations` section
- `tests/test_release.py` — `UpgradeMatrixCriterionTests`, `MigrationNotesTests` (new);
  `EvaluateTest` and `SurfaceTest` follow the ninth key
- `docs/guide/upgrading.md` — *Schema migrations* and *Rolling back* rewritten (PD15)

**Steps**

1. `DEFAULTS['ci_steps']['upgrade_matrix'] = 'upgrade-matrix'` (P15) — a step-name substring, and
   `release.ci_steps` already flattens every job of a run into `[(job, step, conclusion)]`, so the
   new job is readable with no change to how runs are found (P14). `RELEASE_FIELDS` needs no edit:
   `ci_steps` is already a `_MAP` a product may override key by key.
2. `KEYS` gains `'upgrade_matrix'` after `'upgrade'` (PD11), in the position `evaluate` appends it.
3. `evaluate` gains `Criterion('upgrade_matrix', 'Upgrade from every selected release (in CI)', …)`,
   green only when `step_state(steps, cfg['ci_steps']['upgrade_matrix'])` is found and green — so any
   red leg is red, since `step_state` requires every matching step to be `success`. Its evidence uses
   the same wording as its neighbours: `'the forge did not answer'` when `steps is None`, and
   `"CI 'upgrade-matrix' green | red | absent"` otherwise.
4. `render_notes` (`asf/metrics/metrics.py:1132`) gains one section, between *Improvements and
   hotfixes* and *Upgrade*: `### Migrations`, one `- <note>` line per entry of
   `schema.changelog_notes(...)` for the versions the release introduces, and the section is omitted
   entirely when that list is empty. `sync_changelog` carries it into `CHANGELOG.md` unchanged — it
   copies the `## Notes` body as it stands — so no second writer is added.
5. `UpgradeMatrixCriterionTests`: with the step green the criterion is met and its evidence names the
   step; with one leg red it is not met; with the step absent from a run whose other steps are there
   it is not met and says `absent`; with `steps is None` it says the forge did not answer; a product
   overriding `release.ci_steps.upgrade_matrix` is matched on its own substring; and
   `list(evaluate(...)) == list(release.KEYS)` is asserted with nine keys (PD11).
6. `MigrationNotesTests`: `render_notes` over a range that introduced a migration carries the
   `### Migrations` heading and one line per `note`, in version order; over a range that introduced
   none, the heading is absent — "exactly when a migration landed in that range", which is the
   spec's own wording; and `merge_changelog` carries the section into `CHANGELOG.md` verbatim.
7. `docs/guide/upgrading.md` (PD15). *Schema migrations* (`:66-86`): the refusal is stated as what it
   now is — an additive migration is run by whoever noticed it (the tick, `asf upgrade`,
   `asf schema-migrate`), and only a non-additive one refuses with `NEEDS OPERATOR` and exit 3; the
   two version numbers and the two stamped files are named; the rollback sentence becomes D10's
   snapshot-and-`--to` rather than "revert that commit". *Rolling back* (`:88-107`): the per-key
