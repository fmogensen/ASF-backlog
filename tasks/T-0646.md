## Description
stories: S-36452
writes: asf/ci_jobs.py, asf/metrics/metrics.py, tests/test_ci_jobs.py, tests/test_metrics.py
after: Task 2

The spec's `## The design` §4, plus the `p90`/`near_limit` half of §1. After this lands, the waste
table says which cancels were timeouts and which were somebody else's verdict, and lists any job
whose green p90 has crept up on its limit — before it starts timing out.

Read **PD9 before writing `near_limit`**: `read_stream` does not sort by time, so "the job's newest
row" has to be defined, and the spec's own acceptance asks for the case. **PD14** is the golden's one
new line and the arithmetic behind it, reproduced. **PD15** is why `render_daily` ends up with one
stream read where it had one.

**Files**

- `asf/ci_jobs.py` — `NEAR_LIMIT_SHARE`, `NEAR_LIMIT_MIN_RUNS`, `NEAR_LIMIT_TOP`; `p90(xs)`;
  `near_limit(ci, share=…, min_runs=…, top=…)`.
- `asf/metrics/metrics.py` — `_median_p90` reading `ci_jobs.p90` instead of its own rank expression;
  `scorecard_rows(… , ci7=())` and its two new row blocks; `render_daily`'s bound `week_ci`, passed
  to both `scorecard_rows` and `cost_table`.
- `tests/test_ci_jobs.py` — the `p90` and `near_limit` classes beside `CancelCauseTests`.
- `tests/test_metrics.py` — a new `CancelRowTests(Base)`; `job()`'s two new keyword parameters; the
  fixture's cancelled leg gains `cause='failure'`; `EXPECTED_DAILY`'s one new line.

**Steps**

1. `p90(xs)` in `asf/ci_jobs.py`: `ys = sorted(xs)`, `return ys[-(-9 * len(ys) // 10) - 1]`, the
   spec's docstring. `_median_p90` then reads it — `f"median {round(mid)} min, p90 {ci_jobs.p90(xs)}
   min"` — and its own output does not change, which matters because the intake rows it formats are
   goldened. Measured at this branch point: the bare rank returns 7, 9, 11 and 5 for lists of 1, 10,
   12 and 3 values, the same numbers `_median_p90` prints for each.
2. `near_limit(ci, …)` → `[{'job', 'p90', 'limit', 'share', 'runs', 'timeouts'}]`, the spec's
   docstring. Per job name across every run of `ci`: the minutes of its rows whose `conclusion` is
   `'success'` (the **green** runs only — a timed-out run lasted exactly its limit, and counting it
   would make every job that ever timed out "near" its limit, C13); the count of its rows whose
   `cause == TIMEOUT`; and the `limit` from PD9's newest-carrying row. A job with no limit, or fewer
   than `min_runs` green runs, is **not listed** — it is not measured, and absence is never reported
   as safety. `share = p90 / limit`, listed when `share >= NEAR_LIMIT_SHARE`, ordered
   `(-share, job)` and cut to `top`.
3. `scorecard_rows` gains `ci7=()` as its **last** parameter, so every existing call is unchanged —
   measured: the callers are `render_daily` (`asf/metrics/metrics.py:731`), `tests/test_metrics.py`
   `:373`, `:374`, `:379`, `:384`, `:389`, `:1439` and `tests/test_tick_steps.py:151`, all positional
   up to `landing`.
4. Directly **after** the `runner-minutes` row (`:539-541`), the spec's `bycause` block: one row per
   non-zero cause in `CAUSES` order, `cancelled: <cause>` / `<minutes> min in <n> job(s) (<share>
   %)` / up to three `<job> ×<n>`. An unclassified cancel contributes to no row, so a stream imported
   before F-0131 reads exactly as it reads today. The `runner-minutes` row itself and
   `cancelled_minutes` are untouched (C10).
5. After the `red signature` rows (`:589-591`), the spec's `near_limit(ci7 or ci)` block: `near
   limit: <job>` / `p90 <p90> of <limit> min (<share> %)` / `<n> green runs[, <k> timed out]`.
6. `render_daily`: bind `week_ci = read_stream(root, 'ci', week)` once after `week = days_back(day,
   7)`, pass `ci7=week_ci` to `scorecard_rows` and `week_ci` to `cost_table` in place of its own
   `read_stream(root, 'ci', week)` (PD15).
7. `tests/test_metrics.py`: `job()` gains `limit=None, cause=None`; `fixture_streams`' cancelled leg
   becomes `job('e2e', 'cancelled', 12, 'box2', cause='failure')` — which is what the importer would
   have written for it
