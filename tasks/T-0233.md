§2.3: `discover(product=None, sources=None, checked_file=None)` resolves `sources` once at the top
and threads it down; `load(..., sources=None)` passes it through. Not one returned value changes —
that is the Task's whole claim, and `tests/test_evidence.py` green with every private-name patch
gone is its proof (PD4). Plus §3.2's source-level guard and `--record`, the one new operator-facing
output (§4).

**Files**

- `asf/evidence/evidence.py` — `sources=` on `discover`, `load`, `sh`, `resolve`, `read_blobs`,
  `read_trees`, `read_refs`, `read_ref`, `remote_branches`, `pr_list`, `main_commits`,
  `record_since`, `commit_paths`, `merge_facts`, `ancestry`, `ancestor_of`, `ci_green_runs`,
  `id_evidence`, `landed_proves`, `migrate_sources`; `_batch`, `_gh_json`, `_newest_success`'s and
  `_newest_with_job`'s vendor halves gone; `ci_provider`/`GH_ACTIONS` re-exported (PD15);
  `main()` gains `--record`
- `asf/cli.py` — `p_evidence.add_argument('--record')` and its passthrough (`asf/cli.py:252`, `:499`)
- `tests/test_evidence.py` — the six `mock.patch.object` sites replaced by an injected source
- `tests/test_evidence_sources.py` — `NoVendorOutsideTheProviderTests`

**Steps**

1. `discover`: `sources = sources or sources_mod.for_product(product)` as its first act after
   `product`, then threaded to every helper below it. The pure functions (`plan_tasks`, `parse_rows`,
   `naming_ids`, `id_state`, `task_state`, `feature_stage`, …) are untouched.
2. Each row of §2.3's table, in its order: `sh(cmd, timeout, product, sources=None)` keeps its string
   signature, splits with `shlex.split` and delegates to `sources.git.run(argv, timeout)` — it is the
   last shell-string caller and the ~20 call sites in this file are not rewritten (D3); `_batch` →
   `sources.git.cat_file`; `remote_branches` → `sources.git.fetch()` then
   `sources.git.run(['ls-remote', '--heads', 'origin'])`; `pr_list` → `sources.host.prs()`;
   `_gh_json` deleted; `_newest_success`/`_newest_with_job` keep their run-picking logic and take
   their runs from `sources.host.runs`/`run_jobs`; `ci_green_runs` → `sources.host.runs(branch=main,
   status='success', limit=20)` with the `GH_ACTIONS` guard replaced by `for_product`'s choice of
   `NoHost`; `discover`'s `prod`/`dev` pair (`:677`–`:679`) → `sources.deploy.sha('prod')` /
   `.sha('dev')`; `record_since` → `sources.git.run(args, cwd=product.backlog_dir)`; `ancestor_of`
   and `ancestry`'s direct `subprocess.run` (`:1265`, `:1284`) → `sources.git.run`.
3. `load(fresh=False, product=None, checked_file=None, sources=None)` passes it to `discover` and

   source is fed to `discover` directly, recording is not a cache mode). `discover`'s docstring gains
   one line naming the three providers.
4. `main()` gains `--record <dir>`: it writes `git.json`, `host.json`, `deploy.json` for the
   resolved product and prints the path of each; without it, `--json` prints exactly what it prints
   today. `asf/cli.py` passes the flag through. No subcommand is added (§1.3).
