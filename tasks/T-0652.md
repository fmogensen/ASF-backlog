## Description
stories: S-36401
writes: asf/tick/step_wave.py, asf/tick/step_health.py, tests/test_pause.py
after: Task 1

The spec's `## The design` §2 and §3. After this lands, a paused product's tick does the thing the
card exists for: `record`, `health`, `groom`, `prs` and `harvest` run as they always did, the lane
pass still moves finished branches to GATE and its deferred pushes still reach origin, the wave
plans and says one held line per row it will not start — and **nothing starts**, S1 rows and cloud
rows and dead-session retries included. The paused product also stops claiming slots it cannot use.
No reader says any of this yet; Task 3 does.

Read **PD6 first.** Each of the two edits to `step_wave.py` has exactly one legal insertion point
and four neighbours that decide it — one line early and the S1 bypass budget is in play, one line
late and `wave: nothing to launch` becomes the paused wave's last word. Read **PD7** for why no
cloud-lane check is needed, **PD10** for the new `handle_dead` outcome, and **PD11** for the door
that deliberately stays open.

**Files**

- `asf/tick/step_wave.py` — `from asf import pause as pause_mod` beside the module's other
  top-level imports; in `launch()`: `paused = pause_mod.held(product)` beside the host reading
  (`:696`); the `write_demand` call's third argument becomes `0 if paused else demand(…)`
  (`:686-687`); the per-row hold at `:724`; the step's `paused` event, its line and `return 0` at
  `:755`.
- `asf/tick/step_health.py` — `from asf import pause as pause_mod`; in `handle_dead`, the hold
  between the `closed_state` test (`:84-85`) and the `reaped_empty` test (`:86-90`); the docstring's
  list of returns gains `paused` (`:73`, PD10).
- `tests/test_pause.py` — `TheWaveHolds` and `TheOtherDoor` added beside Task 1's two classes, each
  with its own tmp `ASF_HOME` (PD14).

**Steps**

1. `paused = pause_mod.held(product)` immediately after `host_held, host_why, reading = host_hold(planned)`
   (`:696`), with the spec's comment — *the operator's own hold, durable*. Read **once** per wave,
   never per row.
2. The demand write (`:686-687`) becomes `capacity_mod.write_demand(product.name, len(running), 0 if paused else demand(items, product, running, inputs, extra, exclude=dropped))`.
   Note the order: this call sits **above** the `host_hold` read, so `paused` must be bound before
   it — move the `pause_mod.held` read to just above the `write_demand` call and leave the spec's
   comment where the spec puts it, or bind it once at the top of the launch decision; either way it
   is one read. `len(running)` is unchanged, which is what keeps `claim`'s `max(…, inflight now)`
   holding the seats the live sessions already occupy (C4, P8).
3. The per-row hold at **`:724`** — after the `if row.item_id in held:` block's `continue` and
   before `bypass = s1_bypass_open and …` (**PD6**), exactly the spec's four lines: the `job`,
   the `waits    {job:<24} {row.item_id:<10} — held: {pause_mod.hold_reason(paused)}` line, and
   `continue`. No S1 bypass, no room check, no share check — the pause holds every launching row.
4. The step's last word at **`:755`** — after `ctx.event('capacity', …)` closes and before
   `if host_held:` (**PD6**): the `ctx.event('paused', reason=…, by=…, at=…, rows=sum(1 for row in planned if row.launches))`,
   the `wave: {hold_reason} — no new session this tick; recording and harvesting go on, and the
   sessions in flight finish and land` line, and `return 0`. Returning 0 from `launch()` and not
   from `run()` is the whole of C3: `push_deferred` runs in `run()`'s `finally` (`:650`) either way.
5. `handle_dead`'s hold: after `if lifecycle.closed_state(items, session.get('item')): return 'closed'`
   (`:84-85`) and before the `reaped_empty` branch (`:86`), the spec's three lines — the
   `DEAD  {job:<24} launches paused: no cold retry — {item} goes back to its own row` line and
   `return 'paused'`. No `lifecycle.hold`, no `ctx.counts['relaunches']` move, no ledger write
