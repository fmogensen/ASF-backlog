The spec's design §4 (D-0007, D-0010, D-0011; P5, P6). One number, one exclusion, one claim swap, one field
and one reader. This is the half of the fix that stops the **park** — the recovery (Tasks 3 and 5)
stops the drop, and this stops the row from being parked on a `status: done` report when what
failed was the filing.

**Read this Task from `docs/plans/f-0313.md`, not from this card** — the record cuts a Task card's
description at 4000 characters. Three decisions bind it and reach no card: **PD1** (this Task is in
wave 1, ahead of health's §3, because the card's fourth acceptance needs the exemption landed
before the harvest test can assert an unparked row — until §3 lands, nothing writes the mark and
`counted` returns today's answer, which is correct and harmless), **PD8** (`RUN_FIELDS`
membership is what makes Task 5's `review_unfiled=None` clear the mark rather than fold it
forward) and **PD12** (the trunk now carries a second, looser bound on this same loop —
`lifecycle.review_attempts` and the feeder's `attempt_limit` HOLD at 3; `UNFILED_FREE = 1` parks at
2, so this one fires first, and neither function may be folded into the other).

  added beside `review_answered` (`:3233`) and `corrected_on` (`:3259`) — the two precedents D-0010
