The spec's §*`asf/doctor.py`*, §*`asf/conventions.py`* and §*The prose*. After it lands,
`asf doctor` carries one required `ci facts` row per product with CI to collect, and a record whose
CI log stopped eight days ago is RED on every one of those eight days — which is the whole of what
"nobody was told" cost this card.

**After Task 1**, and not for footprint reasons: `check_ci_facts` calls `metrics.ci_facts` and
`metrics.ci_age`, neither of which exists until Task 1 has landed. Do not stub them forward.

**Read PD6 before writing a line.** The spec's P15 names a `_get` in `asf/conventions.py` that is
not there — the new int key is read raw off the dataclass field, and `conventions:
{ci_stale_factor: three}` would raise a `TypeError` inside `doctor.run()`, which wraps nothing, and
take **every** row of `asf doctor` with it. Then **PD8** (render through `metrics.ci_age`;
`format_age` is not touched) and **PD9** (a cadence of 0.0 is reachable, and the floor is what
makes it safe).
