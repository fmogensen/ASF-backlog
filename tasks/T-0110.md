The spec's `asf/workers/report.py — rewritten` section, whole. The module stops being a regex and
becomes the owner of the report: the common fields, the twelve field tables, the checker, the
reader, the renderer and the contract text. `parse`, `unpushed` and `NO_RE` are deleted, not
deprecated (D11), so the three readers outside the module that stood on them are retyped in the
same diff (PD4, PD5, PD12). Nothing yet *demands* a report: `check` exists and nobody calls it.

**Files**

- `asf/workers/report.py` — **rewritten**. `ReportError`; `REQ`, `STATUSES`, `PUSHED`,
  `FENCE_TOKEN`, `UNPUSHED` (unchanged value, PD13), `BAD_REPORT`; `COMMON` and `KIND_FIELDS` as
  the spec tables them, with `adjudicate` carrying `blocked_on`, `writes`, `superseded_by` besides
  `ruling`, `upheld`, `overruled` (PD5); `_CHECK`/`_check`; `schema`, `fence`, `typed`, `check`,
  `failure`, `ruling`, `ruling_fields`, `summary`, `render`, `contract`. Imports `json` and `re`
  and nothing of ASF's — `build.KINDS` is not imported, the table is the spec's twelve words
  written out, and `tests.test_report` is what holds the two equal.
- `asf/briefs/facts.py` — `last_report` reads `report.typed(text, None)` inside a
  `report.ReportError` guard that returns the ledger line alone; `REPORT_FIELDS` becomes
  `('status', 'pushed', 'tests', 'left_out', 'ruling')`; a list-valued field is capped through
  `_cap` on its joined items (PD4). The docstring keeps saying what it says — never the
  transcript above the report.
- `tests/test_report.py` — **rewritten**: `SchemaTest`, `ParseTest`, `RenderTest`, `ContractTest`,
  `ReadersTest`, exactly the spec's cases, plus one case for `ruling_fields` over a typed
  adjudicate report and one asserting an absent field is `None` (PD5, the F-0090 assertions at
  `:105-122` restated against the object).
- `tests/test_brief_facts.py` — the four `last_report` cases (`:189-210`) script typed reports
  through `report.render`; `:245`'s `repo_facts` shape is unchanged.
- `tests/test_workers.py:869` — the held self-reported-unpushed fixture becomes
  `report.render('coder', status='partial', pushed='no', why='the suite is still running in the background')`
  appended to the same prose. The assertions are untouched (PD12).
- `tests/test_lifecycle.py:204` — the groom fixture becomes `report.render('groom', pushed='no',
  why='a ruling is not a commit')`; still `FINISHED`.
- `tests/test_tick_steps.py:255` — the adjudicate log's result becomes
  `report.render('adjudicate', ruling='no open finding; the fix stands')`; `file_rulings` files
  the same line.

**Steps**

1. `COMMON`, the enumerations and `_CHECK`/`_check`, mirroring `asf.metrics.metrics`'s
   `_CHECK`/`_apply` idiom (D2) without importing it: a tuple of `_CHECK` names is a type list, a
   tuple of plain strings is an enumeration of values.
2. `KIND_FIELDS`: one entry per kind of the spec's table, each field
   `(types, default, instruction)` and the instruction being the `Final message:` line it
   replaces. `adjudicate` gains PD5's three, whose instructions are the prose TAIL's own words
   (`the id this item must wait for, or none`, and so on).
3. `schema(kind)` — `COMMON` merged with the kind's, `ReportError` on an unknown kind.
4. `fence(text)` — the last fenced block whose info line carries `asf-report`; a fence is three
   backticks or more and closes on the first line of at least that run.
5. `typed(text, kind=None)` — parse, then check: unknown keys (`kind` given only), required keys,
   types, enumerations, then D5's three cross-field rules. One sentence per failure, each naming
   the key, in the spec's words.
