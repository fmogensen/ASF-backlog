The spec's §4 — D-0004, D-0005, O1, O7. The lane reads an `index.json` snapshot, so even with Task 1
landed there is a window in which the record still says `Resolved` and the lane closes the PR:
that window is what shut #1244, a PR green and open for hours. One pure helper and one
`merge-base` per done-card branch closes it without depending on step order inside the tick.

Independent of Task 1 in every direction (**PD9**): no `Ev`, no rule, no evidence key. Two
decisions bind it: **PD5** (where `follow_up` is computed, and why `orphan_facts` computes its
own) and **PD6** (the state gate that keeps the fork off every other branch).

**Files**

- `asf/harvest/lane.py` — `builds_on_landing(card, head, is_ancestor)` added beside
  `superseded_by` (`:1253`), with the spec's docstring byte for byte; `superseded_by` gains a third
  parameter `follow_up=False`, **never reordered ahead of `item`**; `Lane.branch_facts` (`:1770`)
  computes `card`/`follow_up` after `:1813` and uses it at the adoption guard (`:1820`) and at
  `:1860`; `Lane.orphan_facts` (`:2045`) computes its own and uses it at both clauses
  (`:2075-2076`). `Lane.transplant_case` (`:3089`) is **not** touched (O7): it is a rescue path
  whose refusal costs a follow-up nothing — the branch keeps its PR by this Task and the card is
  `Active` by the next ingest.
- `tests/test_lane.py` — new `FollowUpFixIsNotSuperseded(LaneRepo)` (`LaneRepo` at `:597`).
  `Orphans` (`:1196`) is untouched: its done-card case (`:1214`) uses a `T-0007` that carries no
  landing stamp, so `builds_on_landing` answers False and the close stands.
- `tests/test_harvest.py` — `:916` is **not edited**. It calls `lane.superseded_by(items,
  'B-0001')` positionally with two arguments and expects `'removed'`, and it is run as the
  regression that proves the new keyword changed no old answer. The path is claimed because that
  is the file the proof lives in and a coder who makes `follow_up` required or moves it ahead of
  `item` will be tempted to "fix" the call site instead of the signature.

**Steps**

1. Write `builds_on_landing(card, head, is_ancestor)` exactly as §4 gives it, docstring included:

   stands — no landing sha to compare, or a head the checkout cannot resolve, where
   `git merge-base --is-ancestor` exits non-zero and `Lane.is_ancestor` (`:1684-1686`) answers

   no `self.items` — `is_ancestor` is passed in, which is what makes
