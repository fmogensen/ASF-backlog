**Files**

asf/logs.py, asf/scorecard/program.py, asf/release.py, docs/guide/troubleshooting.md, tests/test_logs_tree.py

**Steps**

1. `asf/logs.py` gains the two readers of §3. `generations(path)`: the existing files holding that
   path's text, **oldest first** — `[path + '.1', path]`, each only if it exists, `[]` when
   neither does. `lines(path)`: every line of every generation, oldest first, **streamed** —
   iterating the open file, never `f.read()` — with undecodable bytes replaced and an unreadable
   file skipped.
2. `asf/scorecard/program.py`: `_tick_logs` (`:314-322`) becomes `_tick_lines(product)`, yielding
   lines across `logs.generations` of each `tick-<product>-*.log` instead of returning texts, and
   `offline_lines` (`:153-165`) takes lines rather than texts — its only caller (`:358`) splits
   them again immediately today. Its stamp rule is unchanged: an offline line is dated by the
   newest stamp seen before it, which is why the carry set keeps the stamp with the line.
3. `asf/release.py`: `read_tick_logs(log_dir)` (`:283-291`) keeps its signature and its
   `[(name, events)]` shape and builds each entry from `logs.lines` over that transcript's
   generations — so `tick-p.log` and `tick-p.log.1` are **one** entry, parsed oldest-first, and no
   glob misses a rotated generation. `parse_tick_log` (`:265`) grows a line-wise twin and keeps
   the text-wise one `tests/test_release.py:143` uses.
4. `docs/guide/troubleshooting.md:4`: the glob the guide opens with becomes
   `tick-<product>-*.log*`, so the first rotation does not take the older half of the answer away
   from the one command it leads with. `docs/guide/operating.md` and
   `docs/guide/product-config.md` name a single transcript and need no change; the five globs in
   `docs/specs/f-0290.md` are another card's acceptance record and are not touched.
5. `tests/test_logs_tree.py` gains `GenerationsTests` — one case per acceptance line of
   `S-134157`: the generations of a rotated transcript, of one that never rotated and of a path
   with no files; lines yielded rotated-file-first and never a whole generation in memory; a
   generation of undecodable bytes read with replacement; the scorecard's reader and its offline
   count over a rotated transcript, with a carried offline line still dated by its own stamp; the
   release reader's entries, their order, an upgrade chain split across a rotation read back whole
   with no invented break, an older chain under-reported and still inventing none, and its return
   shape unchanged for a transcript that never rotated; and the guide's grep matching a rotated
   generation.
