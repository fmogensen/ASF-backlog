The spec's **In** 2 and its design §2: one branch in `branch_rows`, ahead of the
`_pr_closed` → `_pr_conflicting` → `idle_branch` chain, for a Task only, so an Active Task whose
`reshape:` is pending gets the RESHAPE → PLAN row and holds its PLAN → CODE row — the pair
`task_rows` already builds at `state: New`. This is `T-0537`: three days Active with a groom reshape
on its card while the feeder launched coders at the unchanged Task, because the reshape branch
sits inside a loop filtered to `state == 'New'` (`asf/feeder/rows.py:1870-1872`, unmoved at
`55d9ec2`, `PD25`/`PD26`; measured again in `PD12`: New yields two rows, one of them the RESHAPE
row; Active yields one launching row and no reshape row).

**Read this Task from `docs/plans/f-0330.md`, not from this card** (`PD18`). Three decision rows
bind it and are on no card: `PD15` (the held row's `branch` is `_branch_of`, not
`branch_for(product, 'code', …)`, and that is right rather than a drift — do not "fix" it), `PD8`
and `PD25`/`PD26` (**every number below is measured at `55d9ec2`, the `origin/main` this Task will
be cut from, and not at this branch's base** — the file moved under ASF 0.2's kernel and is
byte-identical between `c34fd09` and `55d9ec2`, so `PD25`'s reading of it is still the head's:
`branch_rows` is **`:1231`** and not `:1189` or `:1218`, its loop filter `:1241`, the
`_pr_closed` arm `:1246` and `idle_branch`'s three guards **`:1254-1255`**;
`correction_rows` `:1018` with `ids.add(iid)` at `:1038` and its `parked` branch at `:1045`;
`task_rows` `:1865` with its `on_trunk` skip `:1878` and its reshape branch
`:1907-1916`. The previous rounds' claim that the spec was wrong to cite `:1212-1213` stays
**withdrawn**: that was their correct place at the base the spec measured) and `PD13` (this Task's
`--touched` set is 55 modules, the heaviest of the four).

**Files**

- `asf/feeder/rows.py` — the one branch the spec fences in §2, inserted in `branch_rows`
  (`:1231`) between its loop filter (`:1241`) and the `if _pr_closed(v):` arm (`:1246`), ending in
  `continue`. `replan_mod` is already imported (`:91`) and `RESHAPE` is already defined (`:160`),
  so the branch needs no new import and no new constant. Its predicate is
  `replan_mod.task_pending(v)` and **not** a bare `v.get('reshape')`: the former goes quiet once
  the reshape is applied, the card is removed, or the Task is done (`asf/record/replan.py:115-125`,
  its `removed` guard at `:122`), and the latter would raise a row in all three cases.
- `tests/test_feeder.py` — `ReshapeRowsTest` (`:915`, the same line at all three heads, `PD26`)
  extended. Its `idx`/`task` helpers and `rows.candidates(idx, product(), [])` are the whole
  fixture; the side-by-side parity case builds
  the same card twice, once `state: New` and once `state: Active`, and compares the four
  attributes `PD15` names.

**Steps**

1. Add the branch exactly where §2 puts it, with all three of its in-branch guards —
   `v['id'] not in held`, `v['id'] not in landed`, `not delivered(items, v, landed_shas)`. They
   are not optional and they are not inherited: because the branch `continue`s it never reaches
   the `idle_branch` arm, which is the only place `branch_rows` carries `held`, `landed` and
   `delivered` (`:1254-1255`), and `branch_rows`' own loop filter carries only `type`, `state` and
   `busy` (`:1241`). `landed` is already in hand one line above the loop (`:1239`).
2. `blocked` is **deliberately not** checked, matching every other arm of `branch_rows`: an Active
   Task marked `blocked:` that carries a pending reshape is exactly the case the reshape session
   answers.
3. Assert the ordering both ways round, because the `continue` pre-empts two arms and not one: a
   conflicting PR plus a pending reshape yields the reshape pair and **no** CONFLICT → REBASE row,
   and a PR closed unmerged plus a pending reshape yields the reshape pair and **no** STA
