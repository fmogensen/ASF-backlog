The order pass has written `after: <owner>` on a standing overlap since it was built, and nothing
has ever read that edge back. The footprint moves under it — a card edit, a widening, a reshape, a
revert — and the hold outlives its reason, holding the card and everything bundled with it behind
an item it no longer conflicts with. This Task writes the inverse of the write: the same test, run
again, on the footprints as they are now.
