The spec's `## The design` §3, minus the reset credit, and nothing else. After this lands, an
account at 92% of its weekly window is skipped *before* a fifty-minute run is spent on it rather
than after, and an account whose 5h reading is unreadable while its weekly one is not is judged on
the window that can be read instead of waved through. The 5h line the operator has read for weeks
does not move by one byte.

**Read PD3 before writing the message, and PD4 before touching `over`.** They are the two findings
in this plan that turn a correct-looking §3 into a red suite. PD3: the number printed as
`committed` is the taken sum **plus** the running allowance, rounded once, or `:145` reddens with
`+0% committed`. PD4: the overshoot D5 wants cannot be computed from the public triple, so the
public triple stays and a private method carries the fourth value.

**Read PD7 before you conclude the suite is broken.** Five `tests/test_workers.py` fixtures read
`seven_d_pct` with no `five_h_pct` and are judged for the first time in this Task. They are green
by about three points, and only because of PD6's rounding. If `:353`, `:366` or `:372` goes red the
long-window share was mis-derived — `tests/test_workers.py` is not in this Task's `writes:` and is
not the thing to change.

Three things are load-bearing. **`five_h` is judged first and names itself with no label**, because
`quota.WINDOW_KEYS` is ordered `five_h`, `seven_d`, `seven_d_model` and `label` is `''` only for
`five_h` (D4) — so the first window that fails in that order is the one named, and for a 5h miss
the string is character-for-character today's. **The early return loses exactly one clause**: a
stale reading still short-circuits to `(True, '', 0.0)` (D10, and `quota.stale_since` is still the
only thing asked), while an unreadable `five_h_pct` no longer short-circuits anything. And **`total
< S` stays strict**: an account landing exactly on its stop does not fit, today and after.
