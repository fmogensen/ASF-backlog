## Description
stories: S-17351
writes: asf/conventions.py, tests/test_conventions.py
after: none

§2.2: `outcome_share_pct`, `outcome_min_sessions`, `repeat_failure_n` and `idle_wave_ticks` as
four typed fields with a module-level `DEFAULT_*` each, so a product may differ on them and no
module under `asf/` may spell the number (`tools/check_conventions.sh`). Nothing reads them yet —
Tasks 4 and 5 do, through `file_bugs._conventions`, which already resolves and already falls back
to `DEFAULTS`.

**Files**

- `asf/conventions.py` — `DEFAULT_OUTCOME_SHARE_PCT`, `DEFAULT_OUTCOME_MIN_SESSIONS`,
  `DEFAULT_REPEAT_FAILURE_N`, `DEFAULT_IDLE_WAVE_TICKS`; the four fields on `Conventions`; the
  four lines in the module docstring's `conventions:` example
- `tests/test_conventions.py` — `SelfBugThresholdTests`

**Steps**

1. Four `DEFAULT_*` constants (10, 20, 2, 6), each with the doc comment §2.2 shows — what the
   threshold means, not that it is a threshold.
2. Four fields on `Conventions`, `int`, beside `prs_per_tick` / `area_depth` / `batch_max_globs`
   (`asf/conventions.py:148`), each defaulting to its constant.
3. The module docstring's `conventions:` example gains §2.2's four lines verbatim, comments
   included — the docstring is where an operator reads what a product may set.
4. Nothing else: `from_mapping` and `get` already cover any typed field by name
   (`asf/conventions.py:178`, `:283`), and a typed field is by construction not a survivor in
   `extra`.
5. `SelfBugThresholdTests` per T2: `Conventions()` gives 10, 20, 2, 6;
   `from_mapping({'outcome_share_pct': 25, 'idle_wave_ticks': 3})` overrides those two, leaves
   `outcome_min_sessions` at 20 and `repeat_failure_n` at 2, and leaves `extra` empty;
   `conv.get('repeat_failure_n')` equals `conv.repeat_failure_n`.
6. `ModuleConstantsTests.test_the_defaults_are_exported_one_per_field`
   (`tests/test_conventions.py:256`) gains the four `DEFAULT_* == Conventions().field` pairs, as
   it carries them for every other field of its kind.
