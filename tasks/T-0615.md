The spec's `## The design` §1 and §4. `lifecycle` gains the number, the predicate and the index;
`health` keeps the name as an alias and asks the predicate instead of computing the same
subtraction itself. **Nothing the factory does changes when this lands** — the same runs are
stopped, at the same minute, under the same three proofs — which is why T-0196's twelve cases,
unchanged, are this Task's proof rather than its casualty.

Read **PD3** (the signature stays; the alias is what makes that safe, and never `grace_s or …`),
**PD4** (one more line comes out than the spec shows), **PD5** (the new parser accepts more than
the old one — named, not guarded), **PD6** (`.get('session')`, or the pool raises every step) and
**PD8** (F-0160 writes these same four lines) before starting.
