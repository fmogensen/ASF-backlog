after: none

The spec's first three **In** bullets and `## The design` §`asf/scorecard/score.py`, plus the
four-line `_delta_cell` delegation from §`asf/views/scorecard.py` (D9 — it is part of this Story,
whose acceptance names `tests.test_direct_lane`). Everything here is pure: no file, no clock, no
product. Nothing calls `total_row`, `delta_row` or `total_line` yet — Task 2 and Task 3 do — so on
this Task alone the only observable change in the whole tree is that `--by-lane`'s delta cells are
rendered by a function one module down, byte for byte identically. That is the point: the pure half
lands provable first.

**Files**

- `asf/scorecard/score.py` — `TOTAL_SUMS`, `TOTAL_MEDIANS`, `CLUTTER_SUMS`, `DELTA_KEYS`;
  `total_row`, `_clutter_total`, `delta_row`, `delta`, `total_line`
- `asf/views/scorecard.py` — `_delta_cell` (`:165-176`) rewritten over `score.delta`
- `tests/test_scorecard.py` — `TotalRowTests`, `TotalLineTests`

**Steps**

1. `asf/scorecard/score.py`, at the bottom beside `headline_line` and `lanes_line` (P17, verified at
   `:392-428`): the four module constants with the spec's `#:` comment on each, byte for byte —
   `TOTAL_SUMS` (thirteen keys), `TOTAL_MEDIANS` (three), `CLUTTER_SUMS`
   (`open_prs`, `stale_prs`, `branches`), `DELTA_KEYS` (six, in print order). They are constants
   because the total, the view's table and the line all read the same sets.
2. `total_row(rows)` with the spec's docstring and body byte for byte. Do not add
   `repair_sessions` to the int-coercion tuple — PD10 is why. The three ratios are recomputed over
   the sums and are `None` when `landed` is `0`, which is the "a week where nothing landed" case of
   the S-34950 fence and the reason there is no division by zero.
3. `_clutter_total(rows)` to PD8's contract, which is the spec's one sentence made exact: each of
   `CLUTTER_SUMS` summed over the rows whose `clutter` block carries it as a number, and `None` for
   a key no row could read. Reuse `_num`'s own test (`:65-66`) for "is a number" rather than writing
   a second one — `isinstance(v, (int, float))` and not `bool`. A row whose `clutter` is absent,
   `None` or not a dict contributes to no key and raises nothing.
4. `delta_row(now, prev, keys=DELTA_KEYS)` with the spec's docstring byte for byte: `now[k] - prev[k]`
   for every key **both** rows carry as a number; a key missing or `None` on either side is absent
   from the result, never `0`. `now` or `prev` being `None` or empty yields `{}`.
5. `delta(v, money=False, unit='')` with the spec's docstring and body byte for byte. Its output must
   equal today's `_delta_cell` for all three keyings, which is what step 6 and the Gate's first line
   prove: `money=True, unit=''` is the `cost` form, `money=False, unit='d'` the `lead_days` form,
   and `money=False, unit=''` every other. `format(abs(v), 'g')` and `f'{abs(v):g}'` are the same
   call — do not change which one is written, the spec's body uses `format`.
6. `asf/views/scorecard.py`: replace `_delta_cell`'s five-line body (`:166-176`) with the spec's one
   line, `return score.delta(v, money=(key == 'cost'), unit='d' if key == 'lead_days' else '')`.
   Keep the function's name, its signature and its position; `score` is already imported at `:13`.
   `PAIR_METRICS` keying stays in the view (D9). Nothing else in that file moves in this Task.
7. `total_line(totals)` with the spec's docstring byte for byte, rendering the spec's three worked
   examples exactly:
   - measured, with deltas —
     `scorecard week 2026-09-21 (day 3/7, 3 products): 7 on prod (+2) · 11 landed (+3) · $38.40/feature all-in (-$6.10) · $21.00/feature own (-$1.40) · 2.1 repair sessions/feature (-0.4) · $422.40 all-in (+$40.00)`
   - measured, nothing to compare against — the same shape with every delta `—`;
   - nothing measured — `scorecard: no weekly snapshot yet — the daily step writes the first one`.
