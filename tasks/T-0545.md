The spec's `## The design` §2: a third pass in `widen_footprint`, run by the health step after
`revert_overlaps`, giving the later-minted card of every unordered Active overlap `after: <the
earlier>`, one History line and one commit (C4, C5, C6). This is the Task that takes
`asf check --invariants --product asf` to `0 violation(s)`.

**Read PD3 before writing the loop and PD4 before choosing where `tasks` comes from.** They are
one defect each in the spec's own design, both measured, and a coder who follows §2 literally
writes 27 commits where 24 are due (PD3) — including one for a pair the audit never reported
(PD4) — and ships a cycle-freedom argument that does not cover the graph the pass actually builds.
Then **PD2** (no count goes in a docstring; the number moves) and **PD10** (`rows()` answers for
`T-0001`, and the held card is `T-0002`).

On landing, against today's record: **24** edges onto **11** cards, 24 record commits, 0 unordered
pairs left, no cycle — measured, and a snapshot, which is why no test asserts a number against the
live record. `revert_overlaps` runs first and is untouched: a widening reverted in the same tick
has already removed its path, so that pair never reaches this pass and the narrower repair keeps
precedence.

  moves; `WIDENED_RE`, `widened_paths` and `overlapping_widenings` are `revert_overlaps`' and are
  not read by this pass.
- `asf/tick/widen_footprint.py` — `serialize_overlaps(ctx, items, out=print)` with the spec's
  docstring, placed after `revert_overlaps`; `run()`'s last line becomes
  `serialize_overlaps(ctx, items, out=out)`. `write_card`, `_card`, `_reindex`, `report_facts`,
  `apply` and `revert_overlaps` are untouched — this pass is a new caller of the first three, not
  an edit to them.
- `tests/test_widen.py` — a new `class SerializeOverlapTests(WidenStepBase)` with
  `active = WidenStepTests.active` and `check = WidenStepTests.check` assigned as unbound methods
  (P14), **never** a subclass of `WidenStepTests`, and its **own** `rows(self, iid)` (PD10).
  `WidenStepTests` itself is not edited.

1. `SERIALIZED` in `widen.py`. One line, and the only edit to that module.
2. `serialize_overlaps` builds its `tasks` map from `canonicalize(load_items(root))` — the same
   source `check_i3` reads — **not** from `items` (PD4). Normalise to the
   `{'type', 'state', 'writes', 'after', 'removed'}` shape `unordered_overlaps` takes, through
   `frontmatter.split_machine` for the state.
3. One mutable `edges` map, seeded from `invariants._after_edges(tasks)` and **updated after every
