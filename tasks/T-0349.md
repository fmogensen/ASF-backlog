3. `classify(session, now, silent_min, alive, verdict=None)` — the four rungs it has, in the order
   it has them, then `return 'STUCK' if verdict is not None and verdict.cls == progress.STUCK

   its samples say, with no extra test in `classify` itself (P3).
4. `stall(product, now=None, alive=None, session_source=None, out=print, sample=True)`. Before the
   loop: `limit = progress.progress_min(product)`. Inside it, per live session, when `limit > 0`

   died costs resolution, never sight), `progress.read` the store, `progress.judge(samples, now,

   `s.get('progress')`, `pool_mod.update_session(product, s['job'], progress=verdict.cls)`; when it
   is the same, nothing is appended.
5. The row tuple. `STALL`/`DEAD` keep the silence minutes they compute today. `STUCK` carries

   or ''))`; a question that is not None is an `INPUT` row. The acks apply to it exactly as they do
   to the other three — the grammar matches on the state word and needs no change (P13).

   `stall: none` when there are no rows at all.
