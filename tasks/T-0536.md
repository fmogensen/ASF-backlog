The spec's seventh **In** bullet. Two sentences and one assertion. It waits on Task 2 because it
describes a refusal that does not exist until Task 2 lands, and because it writes `asf/upgrade.py`.

The guide's own example — `asf upgrade --to 7a5881d    # or any commit sha` (`docs/guide/upgrading.md:30`)
— needs no edit: Task 2 is what makes it true. This Task adds what the guide has never said, which
is what happens when the sha is wrong.

**Files**

- `asf/upgrade.py` — the module docstring's marker paragraph (`:19-24`)
- `docs/guide/upgrading.md` — the paragraph under the upgrade fence (`:33-38`)
- `tests/test_readme.py` — `InstallSectionTests` (`:421`), beside `test_upgrading_names_to` (`:468`)

**Steps**

1. `asf/upgrade.py`'s marker paragraph (`:19-24`) gains one sentence, after *"parks nobody
   (:func:`refuses`)"* and before the B-0141 clause: a ref the install source does not have is
   refused before the marker is reached at all — `resolve_target` asks the source whether the commit
   exists and `install` returns 2 on its `False`, so a mistyped sha parks nobody and installs
   nothing; an answer that cannot be had is not a refusal, and the upgrade proceeds as it does
   today. Name F-0162 in it. Do not touch the B-0141 sentence, the TTL paragraph or the drain
   paragraph.
2. `docs/guide/upgrading.md`, in the paragraph that follows the upgrade fence (`:33-38`, after *"so
   `asf --version` reports the release"*): one sentence saying a sha or tag the repo does not have is
   **refused** before anything happens — nothing is left pending and no tick is parked — and that a
   short sha is expanded by the repo, so the `7a5881d` form above works. Keep the existing sentences
   as they are.
3. `InstallSectionTests` gains one test beside `test_upgrading_names_to` (`:468`), shaped exactly
   like it — `text = self._read('docs/guide/upgrading.md')` and `assertIn` over the needles, no
   fixture and no command. Assert the guide names the refusal and names that nothing is parked;
   choose the needles from the sentence written in step 2 and keep them short enough to survive a
   reword (the class's own idiom at `:471-479` is a tuple of needles with the needle as the assert
   message). This is the class the spec's fence names, and it is the right one: it already reads
   `docs/guide/upgrading.md` at `:468-470`.
