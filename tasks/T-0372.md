## Description
stories: S-33103, S-33108

§2.11 and §2.6. The lint is what stops a probe job that can reach the runner's other secrets from
ever being dispatched; the origin judgement is ASF's own check over what the engine says it did;
the consent stamp is the operator gate the card names, recorded rather than assumed. The ledger
lands here because the stamp lives in it (PD8).

**Files**

- `asf/probe/fence.py` — `lint(probe, workflow_text, pool)`, `judge(results, probe)`,
  `consents_granted(product)`, `grant(product, now)`, `revoke(product)`
- `asf/probe/ledger.py` — `path(product)`, `load(product)`, `save(product, data)`, `entry_key`,
  `mark`, `abandon`, `open_runs`
- `asf/roles/roles.py` — `UNBOUND['asf-prober']`'s reason (l. 60)
- `tests/test_probe_fence.py` — T8's unit half
- `tests/test_launch.py`, `tests/test_roles.py` — the invariants asserted unchanged

**Steps**

1. `ledger.py`: `state/<product>/probe.json` written through a temp file and one `os.replace`, as
   `deploy._load_records` / `deploy.record` do (`asf/harvest/deploy.py:345-375`). The key is
   `<target>@<sha7>`; `state` is `dispatched` | `read` | `abandoned`; the file holds `consents`
   and `runs` and nothing else. No verdict detail and no screenshot — those are the record's.
2. `lint(probe, workflow_text, pool)` returns one problem per breach of §2.11: `secrets: inherit`;
   a secret that is neither `probe.secrets.identity` nor `probe.secrets.mailbox`; a `permissions:`
   granting anything beyond `contents: read`, **or absent at all** — the repository default is not
   read-only; a `runs-on` no runner in `ci.pool` carrying `probe.role` satisfies
   (`ci_pool.parse_runs_on`, `ci_pool._satisfies`); no upload of `probe.artifact`.
3. `judge(results, probe)` (PD10), pure: a `Result` whose `origins` holds a scheme+host outside
   `probe.origins` comes back with `origins_ok` false and its verdict forced to `fail` at step
   `left the product's host (<origin>)`, **whatever the engine reported**. Nothing else changes.
4. `consents_granted` / `grant` / `revoke` read and write `consents.granted_at` in the ledger.
   `grant` records the stamp and who by; `revoke` clears it.
5. `roles.UNBOUND['asf-prober']`'s reason becomes *"the production probe is a workflow run, not a
   session (F-0051) — the launch row's boundaries are the contract `asf probe lint` holds its job
   to"*. That one line of text is the whole change: no binding, no new `TABLE` row, no change to
   `asf-prober.md`, `BINDINGS` untouched (D12, §1.2).
6. `tests/test_probe_fence.py`: `lint`'s six problem cases and the clean job of the guide;
   `judge` forcing an engine-reported `pass` to `fail` on an off-origin and leaving an in-origin
   `pass` alone; `grant` then `consents_granted` then `revoke` round-tripping through a ledger
   fixture; the ledger written through a temp file and one `replace`, asserted on a fake.
7. `tests/test_roles.py` and `tests/test_launch.py`: `roles.validate` over every role file still
   returns no problem, `UNBOUND` still holds `asf-prober` with a reason, and
   `set(BINDINGS) == set(briefs.build.KINDS)` still holds (`tests/test_roles.py:361`, untouched).
