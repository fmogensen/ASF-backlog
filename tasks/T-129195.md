The spec's **In** 3 and its design §3: `report_facts` loses its `FINISHED` gate, **narrows** its
`pending_correction` gate to a *parked* correction, and carries the surviving correction's text
into the footprint hold that replaces it. This is `T-0436`: the same two paths named under
`needs writes:` in three reports, fourteen sessions, and not one `footprint widened:` line on the
card — because all three runs ended in a failure class and `report_facts` reads a claim only off a
clean `finished` run with no correction pending (`:145` and `:147`).

**Read this Task from `docs/plans/f-0330.md`, not from this card** (`PD18`). Two decision rows
bind it and are on no card: `PD14` (why `tests/test_widen.py` **is** this Task's footprint where
it was not Task 1's) and `PD8` (every anchor below re-measured at this base; `report_facts` is
`:134` with its gates at `:145` and `:147`, `refusal_facts`' carry — the landed precedent this
section adopts — is `:292-293`; all four of those, and `correction_text`'s
`failing tests:` line at `:352`, are unchanged from the base the spec measured. One trap `PD8` names and this Task must not fall into:
`diff_facts` (`:174`) carries the **same two gate lines** at `:189` and `:191`. They are not this
Task's — §3 changes `report_facts` only, and a grep for the gate text alone will find both).

**Files**

- `asf/tick/widen_footprint.py` — `report_facts` only (`:134-171`). The two gate lines become
  `if not run.get('ended') or lifecycle.landed(run): continue` and
  `corr = lifecycle.pending_correction(run, path)` / `if corr and corr.get('parked'): continue`,
  and the `footprint_hold` call gains the carry. `'ended'` is a real run field, a timestamp
  (`ENDED` at `asf/workers/lifecycle.py:90`, in `RUN_FIELDS` at `:114` — the same two lines at both

  and `diff_facts`' identical pair of gates at `:189`/`:191` is nobody's.
- `tests/test_widen.py` — `WidenStepTests` and `RefusalWidenTests` extended in place.

**Steps**
