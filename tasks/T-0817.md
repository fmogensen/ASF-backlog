**Steps**

1. `origin_top(root, prefix, remote='origin')`, the spec's docstring, PD5's mechanism, PD6's
   return. Through `asf.gitops.git` only — no `subprocess`, no `['git', …]` argv, because this
   file's client ceiling is 0. One `fetch` of the remote's trunk, then one `ls-tree -r
   --name-only <remote>/<trunk> -- <folder>` per folder the prefix owns (the same
   `set(TYPES.values())` walk `record_top` does, `:35-37`), the highest `<prefix>-(\d+)\.md` of
   them. `reachable` is the fetch's `ok`. **Any** failure — no origin, no trunk, a timeout, an
   unreachable remote — is `(0, False)`: a floor that cannot be read is no floor, never a
   refusal, which is the docstring's own sentence.
2. The gate becomes `if idclaim.has_origin(root) and _claims_enabled(root):` (`:94`, D6). `claim`
   and `worktree` both stay parameters: `claim` because `asf new` passes it (`asf/record/new.py:154`)
   and PD6's part (b) exempts it, `worktree` because the `BACKLOG_ALLOW_MINT` refusal below
   (`:100-102`) is unchanged for a record with no origin.
3. Inside the gate, PD6's three parts: read `top, reachable = origin_top(root, prefix)` **once**;
   when not `reachable` and not `claim`, fall through to the local path with one line on stdout
   naming it; when not `reachable` and `claim`, keep today's behaviour and let the refusal stand;
   otherwise `idclaim.claim_one(root, prefix, who, floor=max(max_n, top))`, whose `ClaimError`
   stays the landed `SystemExit` (`:98-99`). Note for the Task: `claim` already does its own
   `fetch` of `refs/asf/ids/*` and takes `lo = max(start, floor + 1, top(cl, prefix) + 1)`
   (`asf/record/idclaim.py:157-161`), so three successive mints over a floor of 270 answer
   `B-0271`, `B-0272`, `B-0273` without the caller tracking anything — which is exactly what A4's
   first case asserts.
4. The local fallback (`:103-109`): `n = max(max_n, origin_top(root, prefix)[0]) + 1`, and the
   `idclaim.claims(root)` mirror at `:104` read **after** that fetch, so the "step over a block a
   job holds" guard stops being as stale as the checkout (P11). Reuse the `top` from step 3 when
