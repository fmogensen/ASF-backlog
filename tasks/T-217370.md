1. `asf/record/archive.py`: `with_archive(items, root)` with §2.4's docstring verbatim, and the one
   shape that makes its three promises true at once. `items` is an
   `index_reader.Items`, already folded, so its retired cards are in `retired_done`/`retired_open`
   and not in the mapping: the merge is `raw = dict(manifest['items'])`, then
   `raw.update(items)`, `raw.update(items.retired_done)`, `raw.update(items.retired_open)`, then
   `index_reader.live(raw)`. A live entry always wins (it updates last), and the retired sets are
   folded by `live` itself — so a reader that walks them needs no change at all (D-0009, P15), and the
   fold is `index_reader`'s own and not a second copy of it.

   the Task reaches neither **Features landed** nor **Bugs fixed** nor **In progress**, and the
