## 4. What this plan does not do

- **It mints no Story and renumbers none.** The ten ids are the spec's own (`K1`).
- **It does not touch the 0.1 floor's `sweep` or `launch`.** The kernel reads the same observation
  and ends the session through its own action, so one wave owns the seat.
- **It does not teach the review lane a beat.** A reviewer pushes nothing and has no branch of its
  own; the spec's Out keeps it on pid and REPORT, and that is its own card.
- **It does not change `waits.max_session_age` or the p90 bounds.** They keep their meaning and
  their measurement; what changes is who they apply to.
- **It does not add a tool-name pattern to `tools/forbidden-names.txt`.** The scan the spec asks
  for is a test over `asf/kernel/` (`K9`).
- **It does not add a third launchd job.** The install verification plus a reader any clock can
  call is the answer; which clock calls it is operator config.
- **It does not drain the beat refs of the floor's own runs.** `heartbeat.clear_ended` already does
  that in the floor's health pass; the kernel calls it for its own jobs where it settles.
