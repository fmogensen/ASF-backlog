§2.6's readers and §2.7's five. `Conventions.stage_limits` is declared and read by nobody: all six
readers of the block go to the top-level key through `env.Product.stage_limits` instead, and each
invents its own grammar — `'3d'`, an integer of hours, an integer of minutes, `'3d'` again.
Nothing validates any of them, so a typo is silently the default. Task 1 made one grammar, one key
set and one reader; this Task is every caller arriving at it, and the four feeder numbers arriving
at their fields. Queues behind Task 5 because both write `asf/feeder/rows.py`.

- `asf/feeder/rows.py` — `STALEMATE_ROUND`, `ATTEMPT_LIMIT`, `DECISION_ROWS`,
  `MAX_SPECS_IN_FLIGHT`, `CORRECTION_ROUNDS` and their `.get()` guards replaced by the fields
- `asf/feeder/render.py` — `s1_hours` through `stage_limit`, still hours (PD8)
- `asf/tick/stale.py` — `DEFAULT_LIMITS`, `DURATION_RE`, `_DURATION_SECS`, `load_limits`,
  `limit_seconds` deleted; `find_stale` on `conv.stage_limit`
- `asf/evidence/closing.py` — `BUG_QUIET_DEFAULT` deleted
- `asf/workers/continuation.py` — `heartbeat_min` through `stage_limit`, still minutes (PD8)
- `asf/workers/stall.py` — `silent_minutes` through `stage_limit`, still minutes (PD8)
