The spec's fifth and sixth **In** bullets and `## The design` §5 — `report.HOOK_RETRIED_RE`,
`report.hook_retried`, `retried` through health, and the one changed line of
`hook_refusal_hold`'s cap test. This is where the card stops spending a second session to learn what
the first already wrote down: a session that fixes the refusal in-run costs **zero** dead runs where
it cost two, and one that retries and is genuinely blocked costs **one**.

It waits on Task 1 because Task 1 is what asks a session for the shape this reads back (PD15, D4):
the rule prints `pushed: no — hook refused twice: <what it said>`, and `HOOK_RETRIED_RE` believes
it. It also waits because both Tasks touch the same contract from opposite ends, and because
Tasks 2 and 3 share three files.

**The free first relaunch is not touched** (spec `## Out`, D5). A session that never pushed — the
`published` population — still spends no round and still gets its blind retry. `HOOK_REFUSAL_CAP`
stays **2**. Read **PD4** (`push_retry` stays a two-tuple, and why following the design literally
reddens `tests/test_lane.py`) and **PD11** (`hook_retried` takes a parsed dict) first.
