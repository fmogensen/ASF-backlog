**Steps**

1. `asf/kernel/dor.py`: `missing`'s test clause (`:164`, clause (i)) reads `card['acceptance']`
   and `card['gate']` instead of the prose. `acceptance_lines` (`:93`) and `gate_lines` (`:109`)
   keep their names and their bodies and become **Task 10's** readers only — the two regexes stay
   in the tree, used once, by the converter. That is the whole of "no hand-parsed format in the
   kernel's read path". A card with an empty `acceptance` and an empty `gate` is still held, with
   the same reason as today.
2. `asf/kernel/ports.py`: `fill_card` (`:670`) writes a groom-fill verdict's acceptance into the
   **field** as `[{text, test, done}]` instead of `- [ ] <line>` body lines, keeps writing
   `writes` as it does today, and a verdict raising `risk_raise` high sets `risk` and still leaves
   the note it leaves today (`dor.RISKS` at `:39` is the enum's vocabulary). `mint_story`
   (`:753`) writes a spec declaration's checkbox lines into the field. `item_from_card` (`:174`)
   reads the same fields it reads today plus `acceptance` and `gate` — no other key changes (§1.3).
3. `asf/record/new.py` and `asf/record/ids.py`: `asf new` and the Task mint write `acceptance` and
   `gate` as fields; `asf/record/setfield.py` lets `asf set` write both. `gate` is an array of
   command strings (D-0008) — the plan's Task **Gate** block is where a minted Task's commands come
   from.
4. `asf/proves.py`: `card_bullets` (`:188`) stops opening the `.md` and reads
   `card.load(...)[0]['acceptance']`. `bullets(body)` (`:171`) **stays** — it is the schema-1
   card's reader and the reader for a `Proves:` claim against a card the record no longer holds.
   `tick` (`:257`) flips `acceptance[n-1]['done']` and appends the history line, through
   `card.dumps` and Task 3's gate. `line <n>` is the **1-based index** of the array (D-0007): an index
   is the line, so the two can never disagree, and no `line` field is stored.
5. Ticking twice must change nothing the second time; ticking must leave every other entry alone;
   a claim on a line the card does not have is refused with the same words as today.
6. Create `tests/test_card_body_fields.py` for S-133159's twenty-one lines, and extend
   `tests/kernel/test_dor.py` (45 tests) and `tests/test_proves.py` (59 tests) — both green at
   this base (K5) — where the typed read changes what they assert. Keep the schema-1 cases in
   `tests.test_proves`: the fallback is behaviour this Task preserves, not behaviour it replaces.
