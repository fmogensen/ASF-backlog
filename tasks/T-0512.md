`## The design` §3, whole. The entries Task 3 enqueues become real `D-nnnn` cards in the target's own
record, `Closed`, carrying both readings; the decided card gains a History line naming the id; and
the record the tick is about to commit is re-indexed so its own `asf check` does not call it stale.
**Read PD5 before writing the branch** — it has the three placeholders in the spec's snippet turned
into the exact calls. PD6 has the dedupe, PD10 the `do_index` failure, PD11 the unmintable root,
PD12 the shape of the "no finding" assertion.

**Files**

- `asf/scorecard/loop.py` — `drain`'s third branch, `_decision_has`, `_decision_body`, the summary
  line, the 3-tuple returns (`:178-231`)
- `tests/test_scorecard.py` — `DecisionDrainTests`; the `(1, 1)` assertion at `:362`

**Steps**

1. `asf/scorecard/loop.py`, `drain` — `left, n_cards, n_lines = [], 0, 0` gains `n_decisions = 0`,
   and both returns become 3-tuples: `return 0, 0, 0` at `:187` and
   `return n_cards, n_lines, n_decisions` at `:220` (PD7).
2. The third branch, `elif e.get('kind') == 'decision':`, after the `'history'` one. In order:
   `_decision_has(root, e['marker'])` → `continue` (PD6); build/reuse the memoised `canonical`
   (PD5a); `try: new_id = mint_id(root, canonical, 'decision')` / `except SystemExit as why:` →
   `out(f'scorecard: decision for {e["card"]} not minted — {why}')`, `left.append(e)`, `continue`
   (D10, PD11 — the `try` wraps `mint_id` alone); then `write_new_item(root, canonical, 'decision',
   new_id, {'title': e['title']}, _decision_body(e), today(), 'scorecard: ' + e['verdict'],
   acceptance=(), state='Closed')`; then `n_decisions += 1`.
3. `_decision_body(e)` — a module-level pure helper beside `_slug`, so it is readable without a
   record. Three or four paragraphs, the spec's §3 block:
   - `f"The scorecard loop verified {e['card']} against the cause it was filed for, {e['key']}."`
   - `f"Reading: {e['before']} {e['unit']} over the {e['weeks']} weeks before it landed on "`
     `f"{e['landed']}, {e['after']} {e['unit']} over the {e['weeks']} weeks after."` then the
     verdict sentence: for `moved`, "The number moved by at least the fraction the loop verifies
     against, so the change stands."; for `got worse`, "The number got worse by at least the
     fraction the loop verifies against, so the change is being taken back out; the revert is asked
     for on its own card." Do **not** render a percentage here: `min_move` is not on the entry and
     adding it is a field the spec's enqueue does not carry.
   - `f"Landed by {', '.join(e['commits'])}."` — **only** when `e.get('commits')` (PD9). When the
     verdict is `got worse` and `commits` is empty, `f"The record has it {facts.UNTRACEABLE}."`
     instead.
   - the marker line, `e['marker']`, last.
4. `_decision_has(root, marker)` — beside `_inbox_has` and shaped like it: glob
   `os.path.join(root, TYPES['decision'][0], '*.md')`, read each, return True on the marker, swallow
   `OSError`. `TYPES` from `asf.record.core`, imported inside the function like the rest of `drain`'s
   record imports; **never the literal `'decisions'`** (PD6, P18).
5. The link-back History line, immediately after the mint, in the same branch:
   `f"- {today()} scorecard: {'adopted' if e['verdict'] == 'moved' else 'reverted'} as {new_id} — "`
   `f"{e['before']} before, {e['after']} after"`, appended to `_card_path(root, e['card'])` through
   the same `frontmatter.parse` / `append_history_lines` / `frontmatter.render` round trip the
   `'history'` branch uses, guarded by the same `if line in body: continue`, and counting in
   `n_lines` (PD8). A decided card that is not on disk — it cannot normally happen, `verify` only
   judges a card it found — is skipped with the Decision still written, not re-queued: the Decision
   is the record, the line is the convenience.
