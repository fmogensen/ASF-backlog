## Description
stories: S-15001, S-15002, S-15004
writes: asf/feeder/rows.py, tests/test_feeder.py

§2.2, whole. One helper and three rows, every one a pure function of a `landed_shas` argument that
defaults to `None`. The feeder still reads no git and no filesystem (P3 of the spec); with no fact
handed in, this Task changes nothing anybody sees (P5).

**Files**

- `asf/feeder/rows.py` — `ON_TRUNK`, `landed_ids`, the `ON TRUNK` gate in `task_rows`, the
  `landed_ids` substitution in `task_rows` and `hold_unlanded`, the `parked` branch of
  `correction_rows`, the `landed_shas=None` parameter threaded through `candidates`, `plan_rows`,
  `feature_rows` and `task_rows`, and the `hold_unlanded` exemption of P8.
- `tests/test_feeder.py` — `AlreadyOnTrunkTests`, `AfterCountsTheTrunkTests`,
  `ParkedCorrectionTests`, and a case for `landed_ids` itself.

**Steps**

1. `ON_TRUNK = 'ON TRUNK'` beside the other action constants (`rows.py:41-51`).
2. `landed_ids(items, landed_shas=None)` exactly as §2.2 fences it: the record's
   `DONE_STATES` ids, union every key of `landed_shas`. Its docstring is the one in the spec.
   Note in the module what the fold buys — it runs over *all* items, not `ix.feature_tasks`, so an
   `after:` naming a Task of another Feature is answerable at all.
3. Thread `landed_shas=None` through `plan_rows` → `candidates` → `feature_rows` → `task_rows`,
   and into `hold_unlanded(rows, items, landed_shas=None)`. Keyword-only in spirit: every existing
   positional call site stays byte-identical.
4. **Gate one.** In `task_rows`, first thing in the loop body — before the `after:` check — a Task
   whose own id is a key of `landed_shas` gets the non-launching row §2.2 fences: `tier=2`,
   `kind=PLAN_CODE`, `action=f'{ON_TRUNK} {sha[:12]}'`, `waits_on='trunk'`, and the `reason`
   naming the sha, the subject and the card's state. `continue`. `tasks` is already filtered to
   `state == 'New'`, so an Active or Closed Task named by the trunk mints nothing extra.
5. **Gate two.** `task_rows`'s `landed` becomes `landed_ids(items, landed_shas)`, and so does
   `hold_unlanded`'s. One substitution in each; no other line of either moves.
6. **Gate three.** In `correction_rows`, before the `rounds >= CORRECTION_ROUNDS` branch, a
   correction carrying `parked` gets the non-launching `PARKED …` row §2.2 fences, with
   `waits_on='operator'`, and `continue`. It is neither `FIX → CORRECT` nor
   `STALEMATE → ADJUDICATE`, at any round count.
7. P8: `hold_unlanded` leaves a row alone when its action starts with `ON_TRUNK` or `PARKED`,
   the same way it already leaves `GROOM_ADJUDICATE` alone, with the reason in a comment.
8. Tests, in this module's idiom (a hand-built item map, no temp dirs, no git):
   `AlreadyOnTrunkTests` (§3.2), `AfterCountsTheTrunkTests` (§3.3), `ParkedCorrectionTests` (§3.5's
   feeder third), and `landed_ids` over the two disagreements the docstring names.
9. Confirm the existing `DeclaredOrderIsADependency` and
   `NoRowLaunchesBehindAnUnlandedPredecessor` are untouched — they are the regression, and neither
   may change.
