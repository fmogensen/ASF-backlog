The spec's §2.2 down to *"`asf/ci_measure.py` reads them"* — the selector and the fields — and
nothing else. After this lands, a product that configured `conventions.ci_workflow` the documented
way stops recording an empty `ci` stream, and every run it does record carries the four figures the
measure needs: per job the exact seconds it ran and the seconds it waited, per run its wall clock
and the worst wait any of its jobs suffered.

P8 is why this Task exists and it is worth reading before the design: the tick runs
`cmd_backfill(days=1, workflow=ci_workflow(product))` on every tick, `ci_from_api` keeps a run only
when `r.get('name') == workflow` — the run's **display** name against a configured **file** name —
and this repo's own `.github/workflows/tests.yml` declares `name: tests`. For the documented config
form the two differ and the stream is empty. Every other stage of this card reads that stream.

**Read PD2 before writing a line of this Task.** The spec's P9 says the job dicts *"are not
validated and may be added to"*. They are validated: `metrics.py:223` applies `JOB_SCHEMA` to every
one and `_apply` **raises** on an unknown key. Add `seconds` and `queued_s` to the job dict without
adding them to `JOB_SCHEMA` and every `ci` event fails with
`SchemaError: ci.jobs[0]: unknown key(s) queued_s, seconds` — the backfill dies on its first run and
the card's measure reads an empty stream for a new reason. **Read PD14** for why the `skipped`
filter stays.

Three things are load-bearing. **`minutes` does not move** (D4): it floors to whole minutes
(`mins()`, `:1666-1670`) and `score.per_item`, `window_row`, `cost_table` and every stored weekly row
are built on the floored figure, so redefining it would move every historical scorecard number. The
two new job figures sit **beside** it. **Nothing is backfilled onto a recorded run** (D5):
`natural_key('ci', ev)` is `(run, attempt)` and `append_event` dedupes on it, so a recorded run
cannot gain a field without rewriting the stream — `natural_key` is not touched, the window is 14
days, and the readings fill themselves. **A run's `queue_s` is the worst of its jobs' waits, never
their sum** (D21): the wait a Feature's author feels is the wait before the run's slowest-to-start
job began, and the sum double-counts jobs that queued in parallel, which on a fanned-out run is most
of them.

**Files**

- `asf/metrics/metrics.py` — four edits and no fifth. **`JOB_SCHEMA`** (`:160-161`) gains
  `'seconds': (('num', 'null'), None)` and `'queued_s': (('num', 'null'), None)` — PD2; `'null'`
  because §2.2's own comment says *"None when either is absent"*, and defaulted because a job
  recorded before this change must still validate. **The `ci` run schema** (`:117-123`) gains
  `'wall_minutes': (('num',), 0)` and `'queue_s': (('num',), 0)`, §2.2's fence byte for byte — both
  non-null and defaulted, so every recorded event still validates and reads them as 0.
  **`ci_from_api`'s selector** (`:1680-1684`): the listing's jq gains `path`, and the filter becomes
  §2.2's — `r.get('name') == workflow or os.path.basename(r.get('path') or '') == workflow`. A run of
  another workflow is still dropped. **The job and event dicts** (`:1705-1707`, `:1718-1725`): a new
  module-level `secs(a, b)` beside `mins` returning a float to 1 dp or `None` when either stamp is
