**Files**: `asf/groom/inbox.py`, `asf/groom/groom.py`, `tests/test_inbox_shape.py` (adds
`IntakeTest`), `tests/test_groom.py`, `tests/test_t10_gate.py`.

**Steps**
1. `INBOX_KV_RE` widens to `type|parent|signature|severity|writes|stories`.
   `parse_inbox_file(text) -> shape.Card`:
   - The header block follows PD5.
   - `## Features` and `## Acceptance` are lifted out of the rest of the text. A Features item is
     the text after `- `. An Acceptance item is the text after `- [ ] ` or `- [x] `, and empty
     boxes are dropped.
   - Everything else, stripped, is `description`.
   - `writes` and `stories` values stay raw strings in `headers`. Step 3 splits them.
2. `intake_dir(args)` (PD10). Remove `INBOX_TYPES`, `infer_inbox_type`, `BROKEN_WORDS_RE` and
   `GOAL_WORD_RE`.
3. `process_inbox(root, canonical, date, default_bug_parent=None, intake_dir='inbox')`:
   - Skip a file that already has a `## Question`, as today.
   - Call `derive`. A `Question` is appended as today.
   - A `Shape` is minted with `mint_id` and `write_new_item(..., why='inbox', shape=(rule, type), acceptance=card.acceptance, sections={'Features': card.features})`.
   - Typed fields: `title`, `parent`, `decided: False`. A Bug also gets `severity` (the header, if it is `S1`/`S2`/`S3`, else `S3`), `found_in: dev` and `signature`. A Task also gets `writes` and `stories` as lists, split on `,` and stripped.
   - The move to `<intake_dir>/done/<slug>.md` stays as today.
4. `groom.py`:
   - `groom_inbox_section` reads `SHAPE_LINE_RE` from the card's body. When it matches, the why
     is `from inbox as <type> (<rule>), awaiting a decision`. Otherwise it stays
     `from inbox, awaiting a decision`.
   - `cmd_groom` passes `intake_dir=inbox.intake_dir(args)`.
5. `tests/test_groom.py`:
   - Delete `test_infer_bug_from_broken_language`, `test_infer_epic_from_goal_language` and
     `test_infer_feature_default`.
   - Move the two parsing tests to `Card` attributes (PD6), and add
     `test_header_lines_after_a_blank_line` (PD5).
   - The three `Checkout is broken` intake tests add `signature: checkout-pay` in the header
     block. `test_inbox_bug_with_no_default_configured_asks_a_question` still expects its
     Question.
   - The `ambiguous feature` test is unchanged.
6. `tests/test_t10_gate.py`: the `report.md` card becomes
   `# Checkout is broken\nparent: E-0009\nsignature: checkout-pay\nCustomers cannot pay right now.\n`.
   The counts do not change.
7. `IntakeTest`: every case the spec lists. `test_epic_card_keeps_its_features` runs `asf index`
   and then `asf check` on the `make_record` fixture, which has the stream folders (PD11).
   `test_intake_dir_is_the_convention` writes a product file with `conventions:\n  intake_dir: intake`
   under a temp `ASF_HOME` and runs `asf groom --product p`.
