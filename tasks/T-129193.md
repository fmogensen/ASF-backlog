The spec's **In** 1 and its design §1, whole: one new module with one entry point, which reads
each ended run's `report.ruling_fields` once and writes the three typed fields onto the card
through the record's own writer, each behind its own gate. It ships **whole** rather than one arm
at a time, because `apply()`, the `KINDS` table, the once-per-pass `canonical` record, the two
mirror lines and the write-back into the step's shared `items` are one scaffolding and S-92305's
own acceptance pins every part of it (`PD1`). Two seams come with it and are its only changes
outside the new module: `widen_footprint.write_card` gains a `writer='widen'` keyword, and
`step_health.run` gains one wrapper and one call between `file_rulings` and `rejudge`.

**Read this Task from `docs/plans/f-0330.md`, not from this card.** The record cuts a Task card's
description at 4000 characters (`PD18`) and this section is longer, so the card stops inside the
Steps, past which its **Gate** and its **Acceptance** — the blocks copied from the approved spec,
which are what this Task is judged against — do not reach the card at all; the card's `links.plan`
is this file. Four decision rows bind this Task and are on no card: `PD8` (every citation below
re-measured at this base, and the two the spec gives one or two lines off), `PD11` (the four
`set_typed` writes reproduced under `writer='ruling'`, and the exact refusal format string
`D8` turns on), `PD17` (what a refusal line can name, and what it cannot — do not wire
`refused_by` into the `supersede` or `block` arm) and `PD13` (this Task's `--touched` set is 12
modules).

**Files**

- `asf/tick/apply_rulings.py` — **new**, and the spec fences it in §1: the module docstring as
  written there; `KINDS = {'superseded_by': ('adjudicate', 'fix-bug', 'correct'), 'blocked_on':
  ('adjudicate',), 'writes': ('adjudicate',)}`; `reason(text)`; `supersede(ctx, items, canonical,
  run, sid, out)`; `block(ctx, items, run, bid, out)`; `refootprint(ctx, items, run, globs, out)`;
  `apply(ctx, items, out=print)`. `as_list` comes from `asf.record.core` (`:53`); `closed_state`
  and `latest` from `asf.workers.lifecycle`; `supersession_edges` and `cycles` from
  `asf.groom.conflicts`; `removal` from `asf.record.retire`; `write_card`, `_reindex` and
  `refused_by` from `asf.tick.widen_footprint`. The three kind names in `KINDS` are real session
  kinds, not brief aliases: `step_wave.worker_row` writes `kind=brief.kind` onto the row
  (`asf/tick/step_wave.py:719`, unmoved at `55d9ec2`, `PD25`/`PD26`) and `spawn` writes
  `'kind': row.kind` into the session record (`asf/workers/spawn.py:1296`), so `'fix-bug'`,
  `'correct'` and `'adjudicate'` are what a run carries and `'fixer'` is a different kind that
  `KINDS` deliberately omits.
- `asf/tick/widen_footprint.py` — **one keyword and one line**, and nothing else in the file:
  `def write_card(root, item_id, updates, stamp, note, product=None, writer='widen')` at `:364`,
  and `set_typed(rec, updates, writer=writer, product=product)` at `:379`. The default keeps all
  of the widening rule's own call sites byte-identical.
- `asf/tick/step_health.py` — the `apply_rulings` wrapper the spec fences, placed beside
  `widen_footprints` (`:228-237`) and shaped like it, and one call in `run` between `:270` and
  `:271` so the order reads `file_rulings` → `apply_rulings` → `rejudge` → `widen_footprints`.
- `tests/test_apply_rulings.py` — **new**: `SupersedeTests`, `BlockedOnTests` and `FootprintTests`
  on the `WidenStepBase` fixture `tests/test_widen.py:150-222` already provides (`self.root`, its
  `card`/`active`/`items`/`finished` helpers, and its `tick`/`rows`/`writes`/`card_text` readers at
  `:204-222`). `FixKindTests` is **not** this Task's — it belongs
