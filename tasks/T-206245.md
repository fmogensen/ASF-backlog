**Files**

**Steps**

1. `asf/kernel/actions.py`: `Launch` (`:13`) gains `final: bool = False` after `charge`
   (Task 4), with its sentence in the docstring: the one review an item gets at its round cap,
   after which no further round follows. `describe` (`:390`) renders
   `FINAL REVIEW <item> <head>` for it.
2. `asf/kernel/ports.py`: one machine-block key — `FINAL_REVIEW = 'kernel_final_review'`, the
   head the final review ran on — in `KERNEL_KEYS` (`:88-90`) and the docstring's key list,
   cleared by the item's rebuild with the rest of the round state (`asf/kernel/apply.py:243`).
3. `asf/kernel/decide.py`: `_fix_round` (`:1755`) at the cap does one thing before it may record
   a Stuck, and **after** the strong round and the rebuild have had their turn in the order they
   have today (`:1763-1773`): if the PR's current head carries no verdict — `verdict_holds`
   against the ledger (`asf/kernel/model.py:344`, P4) — and the card's `kernel_final_review` is
   not already that head, return `_Judged(State.REVIEW, review_branch=pr.branch, final=True)` and
   let the Stuck wait (D-0009). A PR that conflicts is not sent: the conflict arm at `:1562-1571`
   runs before this and keeps its order.
4. `asf/kernel/briefs.py`: one paragraph in the review brief when `launch.final` — this is the
   last review this item gets, the author will not be given another round, so the verdict decides
   whether the work lands or parks. It goes in `extra` beside the PR-under-review lines
   (`:623-625`), before `VERDICT_RULE`, so it is the last thing read before the verdict is asked.
5. `asf/kernel/decide.py`: the final review's own verdict, judged on the next tick by the path
   that already exists — `approve` goes to Landing by the ordinary route and the item is **never**
   Stuck (this is the case C5 was losing); `changes` goes to `Stuck(owner=operator)` whose reason
   names the cap **and** the final review's findings, with those findings written to the card so
   the operator's answer has them. The answer grants one more round exactly as it does today
   (`ApplyAnswer.extra_round`), and its keys join the ruled set through Task 1's
   `findings.ruled_by`.
6. `asf/kernel/apply.py`: write `kernel_final_review` to the head on a `final` launch, so the
   same head never spends a second final review; a head that moved is new work and gets its own,
   which the cap bounds anyway. A final review is a review: it never touches `FIX_ROUNDS`, which
   Task 4 already made conditional on `a.charge` — a `final` launch carries `charge=False`.
