§2.11 and §2.12. The Task that pays for Task 3: the procedure text that is repeated on every single
launch becomes a citation of the skill that now carries it, and the two readings say what is in force
and what it costs. **The generated facts are asserted unshrunk** (D11): every character of the
preamble's card, parents, documents, footprint, tests, last report, branch state and "Where to look"
is still there, character for character, on the same fixture row. The 93 % of a run's cost that was
the agent discovering what the runner already knew is not what this card buys back.

**Files**

- `docs/design/brief-baseline.json` — **new, and written first** (**PD12**): the procedure and fact
  characters per kind for all 18 kinds, generated at this Task's own head through the same synthetic
  row `doctor.check_briefs` uses (`asf/doctor.py:975-993`), before one character is cut. P23's
  figure is stale — 110,300 characters over 18 kinds here, not 112,911 — and the acceptance compares
  against this file, which is why §2.12 checks it in.
- `asf/briefs/build.py`:
  - `TAIL` (2,727 characters, 47 lines, on every launch — measured here) becomes §2.11's four-line
    citation of `report-shape`. The REPORT envelope, the heartbeat paragraph, the `NEEDS OPERATOR`
    explanation and the uncommitted-work rule are already in the skill, byte for byte, from Task 3.
  - `size_rows(product, kinds=None)` → per kind, the procedure characters, the fact characters and
    the total, for `asf briefs size` and for T13. The split is by section: what `preamble` generates
    is facts, what the template, `TAIL` and the rules contribute is procedure.
- `asf/briefs/preamble.py` — the three `DEFAULT_RULES` lines that are pure procedure (the push rule,
  the heartbeat, the report) become one line naming `report-shape`. The other five stay: they are the
  product's own policy, resolved from its conventions, and the cheapest text in the brief. Every fact
  section is untouched, and `preamble.py:20-24`'s refusal to cut ids, paths, branch or footprint
  stands.
- `asf/briefs/templates/review.md`, `spec.md`, `coder.md`, `plan.md` — each loses its procedure prose
  to its skill and keeps its facts: the paths, the round number, the branch, the deliverable, the
  shape of the final message. `review.md` cites `review-standard`, `spec.md` cites `spec-shape`,
  `coder.md` cites `pre-push-gate`, and all four cite `report-shape`. **These four files are in the
  amendable set** (`asf/amendable.py:53`) and a worker session is refused them at `human-now`
  (**PD16**); the `NEEDS OPERATOR` line below names both ways through, and the rest of this Task
  lands without them.
- `asf/cli.py` — two parsers (**PD18**): `roles` with a positional `roles_command` of
  `choices=['launch']`, `--product`, `--kind` and `--json`; and `briefs` with a positional
  `briefs_command` of `choices=['size']`, `--product` and `--json`. The singular `brief` registered
  by `asf/briefs/build.py:545` is untouched.
- `asf/roles/launch.py` — `table_rows(product, caps=None)` and the text renderer: §2.12's table, one
  row per kind with the role, the model label (`briefs.build.model_for`), the effort, the mode, the
  tool summary, the write surface and both caps, plus the `skipped_flags` line from
  `capability.missing`. That last line is the point: a factory whose runtime lacks `--effort` is not
  one that silently runs everything at the top of the range, it is one that says so on every reading.
