**This is the half the reshape files behind the daily prune.** The spec's §4 and §5, and the
threshold half of §1 and §8. When it lands the factory does not wait for the daily clock: every
`health` tick reads the used total, and at or above `ci.artifacts.prune_at_pct` of
`ci.artifacts.quota_gb` it prunes oldest-first to `HYSTERESIS_PCT` points under the threshold — under
the same protection rule, at any percentage. On the small quota this card came from this is the half
that keeps the product under the quota at all (the spec's own arithmetic: a 2-day retention alone
settles at ≈0.9 GB against 0.5 GB), which is why it is filed behind the prune and not dropped.

Read **PD5** first — the clause goes on **all four** of `runners_cell`'s returns, or the product this
card came from never sees it — then **PD10** (the staleness clause reads the product's own health
clock through a non-raising helper, never `scheduler.clocks`, which raises for a product that
declares no `clocks:` block), **PD13** (the target arithmetic C10 leaves implicit) and **PD7**
(`tests.test_status` does not exist in this checkout).
