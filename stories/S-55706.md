## Acceptance
- [ ] Two `load_items` calls over a record of three cards call `frontmatter.parse` three times, not six — proven by tests/test_record_cache.py
- [ ] Rewriting one card's file makes the next `load_items` call parse exactly that one card — proven by tests/test_record_cache.py
- [ ] Rewriting a card with identical text parses nothing — the cache is validated on content, not mtime — proven by tests/test_record_cache.py
- [ ] `os.utime` on an unchanged card parses nothing — proven by tests/test_record_cache.py
- [ ] The `meta` two `load_items` calls return for one card are different objects, and mutating the first leaves the second exactly as parsed — proven by tests/test_record_cache.py
- [ ] `frontmatter.render` of a loaded record reproduces the original file byte for byte — proven by tests/test_record_cache.py
- [ ] `frontmatter.render` of a mutated clone matches a fresh parse mutated the same way — proven by tests/test_record_cache.py
- [ ] A card with broken frontmatter is reported on the first `load_items` call and on the second — a parse error is not cached — proven by tests/test_record_cache.py
- [ ] `clear_parse_cache()` empties the store — proven by tests/test_record_cache.py
