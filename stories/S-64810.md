## Acceptance
- [ ] After `groom --apply`, a member the delivery landed without has no `delivered_by:`, the lead `delivers:` no longer names it, and each card carries the `delivery: released` History line.

- [ ] A well-formed delivery draws no line from asf check. — proven by tests/test_backlog.py
