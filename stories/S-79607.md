## Acceptance
- [ ] the laid-down repo carries every tag the manifest records, created as the manifest says — `git tag -a -m` with its annotation for each annotated entry — plus one pre-release tag and a `CHANGELOG.md` with their entries
- [ ] the `tags` act runs `version.of_commit`, `version.label`, `version.plan`, `version.tag_entries`, `version.health` and `install.newest_tag` over that repo and asserts each answer against the refs the manifest records
- [ ] an answer that carries the sha of a tag object rather than the commit it points at fails the act, named with both shas
- [ ] the act runs `version.cut` and `version.prerelease` into a scratch clone of the laid-down repo, and `version.health` is green after
- [ ] `install.newest_tag` picks the highest v<x.y.z> by integer tuple and drops a pre-release stray — proven by tests/test_install.py
