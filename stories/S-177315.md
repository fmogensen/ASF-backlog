## Description
Small, low-risk PRs land on green CI and proved acceptance without an LLM review. Measured 2026-10-11: reviews were 510 of 1471 launches (35%); quota is now the binding resource (two accounts at 92-97% weekly).

## Change
- `kernel.review.skip_small: {max_lines: 150}`. A Task or Bug PR under max_lines changed, touching no `risk.high` path, with required checks green and every Acceptance line of the item proved by a landed `Proves:` claim, merges without a review session. A sampled 1 in 5 still gets a review, so quality stays measured.
- Off by default; ASF turns it on.

## Acceptance
- [ ] Such a PR merges on green with no review launch (test). A high-risk path, an unproved line or a diff over max_lines still gets a review (test). The sampling rate is honoured over a fixture of 10 (test).
- [ ] Measured: review share of launches and first-review-approve rate before and after.
