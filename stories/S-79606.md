## Acceptance
- [ ] the `record` act runs a groom pass and applies a replan over the laid-down record, and commits each through the pre-commit of the record (`asf redact --pre-commit`, which runs `staged_guard.check`)
- [ ] each commit is made with the index read at the parent of the commit and the tree at its head — a stale index over a record of real size — and the act reports what the guard answered
- [ ] the act runs once more over an intake note whose `done/` copy is not a byte-for-byte superstring of the note, and names the blobs the guard compared
- [ ] a red `record` act prints the input and the answer on one named line, never a traceback
- [ ] `tests/rehearsal/record/` carries intake in its three states: notes still in the intake dir, notes moved to `done/` with their arrow header, and at least one whose `done/` copy is not a superstring of it
- [ ] `staged_guard.check` refuses a staged card deletion whose text survives nowhere in the commit and holds when the move to `done/` copies it — proven by tests/test_staged_guard.py
