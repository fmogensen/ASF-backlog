## Acceptance
- [ ] nearest_parent climbs the `parent:` of a card to the first open ancestor of a type legal for its shape: a signed card parented to a Task yields the Story above that Task
- [ ] A Feature-shaped card parented to a Task is supplied the Epic above that Task
- [ ] A card carrying `writes:` and parented to an Epic has its `writes:` dropped and is minted a Feature under that Epic, with a clause naming the drop
- [ ] best_epic picks the open Epic the feeder would reach first: decided before undecided, then rank with an unranked Epic last, then id
- [ ] An S1 card sharing no title word with any Epic, in a record holding three open Epics, is minted a Feature under the Epic best_epic names, and an undecided better-ranked Epic loses to a decided worse-ranked one
- [ ] supplied_parent ladders nearest_parent, default_bug_parent, shape.infer_parent_epic and best_epic, preferring default_bug_parent for a signed card and ranking it below infer_parent_epic otherwise
- [ ] A default_bug_parent the record does not hold, or holds Closed or removed, is never supplied: it is validated through core.is_open, and the clause names the Epic supplied instead
- [ ] asf/groom/inbox.py gains no import of asf.tick for that validation, at module level or inside a function
- [ ] Tiers 2 to 5 of the ladder are gated on `epic` being a legal parent type for the shape, so a Task-shaped card is only ever supplied a parent by the climb
- [ ] A `parent:` the record does not hold is dropped, the clause names it, and the next pass answers the Epic question
- [ ] A card carrying both `signature:` and `writes:` keeps the signature, drops the `writes:`, says so in a clause, and the intake file in `done/` still carries the `writes:` line the operator wrote
- [ ] title_acceptance supplies the one acceptance item of a Story from its title: a card parented to a Feature with no acceptance is minted a Story whose `## Acceptance` holds exactly `- [ ] <title>`, and record.check finds nothing on it
- [ ] A card parented to a Story is read against the Feature of that Story, and the clause quotes the rule
- [ ] A card carrying two `## Features` bullets and a `parent:` is minted an Epic with rule `by-rule-features-list`, carrying its `## Features` section, and asf check is rc 0 on the result
