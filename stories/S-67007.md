## Acceptance
- [ ] `harvest` and `watch` both resolve to owner `asf` and both appear in `asf tick --manifest` with their callables
- [ ] `harvest` runs as a tick step: `asf tick --steps harvest` alone lands a finished lane branch and records its sha — proven by tests/test_tick_steps.py
- [ ] `asf tick --steps watch` runs the watch step alone
- [ ] `sample/product.yaml`'s dispatch clock names both `harvest` and `watch`
- [ ] `asf doctor` over a product whose clocks tick every `asf`-owned step but one prints exactly one `NEEDS OPERATOR:` line naming that step, and none over a product that ticks them all — proven by tests/test_doctor.py
- [ ] A step the product declares `off` is not reported by the doctor — proven by tests/test_doctor.py
