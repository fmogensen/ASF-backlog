## Acceptance
- [ ] `capability.flags()` over a fixture binary returns exactly the long-option names of its `--help`, both aliases of a two-name line included — proven by tests/test_launch.py
- [ ] a binary that is absent, exits non-zero, prints nothing, or hangs past `PROBE_TIMEOUT_S` returns `set()` and raises nothing — proven by tests/test_launch.py
- [ ] the probe result is cached until the binary fixture changes, and `reset()` clears the cache — proven by tests/test_launch.py
- [ ] `supports()` and `missing()` read the same probe — proven by tests/test_launch.py
- [ ] `effort_levels` reads the parenthesised list off `--effort` continuation line, and falls back to the built-in order when the entry carries none — proven by tests/test_launch.py
- [ ] `effort_levels` is empty for a runtime with no `--effort` option and for an absent binary — proven by tests/test_launch.py
