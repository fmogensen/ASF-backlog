## Acceptance
- [ ] A delivery branch whose commits name different members of the lead `delivers:` draws no lane refusal. — proven by tests/test_lane.py
- [ ] A commit naming an id that is no member is refused as `naming`, and the text quotes the members. — proven by tests/test_lane.py
- [ ] A member no commit names is not a refusal: the branch lands and the landing line reads `delivered <ids> — no commit for <id>`, under both the combined and the per-branch gate. — proven by tests/test_lane.py
- [ ] A delivery whose every member is named lands with no missing clause on the landing line. — proven by tests/test_lane.py
- [ ] A merge commit on a delivery branch is still refused as `merge`. — proven by tests/test_lane.py
- [ ] A delivery item footprint is the ordered union of the lead and its members `writes:`. — proven by tests/test_lane.py
