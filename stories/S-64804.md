## Acceptance
- [ ] A card carrying `size: small` is small whatever its glob count; the same card without the field, at four globs, is not.
- [ ] A Bug of severity S2 or S3 is small at any glob count; an S1 Bug never is.
- [ ] A Feature or Story with a child card is not small, even at one glob.
- [ ] A decided, open, unblocked small card that declares `writes:` appears in `deliverable_items`; one that is undecided, blocked, Active, carries `after:`, or declares no `writes:` does not.
- [ ] A card already carrying `delivers:` or `delivered_by:` is not deliverable, and neither is a Feature past stage `card`.
- [ ] Two members whose `writes:` overlap are one group; two whose writes share an area are one group at that `area_depth` and two at a deeper one; a component of one yields no proposal.
- [ ] budget_cut walks the component in (rank, id) order and keeps a member while all three ceilings hold, skipping one that breaches a ceiling without pulling a later smaller member ahead of it.
- [ ] delivery_max_items: 0 yields no proposal at all, and a component in which fewer than two members fit yields none either.
- [ ] releasable returns (lead, member) for a member still New with no commit once the lead has landed, returns nothing while every member is New, and returns every open member when the lead itself is the open one.
