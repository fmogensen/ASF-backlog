## Acceptance
- [ ] Every one of the seven markers in `conventions.DEFAULT_DEBUG_TOGGLES` matches its own toggle line — `debugger;`, `breakpoint()`, `pdb.set_trace(`, `it.only(`, `describe.skip(`, `xit(` and a `DEBUG = True` flag
- [ ] None of the seven matches a line this repo lands legitimately — `def fit(sections, limit):`, a `fit(` call, `@unittest.skipUnless(...)`, `unittest.expectedFailure(fn)`, `exit(2)` and `self.skipTest(...)`
- [ ] A marker line that also carries `debug-ok: <why it stays>` is not a hit; the same line with a bare `debug-ok` and nothing after the colon still is; a product waiver that does not compile waives nothing
- [ ] A marker added under `specs_dir`, `plans_dir`, `reviews_dir` or a `conventions.doc_paths` glob is not a hit; the same line added under `asf/`, `tests/` or `sample/` is
- [ ] A product that declared no `debug_toggles` block is checked under the default `**` globs, and a product that declared `paths: []` has every line legal
- [ ] Only the lines a branch adds count — a marker already on the trunk is no hit, and one marker added in two files is two hits, each with its own `file:line`
- [ ] `lane.lane_refusal` over a branch that added a toggle returns the pair `debug-toggle` plus text naming every `file:line`, and returns None over a clean branch
- [ ] A branch carrying both a customer-content hit and a debug toggle is handed back as the customer-content pair — customer content is first in the chain
- [ ] `lane.precheck` over a branch already past PUSHED calls `enter_back` once with `kind=debug-toggle` and leaves every `file:line` in the refusal text
- [ ] `validate_mapping` names `debug_toggles`, `debug_toggles.paths`, `debug_toggles.markers` and `debug_toggles.waiver` on a misshapen block, and its own docstring list of checked keys contains `debug_toggles`
- [ ] The seven marker literals appear in no file under `asf/` other than `asf/conventions.py`
- [ ] The trees `asf/`, `tests/`, `tools/`, `evals/`, `plugin/` and `sample/` of this checkout carry no marker hit that is not waived with a reason
- [ ] `landing_class` keeps its name, its signature and its answer for every path after `is_doc` is split out of it — proven by tests/test_lane.py
- [ ] `customer_content.added_hits` keeps its behaviour and its three-dot `base...head` range after the `-U0` read is promoted to a public `diff()` — proven by tests/test_customer_content.py
