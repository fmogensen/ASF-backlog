## Acceptance

- [ ] [ ] the overlay folds the retired-done and retired-open sets exactly as the index reader folds them

- [ ] [ ] the retention floor is derived from the longest window a working-set reader uses plus a week, not written down twice
- [ ] [ ] a retention below the floor is refused before any card is read, naming both numbers and where the floor came from
- [ ] [ ] a retention of zero turns archiving off and moves nothing
- [ ] [ ] asf doctor carries a row for the retention and the working set's share
