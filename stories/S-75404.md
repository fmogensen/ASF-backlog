## Acceptance
- [ ] report.FIELDS and FIELD_RE's alternation carry 'undelivers' between 'superseded_by' and 'precedent', and parse() reads an undelivers: line as its own field rather than a continuation of the field above it
- [ ] ruling_fields returns a fourth key 'undelivers': whitespace-split ids for 'undelivers: T-0002 T-0003', and None for none, None, n/a, -, the em dash, an empty value and an absent line alike
- [ ] an undelivers: line placed above superseded_by: corrupts the field above it, measured in this checkout: 'writes: asf/a.py' then 'undelivers: T-0002 T-0003' reads as the four-glob footprint ['asf/a.py', 'undelivers:', 'T-0002', 'T-0003'], and 'writes: none' reads as ['none', 'undelivers:', 'T-0002', 'T-0003'] -- a claim nobody made
