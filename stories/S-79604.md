## Acceptance
- [ ] `tests/rehearsal/` holds `manifest.json`, `record/` and `repo/`, and the manifest carries the keys cards, widest_id, refs, intake, built_at, asf_version and source_sha
- [ ] the manifest claims a widest id of at least five digits for one prefix, and at least one refs entry with `annotated: true`
- [ ] `rehearsal.manifest_holds` returns an empty list on the committed snapshot, and one line per broken claim when a card is removed, an id is narrowed to four digits, or a tag annotated flag is flipped
- [ ] `asf rehearse --build --from-product <p>` writes the snapshot and its manifest from a real record and leaves that record unchanged
- [ ] two --build runs over the same source sha write byte-identical files, because the filler is seeded by the length of the cleared run
- [ ] `tools/check_generic.sh` and `tools/check_privacy.sh` are clean with `tests/rehearsal/` tracked, and no title, body, History prose, intake note text or commit message from the real record appears in it
