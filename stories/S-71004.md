## Acceptance
- [ ] every `*.md` in `roles_dir()` has exactly one `TABLE` row and every row names a role file — proven by tests/test_launch.py
- [ ] every row's `permission_mode` is one of the runtime's six and every `effort` one of its five — proven by tests/test_launch.py
- [ ] `launch_for` resolves all 18 `briefs.KINDS` through `roles.BINDINGS` with no `RoleError` — proven by tests/test_launch.py
- [ ] a row's `writes` carries no literal path, only `{convention}` placeholders and `<record>`, and `resolve_writes` fills them from the product's conventions — proven by tests/test_launch.py
- [ ] `connections` is empty for every role — proven by tests/test_launch.py
- [ ] `launch_for` applies an operator `roles:` override of `effort` and `permission_mode` only, and refuses one of `tools`, `writes` or `connections` — proven by tests/test_launch.py
- [ ] no `roles.UNBOUND` reason mentions a launch path — proven by tests/test_roles.py
