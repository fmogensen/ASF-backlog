## Acceptance
- [ ] `.github/workflows/release.yml` carries a rehearsal job with a step named "release rehearsal" that runs `asf rehearse`
- [ ] the release and preview jobs each carry `needs: [rehearsal]`

- [ ] `release.DEFAULTS` carries ci_steps.rehearsal as "release rehearsal", `release_preview.STEPS` carries the same key, and `asf/env.py` needs no change

- [ ] the continue-on-error flag leaves the rehearsal step in the last Task, and after that a red rehearsal job means no tag is cut and no changelog entry is filed for that sha
