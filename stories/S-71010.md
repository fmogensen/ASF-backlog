## Acceptance
- [ ] the `run_turns_by_kind` budget convention exists with an empty default, readable through `budget_for` without a KeyError — proven by tests/test_conventions.py
- [ ] a `close` job at 61 turns is over its own limit, is stopped, and its log last record is `budget.run_cap_result` for kind `close` with that limit
- [ ] the same 61 turns on a `spec` job is not over
- [ ] `runtime.failure_reason` reads that record back as `budget.RUN_CAP`
- [ ] the per-kind lookup is keyed off the row kind written on the session record, never the metrics stream kind, so `close` and `groom-clerk` are not both read as `code`
- [ ] a product `budget.run_turns` of 900 raises every kind the per-kind table does not name and leaves `close` at its own limit
- [ ] a per-kind entry overrides the product `run_turns` for that kind alone, and a measure set to `off` caps nothing
- [ ] the `capped` mark the stall step writes carries the limit the run actually hit
