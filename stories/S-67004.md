## Acceptance
- [ ] `steps.STEPS` ends with `watch` and `ASF_CALLABLES['watch']` is `asf.tick.step_watch:run`
- [ ] `resolve` on a product declaring no `steps:` gives `watch` owner `asf`, and no product yaml is refused for not naming it
- [ ] `asf tick --manifest` lists `watch` with its callable `asf.tick.step_watch:run`
- [ ] `steps: {watch: off}` is not refused and the tick prints `tick: step watch off`
- [ ] `asf tick --steps watch` runs the step alone and prints one line per part — `tables`, `watch`, `probe` — each `ok` or `FAILED`
- [ ] A part that fails never stops the parts after it, and the step then raises `StepFailed` naming every failed part
- [ ] The step reuses `step_daily.run_part` rather than a second copy of it
