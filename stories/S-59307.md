## Acceptance
- [ ] `unhonoured` yields no row for a sequence the card names no number in even though the branch has booked one in it: that sequence is open and the coder is free to pick
- [ ] A card whose `writes:` names a bare directory glob books no number at all, which is what makes that escape hatch the card's own words — proven by tests/test_reservations.py
- [ ] `unhonoured` yields no row for a sequence the card reserves a number in that the branch has booked nothing in: a card whose migration comes later is clean on its first commit
- [ ] `asf reservations --check` for a product that declares no sequence prints `reservations: no sequence declared — nothing to check` and exits 0
- [ ] A brief for a product that declares no sequence carries neither the reservation line nor the standing rule, and every golden brief fixture is byte-identical
- [ ] A product `reservations` command that exits non-zero yields no bookings and a reason, never a refusal — proven by tests/test_reservations.py
- [ ] `asf reservations --check` prints one `warning:` line and still exits 0 when the product's own `reservations` command is broken
