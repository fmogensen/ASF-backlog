## Acceptance
- [ ] [ ] the product file's capacity block gains a floor key, read only by the capacity module and documented in it
- [ ] [ ] the floor is not registered in the checked capacity fields, and is named among the documented unchecked fields so the doctor does not call it unknown
- [ ] [ ] a product whose arithmetic share is below its floor resolves to its floor while its own demand record says it wants work
- [ ] [ ] the same product resolves to its share while its demand record wants nothing and it holds no session
- [ ] [ ] a product holding live sessions counts as wanting work whatever its record says
- [ ] [ ] the floor never raises the number above the product's own ceiling
- [ ] [ ] the floor never raises the number above the operator total less the sessions in flight elsewhere
- [ ] [ ] the floor never raises the number above what the pool can take now
- [ ] [ ] a floor of zero and an unset floor both reproduce today's resolved number exactly
- [ ] [ ] a partner with no demand record, or one older than the freshness window, claims the seats it holds now, or its own floor when that is higher, and never its whole share
- [ ] [ ] a partner holding nothing with no fresh record lends its whole share, so an idle partner's seats are borrowable on the next tick that resolves
- [ ] [ ] a partner with a fresh record is claimed for exactly as it is today, its recorded in flight plus wanted against its live count
- [ ] [ ] a partner holding more sessions than its record said still claims the live count
- [ ] [ ] the resolved value gains the arithmetic share, the floor and whether the floor held, filled whenever a share was computed
- [ ] [ ] the four share fields that exist today keep their meaning exactly, so the capacity table and the wave read what they read now
- [ ] [ ] the kernel seat line names the share, the usable slots, the active products and the floor when it held
- [ ] [ ] the kernel status prints that line off the saved plan with no new read of its own
