## Acceptance
- [ ] A lead carrying `delivers:` at stage `card` gets one `DELIVERY → PLAN` row, brief kind `delivery-plan`, branch `plan/<lead>`, and one `WAITS ON delivery <lead>` row per other member. — proven by tests/test_feeder.py
- [ ] At stage `plan-approved` the lead gets one `DELIVERY → CODE` row, brief kind `delivery-code`, branch `worker/<lead>`. — proven by tests/test_feeder.py
- [ ] A member Bug that would otherwise be a `BUG → FIX` row and a member Feature that would otherwise be `CARD → SPEC` produce neither. — proven by tests/test_feeder.py
- [ ] The delivery row reads `WAITS ON T-0001` when a running Task writes a file in the union footprint, and a Task writing that file reads `WAITS ON <lead>` while the delivery runs. — proven by tests/test_feeder.py
- [ ] A delivery whose every member is Closed emits no row of either kind, and a busy lead emits no launching row while its members still show `WAITS ON delivery`. — proven by tests/test_feeder.py
- [ ] At capacity 1 the `DELIVERY → CODE` row is the launch and the `WAITS ON delivery` rows cost no slot. — proven by tests/test_feeder.py
