## Statement
Four methods are adopted as Features — same-session correction, reviews as check tables, failure classification before relaunch, and a frozen eval set — in that order. The prototype's runner is not adopted as the engine.

## Context
The prototype has shipped no product, its eval set covers one lever of four, and its own record holds over a hundred open findings. The methods are what carries; the engine is not. Our line already matched it on evidence over claims and on rules as checks, and differs on purpose on the constitution — cards and checks rather than a long prompt prefix.
