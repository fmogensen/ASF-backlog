## Statement
0.1 ships the CLI, the interfaces and the git/file backends only. Nothing in 0.1 may assume a single local operator: the operator store and the record are interfaces with one backend each, every operator action is a library call with typed input and output, money and safety are keyed per product, and the front door is a conversation that ends in cards.

## Context
A hosted 'an idea in, a working product out' service is a possible future. Designing for it now costs five constraints in the spec; retrofitting it later costs a rewrite. It is not built now.
