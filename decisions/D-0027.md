## Statement
Once the production verification probe runs after each deploy, the operator's sign-off remains the closing gate only for customer-facing Features — the ones under the product's customer-facing Epics. Everything else closes on probe evidence alone. Until the probe runs, the sign-off is required everywhere.

## Context
The sign-off is the operator's scarcest resource, and spending it on internal work buys nothing the probe cannot prove. Ingest's Feature-closed rule gains the Epic test, and a rule card records it.
