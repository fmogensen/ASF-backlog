## Statement
The factory Epic of the first product's backlog and its Features and Bugs, the core factory rules, and the factory's design decisions move here. Product-specific rules and decisions stay where they are. A card that cannot be made product-agnostic is a product card and does not move.

## Context
The factory's own roadmap was living inside a product's record, where it competed for rank with the product's features and could not be read by anyone else. The split is by whether the statement is true of any factory, not by who wrote it.
