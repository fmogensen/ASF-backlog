## Statement
A product may declare a named class of work an explicit exception to its default priority rule, stated as a Decision and applied to every card in that class. The exception is by name and recorded, never by case-by-case judgement.

## Context
A default priority rule — such as parity with a reference product meaning a feature is needed — will eventually be wrong for a whole class of work. Carving the class out as one recorded exception keeps the rule mechanical and the exception auditable.
