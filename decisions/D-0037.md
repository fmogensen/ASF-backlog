## Statement
A credential that leaks into a local transcript is not rotated on the spot. Every key is rotated in one step before the public release — the repository going public, or the first outside users — not before an internal milestone. A key is still never printed.

## Context
Rotating on the spot costs a broken lane for every leak and buys nothing while the only readers are the operator's own machines. The rotation is a dated, single, checkable step instead.
