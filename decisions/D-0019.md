## Statement
This record numbers from the start. A migrated card keeps its old id as `legacy_id` and carries no link back to the private record it came from. A reference to another product's item is written `<product>:F-0019`, never as a bare id.

## Context
The source record is private, so a public back-link would leak its existence and its numbering. The controller marks the old cards as moved on the private side, from the map this seed produced.
