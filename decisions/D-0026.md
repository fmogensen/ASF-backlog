## Statement
Three parts. (1) Two live items on one subject must be linked by supersession; the newer wins, the older stays as history and leaves the enforced set. (2) A nightly pass pairs same-subject rules and decisions — by statement-token overlap, or by the same scope plus a shared named object — and lists the pairs in the groom; nothing changes until the operator answers keep A, keep B, both or merge. (3) Precedence for a surviving contradiction: a human decision outranks any rule, a newer decision outranks an older, and otherwise the lower number wins. The rule runner reports two contradicting enforced checks as a defect of the register, not of the factory.

## Context
With a hundred and more rules and decisions, conflicts are a certainty and finding them by reading is not. Code proposes the pairs; a human rules on them.
