## Statement
ASF is measured on the products it actually runs: features on production per week, cost per feature, defects per release, per product and as a total. A change to ASF is measured on a live product within a day, or it is not adopted.

## Context
A factory that proves itself on a sample repository proves nothing. The scorecard a customer would pay for is the same scorecard the factory improves itself with.
