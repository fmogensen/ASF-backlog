## Statement
The factory exists to develop any product faster. Its tools, its scripts, its accounts, its runners and its review standard are company-level and serve the next product too; only product artefacts — the product repository, its hosting projects, its DNS, its secrets, its documentation — carry that product's name. The factory reads which product it is operating on from a per-product configuration file outside any repository; no script carries a product path literal.

## Context
A rule that says no product name may appear applies to the product's visitor-facing and asset names, never to the factory. It was once over-applied and the factory's own files were renamed after a product and then reverted.
