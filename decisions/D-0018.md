## Statement
ASF ships a `CONSTITUTION.md`: ten directives adapted in our words with our evidence, each mapped to the core rule cards that enforce it, and a TRIGGERS line saying whether it is checked, hooked or only recited. It is a document people read and cards enforce — not a prompt prefix every session pays for on every call.

## Context
The prototype prepended its constitution to every model call, so every byte was paid on every call, and its own coverage table admitted that several directives had no mechanism at all. The table is the part worth copying: a directive that is only recited is decoration.
