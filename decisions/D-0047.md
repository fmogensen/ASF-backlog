## Decision
The factory decides that work is done by exactly two kinds of evidence: **a named test's result** on
the trunk (the acceptance line's test exists and is green at or after the change), or **a typed
ruling with its evidence** (`closed_by: <sha>` written by the operator through the groom). Nothing
else closes an item — not a commit subject that resembles a title, not a session's report that the
fix "is already there", not a heuristic match. A commit naming an id is linkage (cost, trail),
never proof.

Hand-carried fixes to make ASF itself work are accepted while the lane is being built (D-0046);
they are marked as bridges in the cards they touch and retire when the general mechanism lands.
Inside ASF, a mechanism that rests on a model's judgment or on coincidence is a defect.

## Why
A typed status lied; a matched title lies the same way, only faster. Loops of re-doing finished
work come from closure that depends on someone noticing. Tests and rulings are the two things that
cannot be argued with after the fact.
