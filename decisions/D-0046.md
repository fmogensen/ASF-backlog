## Decision
ASF fixes its own bugs through its own lane. A defect in ASF is filed as a Bug card and reaches a session through `asf tick` (the S1 lane: `BUG → FIX` on `fix/<id>`, the card's `## Fix` as the plan, the named test as acceptance, PR, harvest). Nobody — operator, controller session or worker — patches ASF by hand for a bug that the lane can carry.

The one exception, until it is closed: **when the lane itself is what is broken**, the controller may build the fix directly (a worker job in a worktree, tests, commit to main) — and only for the defects that stand between ASF and the lane working. On 2026-09-21 that set is B-0020 (evidence blind on any product but the first) and the generic-conventions Feature (code carries the first product's conventions). Once one `fix-bug` session has gone card → branch → PR → harvest on the `asf` product, the exception closes and every further inability of ASF to fix itself is filed as a Bug for the lane.

## Why
The point of the factory is that the record drives the work. A hand fix leaves no card, no cost, no evidence and no lesson, and it trains the humans to bypass the lane exactly when it matters most.
