## Statement
The usage tool shows one reset column, and for an account whose short window is empty that column counts down to the next short-window boundary, not to the long-window reset. Capacity planning therefore uses the long-window percentage alone: an account above the guard is out until that percentage actually drops, whatever the timer says. The per-account hourly launch cap applies to local jobs too, because they burn the same window.

## Context
Two accounts showed a high long-window percentage with a one-hour reset; at the hour the timer flipped to a day and a half and the percentage did not move. Four briefs had been queued for that reset.
