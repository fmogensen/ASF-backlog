## Decision

Given by the operator's console 2026-09-27, after a product's last 24 h showed adjudicate as 100 of
its 245 repair sessions (41%), all on the heavy model.

1. **Adjudicate later.** A held item escalates to ADJUDICATE only after CORRECT has failed twice on
   the **same finding** — the same hold kind with the same keys: a review's C-list files (the same
   finding while one C-item survives), the same red check set, the same conflicting files, else the
   hold's first line with shas and numbers blanked. A new, different finding resets the item to
   CORRECT, whatever its round count. Replaces "three rounds of any kind" (B-0048's cap on mixed
   holds).
2. **Adjudicate cheaper.** An adjudicate brief runs on `light` for every item class but S1; heavy
   stays for S1.

Unchanged: naming and copies holds never reach adjudicate; footprint holds go to widen_footprint;
the same-head loop guard parks as before.

## Evidence

A replay of that product's session ledger (last 24 h) under the new rule: 77 of the 100 adjudicate
launches sat on a mixed-finding history and would have stayed CORRECT; 23 met the same-finding bar.

## Consequences

- `asf.workers.lifecycle`: `finding_of`, `same_finding`, `repeats`, `next_finding`; each hold
  carries `finding` and `same`; `at_cap` marks the third hold in a row on one finding.
- `asf.feeder.rows.correction_rows` decides STALEMATE → ADJUDICATE on `same`, not `rounds`.
- `asf.briefs.build.MODEL_TABLE['adjudicate']`: default light, S1 heavy.
- Shipped in fmogensen/ASF PR #53.
