## Statement
A closed Story whose scope implies more than its cited tests prove is split: the proven acceptance lines stay on the closed Story, and every unproven line moves to a new Story under the same Feature, titled for the missing behaviour and carrying a split-from reference and a backlink. A closed Story is never reopened for a missing test.

## Context
The count of done work stays honest because only proven behaviour counts as done. The alternatives — reopening, which makes the count collapse, or noting the gap, which makes it not work — both cost the record its meaning.
