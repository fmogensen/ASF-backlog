## Statement
The production verification probe signs in as a real customer: an alias on an operator mailbox receives the emailed sign-in code and the runner reads it through the mail provider's read-only API, with the refresh token in the CI secrets. No probe bypass route is opened on production. The probe's account is named so every audit row and every mail says so, and its plan is the trial tier. Journeys keep refusing money, mail and delete steps at load.

## Context
Two options were weighed: a sign-in bypass route on production behind an opt-in, which the route refuses by design; and a real account through a mailbox, which was chosen. The identity, the mailbox and the host are all per-product configuration.
