## Decision

A failure of a **worker's work** goes straight back to the session that produced it and is fixed and re-run at once — never a new card, never a wait for the next tick.

- Red tests in the job, a red harvest gate on its branch, a session ending in error: the tick **resumes that session** (`--resume <session_id>` from its log) with the failing output appended, in the same step, and re-gates. Up to three rounds. After the third, one `ADJUDICATE` row on the item — not a Bug.
- The harvest step stops filing Bugs on a red gate; the red gate is the correction's input.
- Bug cards are for **defects in ASF's own code** — a wrong reader, a step that offers landed branches to `gh`. Those have no session to go back to and need a change to ASF. Every other failure is a round on the session.

## Why

Frank, 2026-09-21 20:55: "failures should send it back to be immediately fixed and run again without waiting for next tick or creating a new bugfix … number of fixes will just grow and grow that way." A card per failed branch turns every hiccup into backlog; the session that has the context is the cheapest fixer and the fastest.

## Consequences

- B-0032 is reshaped into this loop (was: make the one-shot correction non-blocking).
- `file-bugs` from harvest is removed; `correct_once` becomes `correct(rounds=3)` with resume.
- Metric: rounds per landed branch, on the scorecard, beside USD.
