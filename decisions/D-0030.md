## Statement
A hosted end-to-end walk signs in without an inbox: the product's own command line mints a login link against the target database, a headless browser signs in from it and saves the session state, and later runs resume from that state.

## Context
Walks stalled waiting for a person to relay sign-in mails. The first mint was refused by the permission classifier as credential materialisation and ran after an explicit instruction — which is the approval matrix's job, not a judgement call per run.
