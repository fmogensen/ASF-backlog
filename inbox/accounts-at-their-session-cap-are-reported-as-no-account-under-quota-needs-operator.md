# Accounts at their session cap are reported as 'no account under quota — NEEDS OPERATOR'

type: bug
severity: S2

At 10:53 on 2026-09-25, botseon's wave printed "waits review-b-1372 B-1372 — NEEDS OPERATOR: no account under quota — wait for a window to reset, or add an account under worker_pool". Both usable accounts were well under quota (about 8-10% of 5h, 24-28% of 7d). They were full, not out of quota: asf held 7 sessions across them (cap 4 each), partly on slots borrowed while botseon was idle. Two defects: (1) the reason is wrong: an account at its session cap is not "over quota", and the line should say "all usable accounts at cap (7/8, asf holds 7)"; (2) it's a NEEDS OPERATOR line for something that clears by itself when a session ends, and questions to the operator are a bug. Want: a distinct "waits — accounts at cap" reason with no NEEDS OPERATOR, reserving that for when every account is truly over its quota guard. Also, once a partner has demand again, a product running on borrowed slots gets its next free slot handed back to the partner first (the loan is repaid before it launches more). Tests: accounts at cap under quota yield the cap reason, not NEEDS OPERATOR; after a loan, the next freed slot goes to the lender.
