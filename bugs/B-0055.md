## Symptom

Every CI run on `main` since 8c25fa7 is red (seven in a row on 2026-09-22, the operator gets a failure mail per landing) while the suite is green on the operator's machine and in the workflow's plain `tests` step. The red step is the last one, "the suite is hermetic (env -i, an operator home, ASF_PRODUCT set, foreign default branch)": 21 errors, every one `asf.env.ConfigError: no product config at /tmp/asf-tests-home-*/products/asf.yaml`, in `test_ingest`, `test_migrate` and `test_evidence`.

## Cause

The step exports `ASF_PRODUCT=asf` the way the tick does. `tests/test_00_home.py` re-homes the suite to a fresh temporary `ASF_HOME` (B-0043) but leaves the caller's identity — `ASF_PRODUCT`, `ASF_JOB`, `BACKLOG_ID_RANGE` — in the environment. A test that runs a record command with no `--product` then resolves `$ASF_PRODUCT` (`asf.env.product_name`) and looks for `asf.yaml` in a home that has no products at all. The suite reads the caller's identity exactly as it once read the caller's home.

## Fix

`tests/test_00_home.hermetic_home()` drops `asf.hermetic.CALLER_IDENTITY` from `os.environ` beside re-homing: the suite's identity is its own, a test that needs a product names one. Failing-first test: `tests/test_00_home.py::HomeIsHermetic::test_the_suite_never_inherits_the_callers_identity` — red with `ASF_PRODUCT=asf` exported, green after.
