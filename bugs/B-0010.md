## Description
On 2026-09-21 a harvest reaped a worktree while that job was still running, taking the checkout away from a live session. Nothing in the reap step checked that the job had ended.

## Fix
Reaping requires both: the job's own record carries a finished line, and the process id in it is dead. The reap step refuses otherwise and prints one hold line naming the job. A test writes a record with no finished line, and one with a live process id, and asserts the worktree, the branch and the registry row all survive a harvest.
