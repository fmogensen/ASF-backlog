## Symptom

Three CI runs fired at 15:21 on `archive/fix/B-0016`, `archive/fix/B-0025` and `archive/fix/B-0043` — two red — and the operator got the mails. `main` was green throughout; nothing was pushed to those branches by a session.

## Cause

`archive_superseded` (B-0057, B-0065) pushed the superseded branch's tip as-is under `archive/<branch>`. A push runs the workflow file of the pushed commit, and those branches were cut before fix(B-0053): their `.github/workflows/tests.yml` still says `on: push` for every ref. Archiving re-fired the stale workflow B-0054 had just stopped.

## Fix

The archive ref's tip is one empty commit over the branch's own tip — `archive(<item>): <branch> — <item> is <state> in the record; superseded, kept for reference [skip ci]` (`git commit-tree`, no worktree) — so the push's head commit carries the marker the CI provider reads and no run fires; the branch's history sits untouched underneath. Test: `tests/test_harvest.py::ProductHarvestTests::test_b0057_a_closed_bugs_branch_is_archived_not_held` asserts the tip's subject, its `[skip ci]`, the untouched parent and an empty diff between them.
