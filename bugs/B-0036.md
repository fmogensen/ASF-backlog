## Symptom

The scheduler runs the product's `repo_dir` checkout (installed editable, so its working tree is the code). Harvest landed `fix/B-0010` on `origin/main` at 21:51, and the checkout stayed at the commit before it: every following tick ran the factory from before its own fix. Nothing ever fast-forwards that checkout — a hand `git pull` did, on every landing, or the fix was landed and never in effect.

## Cause

`asf.harvest.harvest.run_product_harvest` pushes `<sha>:refs/heads/<main>` to origin and deletes the branch, but touches only the throwaway worktree and origin. The checkout it fetches in (`repo_dir`) keeps its own `main` where it was.

## Fix

After a landing, when the checkout is on the trunk with a clean working tree, `git merge --ff-only origin/<main>` in it (one line `harvest: <repo_dir> fast-forwarded to <sha>`); a checkout on another branch, or with local changes, is left alone and named in one line. Never a reset, never a force. Test: a landing through `run_product_harvest` leaves the checkout's `main` at the landed sha; a dirty checkout is untouched.
