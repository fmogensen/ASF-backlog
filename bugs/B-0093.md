`asf status` shows "Runners — (not configured: ci.runner_org)" and "Prod — (not configured: ci.deploy_workflow)" for a product where neither can exist. ASF's CI runs on GitHub-hosted runners (.github/workflows/tests.yml), and ASF deploys nothing: its trunk is production (B-0077), and releases are tags since 626fffe. The rows read as missing setup and invite the operator to configure something that makes no sense for this product.

Expected: Runners says "hosted (GitHub)" when no runner_org is set and the CI provider uses hosted runners, and shows the latest CI run's result on trunk. Prod, for a product without a deploy workflow, shows the newest release (the tag, its sha, its age, and "trunk is prod"). "not configured" is kept only for a setting the product actually needs.
