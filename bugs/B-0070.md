## Symptom

Twice today the controller filed a card and landed its fix a few minutes later; in the tick between, the lane launched its own session for the same card (B-0059, B-0064 — about $2.9 each). Both branches were superseded and archived.

## Cause

A card is emitted the moment it is `decided` and open; nothing says "a fix for this is already in flight outside the lane". The controller's direct fixes (D-0046's exception) are invisible to the feeder until they land.

## Fix

`in_progress_by:` typed on the card (`operator` or a session id) suppresses rows for it; `asf new` takes `--in-progress` and the controller's emergency path sets it; the landing clears it (the ingest sees the commit naming the id). A card with `in_progress_by: operator` older than `stage_limits.operator_hours` (default 2) is named in the groom as stale.

## Tests

`test_a_card_in_progress_by_the_operator_emits_no_row`; `test_the_landing_clears_in_progress`.
