## Description
The wave step writes each launch event to `metrics/events` with `account: <name>` — the operator's worker-account names — and the record is public. Two lines reached the public record on 2026-09-21 17:22Z (scrubbed forward in the next commit; the history rewrite needs the operator). Names of accounts, hosts, people and vendors are operator data: they belong in `~/.ASF`, never in a record.

## Fix
Every record-bound line (events, sessions metrics, cost, releases) refers to an account by its **index in `worker_pool.accounts`** (`account: a3`) or a stable hash, never its name; the sessions registry under `~/.ASF/state` keeps the name. `check` gains a redaction test: no value in `metrics/**` matches an account name from the operator config (the check reads `~/.ASF/config.yaml` when present). Tests: a fixture config with two accounts; a launch event carries `a1`; `check` fails on a planted name.
