## Description
The no-first-beat judge ends sessions that are working. Since the heartbeat came back on 2026-10-13 18:30, ten build sessions were ended with "settle: no beat to land (no first beat after 21m (past the grace))" while their session logs were still growing (128 to 942 lines each, still writing at the moment they were ended). Both lanes are affected: three were local sessions and seven cloud. B-205886 (PR #1715) was meant to stop judging a lane that cannot beat; the cloud sessions are still judged, and local sessions that do run are not seen to beat. Items lose a round each time, then read "session ran past its max age" or go Stuck (T-210358 twice, T-217369 and T-217370 twice each). The console switched `kernel.heartbeat.enabled` off as a stopgap on 2026-10-14 07:40.

## Acceptance
- [ ] A local session that is running and writing its session log is never ended for no first beat; the beat it writes is the one the judge reads (same path, same session id), proved end to end with a real local launch in a test.
- [ ] A cloud session is never ended for no first beat unless the cloud lane is in `kernel.heartbeat.lanes` and its beat source is read.
- [ ] Before ending a session for no beat, the judge checks the session's own log for activity within the grace, and keeps a session that is active.
- [ ] A session ended for no beat does not charge the item a round.
