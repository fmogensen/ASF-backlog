## Symptom

The first scheduled full-lane tick (2026-09-21 20:28) printed `harvest: none to land` while `origin/fix/B-0024` (`af619a2`) and `origin/fix/B-0026` (`d01bbaa`) sat finished on the product repo. Their registry lines read `ended … end_reason: dead pid`, then `corrected: 1` — and stayed there, although both job logs end in a `type: result, subtype: success` line.

## Cause

Two gaps in `asf.workers.health` / `asf.tick.step_health`:

1. `health()` skips every session that already has `ended`. A session judged `dead pid` whose log gains a result line afterwards (the result and the exit race the health check; a corrected run appends a new result) is never re-judged.
2. `handle_dead()` calls `correct_once()`, and on success records only `corrected: 1`. The `end_reason` written by the dead-pid judgement is never replaced, so `harvest.is_eligible()` (which wants `rc: 0` or `end_reason: finished`) refuses the branch forever.

## Fix

- `health()`: a session with `end_reason: dead pid` and no `harvested` mark is re-read; when its log now ends in a result, it becomes `finished` or `failed` (one `re-judged` line).
- `handle_dead()`: after `correct_once` returns, record the outcome on the session — `end_reason: finished, rc: 0` on ok, `end_reason: failed, rc: 1` otherwise.
- Failing-first tests for both in `tests/test_workers.py` (fake runtime, a log that gains its result after the first health pass).

## Evidence

`~/.ASF/state/asf/sessions.jsonl` lines for `fix-bug-b-0024` / `fix-bug-b-0026`; `~/.ASF/logs/tick-asf-record-health-wave-prs-harvest.log` first run.
