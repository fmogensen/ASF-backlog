## Description
A PR check that is also failing on the trunk is charged to the branch. botseon's `p1-e2e` / `p1-e2e-b` jobs are flaky or broken on main: the last 6 main runs alternate red and green on the same sha (9da53dd02 red at 00:42 and 07:46, beae5cb19 green, 01298cbe2 and b6ec40036 red). Yet the lane holds each PR red on p1-e2e as "checks red — back to its session", then STALEMATE → ADJUDICATE: B-1372's #773 and T-0140's #760 each burned correction and adjudication rounds (about $2-3 each) on a failure that was not theirs. Want, as a deterministic rule before any correction or adjudication: for each red required job on the PR, look at that job's recent results on the trunk (the last N runs, from the CI provider). If the job is red or flaky on the trunk (red on ≥1 of the last N, or red then green on one sha), mark the check `trunk-red` and re-run it or wait (bounded) instead of sending the branch back. File (or bump) one Bug per trunk-red job, citing the runs. The hold line says so: "held <branch>: p1-e2e red on trunk too (3 of last 6) — re-gating, not the branch's fault". Tests: a PR red only on a job that is red on the trunk is re-gated and triggers no correction row; a job green on the trunk still sends the branch back.
