## Symptom

The controller blocked eleven held items on B-0054 (`blockedBy: [B-0054]`) so the lane would stop launching sessions against their branches. The next tick launched `adjudicate-b-0016` on `fix/B-0016` anyway (an Opus session, stopped by hand before it committed another invented ruling). `asf next` showed only `BUG → FIX B-0054`, so the board said one thing and the wave did another.

## Cause

`asf.feeder.rows` reads the derived `blocked` flag for Features (`feature_rows`) and New Tasks, but neither `bug_rows` nor `correction_rows` does: a blocked Bug still gets its FIX row, and a blocked item with a pending correction still gets its CORRECT or ADJUDICATE row.

## Fix

`bug_rows` and `correction_rows` skip an item whose `blocked` is set, exactly as `feature_rows` does. Test: `tests/test_feeder.py::CorrectionRowTest::test_b0058_a_blocked_item_gets_no_correct_or_adjudicate_row` — a blocked S1 Bug with a pending correction at round 1 and at round 3 yields no row at all.
