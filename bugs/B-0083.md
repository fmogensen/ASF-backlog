## Description

At 12:51 a typed field's comment was written across two lines in `tasks/T-0042.md`. The
frontmatter parser cannot read a continuation line, so `asf check` refused the card and the
**record step failed on every tick from then until 13:20** — thirty minutes.

What the tick did with a broken record: nothing stopped. The `wave` step kept reading the last
good `index.json`, which still called T-0034, T-0037, T-0040, T-0047 and T-0048 `New` although
all five had landed on the trunk, and it launched **five Opus adjudication sessions on merged
work**. They were killed by hand. Three tasks and one Feature's progress were invisible on the
board for half an hour.

A failing record step is not one red line among six. It is the factory losing its sense of what
is true, and every step after it acts on a stale board with full confidence.

## Acceptance

- A failed `record` step stops the tick: `wave` does not launch, `harvest` does not land, and the
  tick prints one line naming the file and the parse error as the reason nothing else ran.
- The tick's first table says `RECORD STALE — <reason>` whenever the index it is reading was not
  written by this tick.
- `asf check` failing on a card names the card, the line and the rule — it already does — and the
  tick surfaces that line rather than burying it in the step's output.
- A test: a record with an unparseable card makes `tick` exit non-zero with no session launched.
