## Description
`asf new` accepts only `--title`, `--parent`, `--priority`, `--area`, `--legacy-id` and `--body-file`. A card in this schema also carries `rank`, `decided`, `source`, `severity`, `found_in`, `signature`, `scope`, `enforced`, `reason`, `check`, `decided_by`, `date`, `blockedBy` and `links`. Every one of the cards in this record was minted with `new` and then re-rendered by a script, which means the one tool that is supposed to own card creation owns only the id.

## Fix
`new` takes every typed field of the schema, or takes them as `--set key=value` pairs validated against the type's field list, so a card is complete when `new` returns.
