## Symptom

26 worktrees under `state/<p>/worktrees/` on the morning of 2026-09-22, 16 of them for branches harvest landed hours ago; health prints `keep fix-bug-b-0045 ended: branch not pushed` (or `not in origin/main`) for every one, every tick. `reaped` appears 10 times in the whole log. A later relaunch of an item is then refused with `worktree already exists` (B-0039 at 06:45).

## Cause

`asf/workers/health.py` reaps only when `pushed(worktree, branch)` holds and the tip is in `origin/<trunk>`. Harvest (B-0027) rebases the branch onto the trunk — a new sha — pushes that, and deletes `origin/<branch>`. Afterwards the worktree's own tip is neither on a remote branch nor an ancestor of the trunk, so both tests fail forever. The landing is recorded elsewhere: `harvest.mark_session(...)` writes `harvested: <sha>` on the session.

## Fix

- `health`: a session with `harvested` set is reapable regardless of the worktree's tip (the landing is the evidence); remove the worktree and the local branch. Keep the existing rule for sessions without `harvested`.
- A session `stopped by operator` (or any ended session) whose worktree has **no commits ahead of origin/<trunk> and no uncommitted changes** is reapable (B-0025's rule; land it here if B-0025 stays held).
- Print one `reaped <job> (landed <sha>)` / `reaped <job> (empty)` line per removal.
- Footprint: `asf/workers/health.py`, `tests/test_workers.py`.
