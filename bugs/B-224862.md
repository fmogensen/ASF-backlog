## Description
A reviewer fix on a high-risk path is attempted, rejected and charged. Under kernel.review.fix: on (PR #1742) the kernel rejects a fix that touches a kernel.risk.high path, records the verdict as changes and charges a normal round. For this product kernel.risk.high covers the kernel's own directory, which most items write, so a fixing review that touches it wastes its work and pushes the item toward the fix-round cap: T-210362 went Stuck on the operator after its third round with "reviewer fix out of bounds (a high-risk path: asf/kernel/decide.py)".

## Acceptance
- [ ] The fixing review's brief lists the paths a fix may touch (the item's writes and test paths minus kernel.risk.high) and says to report findings on any other path instead of fixing them. — tests/kernel/test_reviewer_fixes.py::Brief.test_the_fix_paragraph_names_the_paths_a_fix_may_touch
- [ ] An item whose writes are all high-risk gets a review without fix authority. — tests/kernel/test_reviewer_fixes.py::Limits.test_an_item_whose_writes_are_all_high_risk_gets_no_fix_authority
- [ ] An out-of-bounds reviewer fix charges no round to the item; the next round carries the review's findings. — tests/kernel/test_decide.py::Rounds.test_an_out_of_bounds_reviewer_fix_charges_no_round
