## Description
From 03:00 to 10:20 on 2026-09-25 the factory host had no network: every tick failed at record with "Could not resolve host: github.com" and logged "RECORD STALE", and nothing launched for 7 hours. `asf status` meanwhile printed the last good snapshot unchanged every 5 minutes: "0 working, 3 dead", quota frozen, Ready 4. The only hint was "(exit 1)" in the Cron cell. Nothing said the table was 7 hours old or why. Want: when the last tick failed at record, or the snapshot is older than two clock periods, the status table's first row says so in words, e.g. "STALE since 03:01 — record failed: Could not resolve host: github.com (13 ticks)", and the per-tick digest (B-0087) carries the same line. A tick that fails for being offline should also say "offline" in its one-line reason instead of the raw git error. Tests: a failed-record tick makes status print the STALE row with the since-time and the count.
