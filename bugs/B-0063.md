## Symptom

Thirty-seven local branches sat in the scheduler's checkout (`git branch --list`) after the floor was cleaned: `fix/B-0002` … `fix/B-0026`, `spec/F-0001` … `spec/F-0087`, most of them fully on `main` (their tip an ancestor of `origin/main`, or every patch on it by `git cherry`), a few landed by a harvest that rebased the tip, two archived. No worktree held any of them. `asf workers health --fix` reaps worktrees and deletes a branch only together with its landed or empty worktree; a branch whose worktree went another way is never looked at.

## Cause

Health owns worktrees, not branches: the local branch is deleted only on the reap of a landed or empty worktree (B-0025, B-0049). A worktree removed by hand, or a branch cut and never given a worktree, leaves its branch behind for ever; `spawn` then refuses the next launch on it when it carries commits (B-0025's rule).

## Fix

`asf.workers.health.prune_branches`, run at the end of every health pass: for every local branch under a lane prefix that no worktree holds — its work on the trunk (every patch by `git cherry`, or every touched file identical to the trunk's), or its run landed or archived by the lane (`harvested` on the registry) — `pruned` with `--fix`, `stale` without; one with work the trunk lacks and no landing is named `stray` and kept, nothing is deleted unseen. Test: `tests/test_workers.py::TestHealth::test_b0063_stale_local_lane_branches_are_pruned_strays_named`.
