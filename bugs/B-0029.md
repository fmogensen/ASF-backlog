## Symptom

Every tick on the `asf` product prints `[step:prs] FAILED 2 PR(s) not opened: fix/B-0014, fix/B-0020` — `gh pr create` answers `No commits between main and fix/B-0014`. Both branches landed on main by fast-forward days ago and sit at the trunk head.

## Cause

`asf.tick.step_prs.candidates()` takes every finished session whose branch is pushed under a PR prefix. It does not read the product's `landing` convention (`asf` lands by `fast-forward`; a pull request is never the mechanism there), does not skip sessions already marked `harvested`, and does not check that the branch is ahead of the trunk. A branch at the trunk head is offered to `gh`, which refuses, and the step fails — on every tick, forever.

## Fix

- `step_prs.run`: when the product's `landing` is `fast-forward`, the step opens nothing (one line `prs: landing is fast-forward — harvest lands the branches`), runs hygiene only.
- `candidates()`: skip sessions with `harvested`, and skip a branch with zero commits ahead of `origin/<main>` (one `prs: <branch> at trunk — nothing to open` line, not a failure).
- Tests: a fast-forward product opens no PRs; a harvested or at-trunk branch is not a candidate; the step exits 0.
