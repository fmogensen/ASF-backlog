## Statement
The launch step is mechanical: the launcher re-reads the quota, turns every SPAWNING row into a brief through the brief generator, launches one launcher per account (at most 12 briefs each) and records every session. Hand-written briefs remain only for the operator's direct asks and for anything the generator refuses.

## Why
A launch decided by hand each tick is a launch that gets skipped when the tick is busy. The quota table in the reply shows the gate that actually stopped a row.

## Check
None yet. The source check for this rule sources a shared library bound to one product's repository, state directory, branch naming and scheduled job, so it cannot be carried as it stands. It becomes a check when the rule runner reads those four facts from the product's configuration.
