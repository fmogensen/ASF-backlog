## Statement
Every tick, before the capacity pass, runs the stall check --probe: local jobs whose pid is dead or whose log is silent past the limit, launcher results grepped for `not found|failed|error|denied|empty`, and cloud sessions with no push to their branch. One line in the runner log per stall found.

## Why
A launcher that fails silently costs a whole tick of capacity, and nobody notices until the work is missing hours later.

## Check
None yet. The source check for this rule sources a shared library bound to one product's repository, state directory, branch naming and scheduled job, so it cannot be carried as it stands. It becomes a check when the rule runner reads those four facts from the product's configuration.
