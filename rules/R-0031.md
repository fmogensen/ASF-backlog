## Statement
An approved PR is not a mergeable PR. Before it goes into a batch it is preflighted against current main — a PR whose head cannot merge cleanly is rebased or refused, not batched — and the preflight is re-run whenever main moves under it, a docs train being a move. A batch is built from the largest set that has passed the preflight, never by trial and error against the queue. **A HOLD is a dispatch:** the fixer brief goes out the same tick the hold is written, with the failing statement pasted in; "later" means never.

## Why
On 2026-09-18 fourteen approved PRs produced six refused batches and three landings; the batches sank on conflicts a preflight would have named. One of the six was red for exactly the cause it had been HELD for the night before, whose fixer was never dispatched. By 15:00 the count was eleven refused or red attempts before four landed — about five CI hours — and the number to report is batch attempts per landed batch.
