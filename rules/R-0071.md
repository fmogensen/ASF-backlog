## Statement
No launch goes to a worker account above 90 % of its 7-day window. In-flight jobs on such an account are expected to die and are relaunched elsewhere. Resets: a worker account Saturday, a worker account and a worker account Sunday, a worker account Wednesday, a worker account Thursday.

## Why
At 100 % the account's running jobs are cut, not queued — the work in them is gone.

## Check
None yet. The source check for this rule sources a shared library bound to one product's repository, state directory, branch naming and scheduled job, so it cannot be carried as it stands. It becomes a check when the rule runner reads those four facts from the product's configuration.
