## Statement
A running job past its budget (site 40 min, a slow job 35, e2e 30, gate-tests 25, everything else 20) is cancelled by the controller and the cause is named in that tick's report. A runner is never restarted without proof its job is dead.

## Why
A stalled job holds a box for an hour and nothing downstream is waiting on anything real.

## Check
None yet. The source check for this rule sources a shared library bound to one product's repository, state directory, branch naming and scheduled job, so it cannot be carried as it stands. It becomes a check when the rule runner reads those four facts from the product's configuration.
