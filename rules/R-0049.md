## Statement
A migration that joins or reads `pg_constraint`, `pg_class`, `pg_attribute`, `pg_index`, `pg_trigger` or `information_schema.*` also carries `current_schema()` — typically `to_regclass(current_schema() || '.<table>')` — or an explicit `pg_namespace` join, in the same statement or DO block.

## Why
Those are cluster-wide views with no schema of their own; under the parallel test harness's wide search_path, 0202 found a sibling schema's table, concluded it had already run, and left the column narrow.

## Check
None yet. The source check for this rule sources a shared library bound to one product's repository, state directory, branch naming and scheduled job, so it cannot be carried as it stands. It becomes a check when the rule runner reads those four facts from the product's configuration.
