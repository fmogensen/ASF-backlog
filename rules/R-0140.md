## Statement
The factory is not a goal. No new factory job — state-store steps, board or table polish, cutter
work — is started unless a product Feature is stuck on it and the fix is the shortest path. Ticks
and waves prioritise product rows; the `/asf:*` tables are maintained, not extended.

## Why
On the night of 2026-09-20 most sessions and most of the controller's time went to factory tooling
(state store, cutter, sessions table, heartbeat refs, the hosting provider noise). The process fixes were needed,
but they crowded out the product Epics. The measure is features released, and factory work is only
valuable when a product Feature is blocked by it. GOAL 9 — FACTORY was removed from `goals.txt`;
its rules line stayed as a footer, and the Epic (an Epic) stays for the work already under way.

## Check
None yet. The source check for this rule sources a shared library bound to one product's repository, state directory, branch naming and scheduled job, so it cannot be carried as it stands. It becomes a check when the rule runner reads those four facts from the product's configuration.
