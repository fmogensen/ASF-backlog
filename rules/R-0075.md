## Statement
The stall line is 45 minutes, not three hours. A session 45 minutes past its launch or past its last push with no push is probed now; a probe answers in about three minutes. A session the probe shows finished, failed or idle without a push — or that the probe cannot find — is relaunched immediately.

## Why
Three hours of silence is three hours of a slot spent on nothing, and the session is usually already dead.

## Check
None yet. The source check for this rule sources a shared library bound to one product's repository, state directory, branch naming and scheduled job, so it cannot be carried as it stands. It becomes a check when the rule runner reads those four facts from the product's configuration.
