## Statement
A brief or a review report goes in the reviewed PR's body, or under the review inputs directory on the branch under review, or into the archive directory on the main branch on main — never on a branch of its own. Where transport branches exist anyway they carry a TTL: `input/*` seven days, `review/*` fourteen. a worker branch is a deliverable and is deleted at merge.

## Why
Review inputs and outputs travelled as branches with no lifecycle at all, and most names carried no hint of which they were.

## Check
None yet. The source check for this rule sources a shared library bound to one product's repository, state directory, branch naming and scheduled job, so it cannot be carried as it stands. It becomes a check when the rule runner reads those four facts from the product's configuration.
