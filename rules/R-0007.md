## Statement
the scheduled capacity job runs the scheduled capacity job — the launcher 12 for agents and the batcher 4 for CI — every ten minutes, under its own locks, logging to `factory-cron.log`. The tick still pastes the tables and runs factory-health, but it no longer needs to launch by hand.

## Why
Capacity that only moves when a human ticks is capacity that stops when the human is asleep.

## Check
None yet. The source check for this rule sources a shared library bound to one product's repository, state directory, branch naming and scheduled job, so it cannot be carried as it stands. It becomes a check when the rule runner reads those four facts from the product's configuration.
