## Statement
Every review brief names the document that is authoritative and says that summaries of it are not
binding, plus the standing instruction to overrule a finding that is wrong, with evidence, rather
than obey it.

## Why
On 2026-09-05/06 the milestone wave-1 reviewer read a stale sentence in the decisions index and
reported five decision-row citations as collisions. One (row `a recorded decision` of the product register) was
real. That cost a full fix round
plus the controller's verification time. One line in the brief prevents it.

## Check
None yet. The source check for this rule sources a shared library bound to one product's repository, state directory, branch naming and scheduled job, so it cannot be carried as it stands. It becomes a check when the rule runner reads those four facts from the product's configuration.
