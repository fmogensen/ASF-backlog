## Statement
A coder brief names the plan by path and the task by its anchor and pastes only what the coder cannot cheaply re-derive: the task heading, its Files/Interfaces/Tables preamble, the gate commands its steps name, and the plan's Global Constraints. Step bodies are never pasted. the task brief generator is the tool; prose stays under 6,000 characters.

## Why
A pasted task body drifts the moment the plan is revised — the coder works from a frozen copy while the plan underneath it moves. The mean legacy brief was 19,900 characters.

## Check
None yet. The source check for this rule sources a shared library bound to one product's repository, state directory, branch naming and scheduled job, so it cannot be carried as it stands. It becomes a check when the rule runner reads those four facts from the product's configuration.
