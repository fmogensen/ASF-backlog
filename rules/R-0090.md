## Statement
A headless job can read only its own worktree and the directories it was granted. Anything it needs — a screenshot, a brief, a ledger — is copied in, not referenced by a path it cannot reach.

## Why
Briefs have pointed jobs at files outside their fence, and the job spent its session failing to open them.

## Check
None yet. The source check for this rule sources a shared library bound to one product's repository, state directory, branch naming and scheduled job, so it cannot be carried as it stands. It becomes a check when the rule runner reads those four facts from the product's configuration.
