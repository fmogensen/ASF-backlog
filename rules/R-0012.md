## Statement
Anything that reduces errors, time or fixes is done without asking. Money, production, security, customer data, legal and new features need the operator. Every dispatch made under the standing authority is logged.

## Why
Asking about every small improvement costs more of the operator's attention than the improvements save.

## Check
None yet. The source check for this rule sources a shared library bound to one product's repository, state directory, branch naming and scheduled job, so it cannot be carried as it stands. It becomes a check when the rule runner reads those four facts from the product's configuration.
