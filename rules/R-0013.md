## Statement
Only the central controller session dispatches work, merges, or cuts batches. A side session answers its own question and stops; it never launches, merges or revises unprompted.

## Why
Two sessions orchestrating means two sessions dispatching the same task to two accounts, and neither one knows the other did.

## Check
None yet. The source check for this rule sources a shared library bound to one product's repository, state directory, branch naming and scheduled job, so it cannot be carried as it stands. It becomes a check when the rule runner reads those four facts from the product's configuration.
