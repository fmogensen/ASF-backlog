## Statement
A review's findings are one table — check, severity, `file:line`, expected — with at most one paragraph per Critical or Important finding. A review that narrates the diff clause by clause has spent its length on the one thing the PR already shows.

## Why
102 pre-rule review files averaged 13,600 characters each, most of it the diff again.

## Check
None yet. The source check for this rule sources a shared library bound to one product's repository, state directory, branch naming and scheduled job, so it cannot be carried as it stands. It becomes a check when the rule runner reads those four facts from the product's configuration.
