## Description
The stage after a working factory. Humans decide what changes to the amendable set are approved, and still own Epics and ranks. The factory decides everything 0.1 gave it, and in addition adopts a change to a brief, a rule, a hook, a role or an eval when the frozen eval set says it beats the incumbent; spec and plan approval move to a panel with a check table, and a human is asked only on a split.

Gate into this stage: three products live on 0.1, the frozen eval set covers every judging tool, and a quarter of metrics exists. Gate out of it: one month of adopted changes with no regression on the scorecard.

## Acceptance
- [ ] the amendable set is named and versioned, and a proposal carries the eval reading that justifies it
- [ ] an adoption is recorded as a Decision with the two readings that produced it
- [ ] one month of adopted changes with no regression on the scorecard

## Non-goals
Anything a human still has to decide in 0.1.
