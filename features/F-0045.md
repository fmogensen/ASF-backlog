## Description
One ledger line per session end, written at the source rather than parsed out of a human-readable log: the Task, the account, the model, the kind, the item id, the result, the round, the pushes, the minutes and the cost. One stream per product, every line carrying its product. The ledger is what every cost and throughput number in the scorecard is summed from, and what a stalled or dead session is diagnosed against.

## Acceptance
- [ ] every session end writes exactly one ledger line, keyed so a re-run is idempotent
- [ ] every line carries its item id and its product
- [ ] the scorecard's cost and round counts are sums over the ledger, never over a parsed log
