## Description
The loop the factory runs on itself: measure a number, diagnose what moves it, act on one change, verify against the same measurement, revert on a worse reading, and reflect into the record. Its thresholds are per product, read from the product's configuration; its readings are that product's own metrics. In 0.1 the loop runs by hand and its adoptions are decisions a person makes; the eval-gated version is the 0.2 Epic, so this card stays undecided until the data exists to gate it.

## Acceptance
- [ ] every step of the loop names the measurement it reads and the stream it reads it from
- [ ] a change adopted by the loop is recorded as a Decision with the two readings behind it
- [ ] a worse reading reverts the change automatically

## Non-goals
Adopting a change without a reading; anything that edits the amendable set without a human merge.
