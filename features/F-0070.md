## Description
The CI health check detects a stalled job only once it passes its budget, so a job hanging on its second step burns the whole budget first. Add: for every in-progress job read its steps' start times through the provider's API; a job whose newest started step is more than ten minutes old with no completed step since is stalled on step silence — reported with the step name, and cancelled by the fix mode under the same rule as the budget class. The threshold is per product, per CI provider.

## Acceptance
- [ ] a job hung inside a step is cancelled within twelve minutes
- [ ] the product's CI stream line carries the stall class
