## Description
Two numbers per product, per CI provider (`product:ci.provider`), printed as value against target by the metrics rollup: cancelled runner minutes below 15 % and red rate below 15 %. The retro computes the seven-day mean and the bug filer files one Bug per number over target, at most once a week. The targets themselves live in the product's configuration (`product:ci.budgets.cancelled_minutes_pct` and `product:ci.budgets.red_rate_pct`, both new keys).

## Acceptance
- [ ] two target lines on the product's daily scorecard
- [ ] a Bug is filed when the seven-day mean misses a target
