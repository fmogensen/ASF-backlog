## Description
The counterpart of the factory loop, pointed at the product rather than at the line: one loop per product, its measurements, its probe identity and its guardrails read from that product's configuration, its proposals landing in that product's own record. The standing guardrails: the loop ships without per-change approval, but a new feature is a proposal for a human; the probe identity is development-only and touches no customer data; a worse measurement reverts; writer, reviewer and fixer are never the same session; and production, money, security, customer data and legal always need a human.

## Acceptance
- [ ] the loop reads only the product's own measurements and writes only to that product's record
- [ ] a proposed product change arrives as a card, never as a merge
- [ ] a worse measurement reverts the change

## Non-goals
Anything that changes the factory itself — that is the factory loop.
