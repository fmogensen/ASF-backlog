## Description
The prototype snapshotted the tree and reverted writes outside the declared footprint, keeping the reverted content — three runs died on an incomplete footprint. Here harvest **holds** instead: a branch whose diff touches files outside its Task's `writes:` is not landed, and the out-of-footprint diff is listed for the groom. Detect and hold, never silently revert.

The specification must also separate *footprint* (what sizes a wave) from *grant* (what a session may write), because the prototype's worst boundary defect was exactly the two meanings disagreeing.

## Acceptance
- [ ] a branch touching files outside its Task's footprint is held, with the extra diff listed
- [ ] no content is ever discarded by the boundary
- [ ] footprint and grant are separate fields with separate meanings
