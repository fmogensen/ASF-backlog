## Description
The wave runs dry: 73 of the 89 Features under E-0001 are cards with no spec, and nothing moves a card toward a spec. The CARD → SPEC row only fires for a *decided* card, and deciding a card waits for the operator's groom answers. Once F-0075, F-0086 and F-0073 finish building, the tick has nothing left to launch while the roadmap is still full.

Observed 2026-09-23: `asf backlog` showed card 73, landed 12, building 3, spec-approved 1.

Expected: a factory with a ranked roadmap keeps its session slots busy. For example, the groom's policy/adjudicate pass (F-0085) decides the top-ranked cards within the approval matrix, or the wave tells the operator how many decisions it is waiting on and which ones. It should never go quiet without saying why.
