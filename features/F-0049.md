## Description
Cleanup happens continuously, by one sweep in the tick, per product. (1) Worktrees: a registry row written at spawn; a finished job whose branch is on the remote is reaped by harvest; a job dead for more than a day with unpushed commits has them pushed to a hold branch and is then reaped; a hold branch older than a week becomes a groom line and is deleted on a yes. (2) Branches: a worker branch with no pull request and no push for a fortnight is tagged for the archive and deleted; heartbeat refs older than a day are deleted every tick; a merged branch is deleted at its merge. (3) Scratch: every tool writes under a per-job scratch directory; the sweep deletes anything older than three days there and anything without a job name at once; briefs archive with a week's TTL. (4) Logs: compressed after a week, deleted after a month — the metrics stream is the durable record, never the transcript. (5) A floor rule check: counts and TTL violations per class on the health table, the safe classes fixed by the sweep and the rest raised as groom lines.

Transcript retention and redaction: session logs hold every tool output, so a redaction pass removes token-shaped strings before the compression step.

## Acceptance
- [ ] the health table shows a floor row with zero TTL violations after one sweep
- [ ] a dead job's worktree is gone within a day, with its commits preserved on a hold branch
- [ ] no transcript older than the retention window survives, and none survives unredacted
