## Description
An Epic's typed budget is shown today and enforced by nothing. This enforces it: work under an Epic whose accumulated spend has passed its budget is held rather than dispatched, and the hold is one line for the operator. The budget is a per-record typed field, so enforcement is per product by construction.

## Acceptance
- [ ] an Epic over its budget holds its ready rows and raises one line
- [ ] the hold is visible in the roadmap, not only in a log
