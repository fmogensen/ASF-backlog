## Description
The "NEEDS OPERATOR" row tells the operator to answer an inbox question in groom/<today>.md and says "the next tick applies it". It doesn't. The tick's groom step applies only the previous day's file, so today's answers wait for tomorrow or a hand `asf groom --apply`. Answering the line also left an uncommitted edit in the record checkout. From then on the record step stopped syncing, yet logged `record ok`: the checkout fell 28 commits behind origin in about an hour. A hand `groom --apply` on that stale checkout then minted B-0268 to B-0270, which collided with different Bugs already pushed under those ids. Seen 2026-10-06 on ASF's own record.

## Second case (2026-10-06 21:19)
A spec-amend session's `asf new story` committed S-51600 to S-51602 locally. Its push was refused because the record was behind, and nothing retried. The checkout sat 3 ahead and 9 behind, with index.json in conflict on rebase. Added acceptance: a refused record push is retried on the next record step with fetch, rebase and a rebuilt index.json (`asf index`), and is never left diverged. Tested.

## Root cause (2026-10-06 23:15, read off the code)
- The tick commits from its own clone, state/<product>/record (asf/tick/tick.py:242-247, asf/tick/shadow.py:50-75). It never commits from backlog_dir.
- `asf new` and `asf inbox` commit in backlog_dir (asf/record/publish.py:33-44). When the push is refused after 3 fetch-rebase rounds (shadow.py:117-157), publish only prints "push the record by hand", and nothing retries.
- The tick's `sync_operator_checkout` (shadow.py:196-230) does `merge --ff-only` only, and skips a dirty tree. A checkout holding unpushed commits therefore stays diverged ("not fast-forwarded" logged 48 times on 10-06).
- Ids do not collide: ref claims under refs/asf/ids. F-0025's 8 Stories are distinct.

## Acceptance (from the root cause)
- [ ] sync_operator_checkout rebases local-only record commits onto origin (clean tree) and pushes them. It does not only fast-forward. A test with 3 local commits and a moved origin ends at 0 ahead and 0 behind.
- [ ] A rebase conflict on index.json is resolved by re-deriving the index, with a regression test across several commits.
- [ ] A push refused in publish is retried by the next tick's sync. `asf new` and `asf inbox` exit non-zero and name the unpushed commits when they leave the checkout ahead.
- [ ] A dirty backlog_dir is reported once as a named finding and does not block later syncs once clean.
- [ ] The record_behind watchdog stays quiet for 3 consecutive ticks while local `asf new` runs continue.
